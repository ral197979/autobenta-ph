# DAY 60 SUCCESS REVIEW
## AutoBentaPH — Month 2 Review Template & Guide

**Meeting Type:** Video call (preferred) or phone | **Duration:** 30 minutes
**Who attends:** Founder + Dealer #1

---

## WHAT'S DIFFERENT AT DAY 60

At Day 30, you were establishing baseline expectations and checking if the foundation was set. At Day 60, the answer is already visible in the data:

**The dealer is converted** — logging in regularly, responding to leads, using the CRM, tracking pipeline. They're getting value. Your job is to reinforce it, deepen it, and introduce expansion.

**Or the dealer is at-risk** — engagement has plateaued or dropped. They're logging in less. Leads are going unanswered. They haven't moved a deal through the CRM. Your job is to stop the drift before it becomes a churn decision.

There is no neutral at Day 60. Look at the data and decide which situation you're in before the call starts.

**Key benchmark at Day 60:**
- Health score should be ≥75 (Healthy)
- Cumulative leads received: ≥10
- Lead response rate (Month 2): ≥80%
- At least 1 lead has progressed to test drive or inquiry with visit confirmed

If the dealer is below these benchmarks, this is a recovery review, not a growth review. Adjust your agenda accordingly.

## MEETING AGENDA (30 Minutes)

### Minutes 0–3: Warm-Up
Same as Day 30. Start with the dealer's world, not the platform.
> "Kumusta na ang negosyo? Kamusta ang inventory? Maraming bago?"

Note: At Day 60, most dealers have a relationship rhythm with you. The warm-up is shorter because you already know each other.

### Minutes 3–10: Month 2 Metrics Review

> "Okay, tingnan natin kung ano ang nangyari sa ikalawang buwan."

Walk through:
1. **Month 2 leads:** "[X] bagong leads ang natanggap mo ngayong buwan."
2. **Cumulative leads:** "Total, [X] leads na ang nanggaling sa AutoBentaPH sa 60 araw."
3. **Lead response rate:** "[X]% — [above/below] iyong target nating 80%."
4. **Health score trend:** "Ang health score mo ay [X] ngayon, [up/down/flat] mula sa [X] noong Month 1."
5. **Pipeline:** "Nakita ko sa CRM na [X leads] ang may stage na updated. [X] na ang nasa [deepest stage]."

Invite reaction: "Ano ang naiisip mo dito? Tama ba ang nararamdaman mo sa platform?"

### Minutes 10–17: Advanced Feature Walkthrough (if Healthy)

If health score ≥75, introduce one or two advanced features the dealer hasn't used yet.

**Pick from this shortlist:**
- **CRM automation rules:** "Pag may bagong lead, pwede kang mag-set ng auto-reply para hindi mo palagi kailangan na manual na sumagot agad. Gusto mo bang ituro ko sa iyo ngayon?"
- **Analytics interpretation:** "Dito sa analytics page, makikita mo kung aling listings ang pinaka-maraming views at clicks. Makakatulong ito para malaman mo kung aling sasakyan ang pinakainteresting sa buyers ngayon."
- **Bulk inventory updates:** "Kung maraming listings kailangan i-update nang sabay-sabay — halimbawa, baguhin ang presyo ng 10 cars — may feature tayo para doon. Makatipid ito ng malaking oras."

Choose ONE. Walk through it on screen. Don't dump three features at once.

### Minutes 17–22: The Expansion Conversation (if Healthy)

Introduce the featured listing add-on only if health score ≥75 and dealer is clearly engaged.

> "[Name], ngayon na alam na nating gumagana ang AutoBentaPH para sa iyo, gusto kong ipakita sa iyo ang isang option para mapalakas pa ang visibility ng pinakamagandang listings mo.
>
> Ang Featured Listings ay naglalagay ng iyong piniling sasakyan sa itaas ng search results — mas maraming buyers ang makakakita. Add-on ito ng ₱999 bawat buwan para sa hanggang [X] featured listings.
>
> Hindi ito requirement — ang base plan mo ay gumagana na. Pero kung may unit kang gusto mong mapabilis ang benta, worth exploring ito.
>
> Gusto mo bang tingnan natin kung aling listings ang pinaka-angkop?"

**If they say yes:** Set it up together on the call.
**If they say not yet:** Note their response. Bring it up again at Day 90 if health is maintained.
**If health <75:** Do not introduce this. Focus on recovery first.

### Minutes 22–26: Renewal Early Signal

At Day 60, it is appropriate — and important — to plant the renewal seed.

> "[Name], ang renewal mo ay around [DATE] na. Dalawang buwan nang gumagamit ka ng AutoBentaPH. Bago pa natin dumating doon, gusto ko lang malaman: sa ngayon, kumusta ang nararamdaman mo? Parang okay ba ang direction na ito?"

Listen carefully. The dealer will tell you everything you need to know with their answer.

**"Oo, okay naman"** → Renewal is on track. Schedule the Day 90 review.
**"Hmm, hindi ko pa sure"** → There's a concern they haven't raised yet. Ask: "Ano ang nagpapag-alinlangan sa iyo?" Address it now.
**"May tanong ako sa presyo"** → See the "Complaint #3" section in `DEALER_1_PLAYBOOK.md`.

### Minutes 26–30: Action Items & Next Steps

> "Okay [Name], ito ang gagawin natin sa susunod na 30 araw para sa Month 3. [Summarize 2–3 actions.] At mag-schedule tayo ng final review — iyong 90-day review — para sa [DATE]. Mas matagal iyon kasi gusto kong ipakita sa iyo ang buong 3-buwan na resulta at pag-usapan natin ang susunod na hakbang."

## HEALTH SCORE BENCHMARK AT DAY 60

| Score | Status | Action |
|-------|--------|--------|
| ≥75 | On track for renewal | Reinforce value, introduce expansion, plant renewal seed |
| 60–74 | Needs attention | Identify the one metric dragging the score; fix it before Day 75 |
| 50–59 | Recovery required | Skip expansion; run a focused recovery plan this month |
| <50 | Intervention | Do not wait for Day 90. Escalate to At Risk protocol now. |

If health score is below 60 at Day 60, the renewal is not automatically at risk — but it requires active intervention. Document the issue, create a recovery plan with specific milestones, and check in every week until Day 90.
