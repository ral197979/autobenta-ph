# CASE STUDY TEMPLATE
## AutoBentaPH — Dealer #1 Success Story Framework

**Version:** 1.0 | **Status:** Collect data from Day 0; finalize at Day 90

---

## WHY THIS MATTERS

Dealer #1's story is not just a nice-to-have. It is your primary sales asset for every dealer conversation from #2 to #50.

When a prospective dealer asks "Does this actually work?", you don't answer with features — you answer with a real dealer's real numbers. A case study with a name, a face, and a specific result is worth 10 feature demo calls.

Every data point you collect from Dealer #1, from Day 0 onward, is raw material for this story. Capture it as you go. Don't try to reconstruct it at Month 3 from memory.

**Target uses for this case study:**
- AutoBentaPH for-dealers landing page (headline testimonial)
- WhatsApp outreach to prospective Dealers #2–5
- Demo call intro slide ("Here's what a dealer achieved in 90 days")
- Proposal deck (proof section)
- Dealer #2–10 pitch ("Your neighbor is already using this")

---

## WHEN TO START COLLECTING DATA

**Day 0 — Baseline capture (before the dealer logs in for the first time)**
This is the most important and most often missed step. You need "before" data to tell a before/after story.

**Day 30 — Month 1 metrics**
First full month of lead data, response rates, and CRM activity.

**Day 60 — Month 2 metrics**
Pipeline depth, first sales or near-sales, advanced feature usage.

**Day 90 — The case study interview**
Formal 20-minute interview. Verbatim quotes. Permission to publish.

   *Record answer: ______*

   *Record answer: ______*

## MONTH 3 CASE STUDY INTERVIEW

**When:** At or immediately after the Day 90 renewal review.
**Duration:** 20 minutes.
**Format:** Video call (record with permission) or phone.

**Ask permission before starting:**
> "[Name], okay lang ba na i-record ang conversation natin ngayon para sa case study? Para ma-capture namin ang exact na sinabi mo — hindi ko gustong baguhin ang salita mo."

**The 10 Case Study Questions:**

**On the "before":**
1. "Bago ka sumali sa AutoBentaPH, ano ang pinaka-malaking challenge mo sa pag-attract ng buyers para sa iyong inventory?"
2. "Ano ang iyong nararamdaman tungkol sa digital marketing para sa used cars bago mo subukan ang AutoBentaPH?"
3. "Bakit ka nag-decide na subukan ang AutoBentaPH?"

**On the experience:**
4. "Ano ang unang nangyari na nagsabi sa iyo na gumagana ito?"
5. "Anong specific na feature ang pinakamahalaga sa iyong negosyo?"
6. "Kung i-compare mo ang AutoBentaPH sa kung paano ka nagma-market dati, ano ang pinakamalaking pagbabago?"

**On the results:**
7. "Sa 90 araw, ilang leads ang natanggap mo mula sa AutoBentaPH?"
8. "Nakakuha ka ba ng sale na directly mula sa AutoBentaPH lead? Magkano ang halaga?"
9. "Kung susukatin mo ang ROI — return on investment — ng ₱3,599 bawat buwan, ano ang masasabi mo?"

**On recommendation:**
10. "Kung may ibang dealer na nagtatanong sa iyo kung sulit ba ang AutoBentaPH, ano ang sasabihin mo sa kanila?"

**Bonus (for the testimonial):**
> "Kung mayroon kang isang pangungusap para sa ibang dealers na nag-aatubiling sumali, ano iyon?"

## THE CASE STUDY FORMAT

Use this structure when writing the final case study for publication.

---

### HEADLINE
> "[DEALER NAME]: [X] Leads in [X] Days — How [Dealer Name] Grew Their Pipeline with AutoBentaPH"

**Formula:** [Dealer Name] + [Specific Result] + [Timeframe]
**Example:** "ABC Motors Generated 23 Leads in 30 Days with AutoBentaPH"

---

### THE CHALLENGE (100–150 words)

*Before AutoBentaPH...*

Write 2–3 sentences describing the dealer's situation before joining. Use their own words from Question 1 and 2 of the interview. Focus on the pain, not the solution.

> "[DEALER NAME] had been running their used car dealership in [LOCATION] for [X] years. Like most dealers, they relied on [CHANNELS] to attract buyers — and while it worked, [DESCRIBE THE PROBLEM: slow response time, low lead volume, too manual, etc.].
>
> '[VERBATIM QUOTE from Q1 or Q2],' [Dealer Name] recalls."

---

### THE SOLUTION (100–150 words)

*Then they found AutoBentaPH...*

Describe what the dealer now uses and how. Be specific about features. Write in plain language — no jargon.

> "[DEALER NAME] joined AutoBentaPH as a Founding Dealer in [MONTH YEAR]. Within [X] days, they had [X] listings live and were receiving leads directly in their CRM.
>
> '[VERBATIM QUOTE from Q4 or Q5],' they said.
>
> The features they use most: [LIST 2–3 features]. '[VERBATIM QUOTE about the experience].'"

---

### THE RESULTS (100 words + metrics block)

**Metrics block (always present as a visual highlight):**
```
📊 90-Day Results
• [X] leads received
• [X]% lead response rate
• [X] active listings at peak
• [X] documented sales from AutoBentaPH leads
• ₱[X] estimated pipeline value
• [X]× ROI on platform cost
```

Then 1–2 sentences of narrative:
> "In 90 days, [Dealer Name] received [X] leads from AutoBentaPH — [compare to baseline if available: 'compared to [X] per month before']. '[VERBATIM QUOTE from Q9 — the ROI question].'"

---

### THE QUOTE (Callout)

Pick the single best quote from Q10 (the recommendation question). Use it as a large pull quote.

> *"[VERBATIM QUOTE]"*
> — [Dealer Name], [Dealership Name], [City]

---

### NEXT STEPS (2–3 sentences)

> "[DEALER NAME] plans to [what they said about growing on the platform]. They are exploring [featured listings / expanding inventory / etc.] with AutoBentaPH to [specific goal].
>
> 'Kung may ibang dealer na nag-aatubili, ito ang sinasabi ko sa kanila: [VERBATIM QUOTE from bonus question],' [Dealer Name] says."

## PERMISSION FORM

Before publishing anything — quote, metrics, name, photo — get explicit written permission. Send this via WhatsApp and ask for a confirmation reply.

> "Hi [Name]! Para sa case study na gagawin namin tungkol sa iyong experience sa AutoBentaPH, gusto naming gamitin ang:
>
> ✅ Iyong pangalan at dealership name
> ✅ Iyong lokasyon (lungsod lang)
> ✅ Iyong metrics (leads, response rate, etc.)
> ✅ Iyong mga quote na nasabi mo sa interview
>
> Hindi namin gagamitin ang iyong contact number, eksaktong address, o anumang impormasyon na hindi mo gustong public.
>
> Ipapakita namin sa iyo ang final version bago kami mag-publish ng kahit ano.
>
> Pwede mo bang i-reply ng 'Approved' dito para ma-confirm? Salamat!"

**File the confirmation reply.** Screenshot it. Do not publish without it.

## VIDEO TESTIMONIAL GUIDE

A 60-second video testimonial from Dealer #1 is worth more than any written case study.

**When to ask:** At Day 90, after the case study interview, if the dealer is in a positive state.

**How to ask:**
> "[Name], isa pang request — pwede ka bang mag-record ng isang minutong video testimonial? Hindi kailangan na professional — video lang sa phone. Sabihin mo lang sa iyong mga sariling salita: who you are, what challenge you had, what AutoBentaPH did for you, at kung irerekomenda mo ito sa ibang dealers.
>
> Pwede mo itong i-record ngayon lang, o sa susunod na may oras ka. I-send mo sa WhatsApp."

**What to tell them to cover (in order):**
1. Name, dealership name, and location (5 seconds)
2. What their challenge was before AutoBentaPH (15 seconds)
3. One specific result they achieved (20 seconds)
4. Recommendation to other dealers (15 seconds)
5. Optional: show a listing or the dashboard briefly (5 seconds)

**Editing:** None required. Authentic > polished. A shaky phone video from a real dealer converts better than a studio-quality generic ad.
