# DEALER #1 OPERATING PLAYBOOK
## AutoBentaPH — Complete Guide from Signature to Renewal

**Version:** 1.0 | **For:** Founder / Account Owner | **Updated:** 2026-05-31

---

## 1. THE GOAL

This is not a retention exercise. Dealer #1 is your proof of concept, your case study, and the unlock for Dealers #2–50.

The goal has three parts:

**Part 1 — Retention.** Keep the dealer active and paying through the 90-day renewal. Straightforward.

**Part 2 — Value delivery.** The dealer must receive a tangible result — a lead they responded to, a car they can trace back to AutoBentaPH, a workflow that saves them time. "They're logging in" is not value. A qualified lead that progressed to a test drive is.

**Part 3 — The case study.** If the dealer is happy at Day 90 and you haven't captured their story, you've left your most powerful sales asset on the table. Every future dealer conversation starts with: "Here's what [Dealer #1] achieved in 90 days." Build this from Day 1.

**What success looks like:**
- Day 90: renewal confirmed, invoice paid
- Day 90: at least one documented result (lead → sale, or clear pipeline value)
- Day 90: dealer willing to give a quote or video testimonial
- Day 90: dealer referred one contact, or agreed to a referral introduction

If you hit all four, Dealer #1 is not a customer — they're a business partner.

## 2. THE 90-DAY TIMELINE

### Week 1 (Days 1–7): Foundation
**Goal:** Dealer is set up, has published listings, and has received or is about to receive their first lead.

| Milestone | Target Day | What "Done" Looks Like |
|-----------|-----------|------------------------|
| Agreement signed | Day 0 | Signed copy on file |
| Invoice sent | Day 1 | GCash/bank details sent via WhatsApp |
| Invoice paid | Day 3 | Payment confirmed, receipt sent |
| First login | Day 1 | Dealer accessed the platform |
| First listing published | Day 3 | At least 1 active listing visible |
| Inventory setup complete | Day 7 | ≥5 listings published |

**Red flag:** If there is no login by Day 3, stop everything. Message the dealer directly. The first week is when onboarding failure happens.

### Week 2 (Days 8–14): First Value
**Goal:** Dealer has seen their first lead. They've used the CRM to track it.

| Milestone | Target Day | What "Done" Looks Like |
|-----------|-----------|------------------------|
| First lead received | Day 7–10 | Lead appears in their CRM inbox |
| First lead response | Day 7–11 | Dealer responded within 4 hours |
| CRM being used | Day 14 | At least 2 leads tracked with a stage set |

**Red flag:** Lead received but not responded to. This is a habits problem — address it at Day 14 check-in.

### Weeks 3–4 (Days 15–30): Habit Formation
**Goal:** Dealer logs in daily (or near-daily). Lead response rate ≥70%. Health score ≥75.

**What to watch:** Login frequency, lead response rate, CRM stage updates. If any metric is declining, act before Day 30 review.

### Month 2 (Days 31–60): ROI Visibility
**Goal:** Dealer can point to at least one lead that moved to a test drive or sale. Health score maintained ≥75.

**What to introduce:** Featured listings add-on (₱999/mo) — only pitch if health score is ≥75 and they've asked about visibility.

### Month 3 (Days 61–90): Renewal Runway
**Goal:** Renewal conversation starts no later than Day 75. Invoice sent Day 88. Confirmed by Day 90.

**What to build:** The case study. Start collecting quotes and screenshots at Day 60.

## 3. YOUR WEEKLY RHYTHM

Every week, this takes 30 minutes. Do it every Monday morning before 9am PHT.

**Minutes 1–10: Pull the dashboard**
- Open the Dealer #1 health score. What tier are they in?
- Check login activity for the past 7 days. How many days did they log in?
- Check lead count and lead response rate for the week.
- Check CRM: any new stage updates? Any leads stalled?
- Check listing count: any new listings added or removed?

**Minutes 11–20: Interpret and decide**
- Healthy (≥75): No action required unless you want to reinforce a win. Send one encouraging WhatsApp message this week.
- Watch (50–74): Identify the one metric dragging the score. Plan a targeted check-in. Don't wait for the scheduled CS task.
- At Risk (25–49): Send a WhatsApp message today. Don't wait for Monday. Find out what's blocking them.
- Critical (<25): Call them. Today. Not tomorrow.

**Minutes 21–30: Log and prep**
- Log last week's actions in the dealer CRM (what you sent, what they replied).
- Note the next scheduled CS task and what you need to prepare.
- Flag any pattern: 2+ weeks in Watch = escalate to At Risk protocol even if score says otherwise.

## 4. THE 7 CS TASKS — EXPANDED GUIDE

### CS Task 1: Day 1 — Welcome & Setup
**Timing:** Same day as agreement signing, within 2 hours.
**Channel:** WhatsApp

**What to send:**
> "Hi [Name]! Welcome to AutoBentaPH! I'm [YOUR NAME], your dedicated account manager. Sa susunod na ilang araw, tutulungan kita ma-set up ng account mo para mabilis ka makatanggap ng leads. Magpadala na rin kami ng invoice ngayon. May tanong ka man, nandito lang kami. 🚗"

**What to do:**
- Send the invoice (GCash or bank transfer, due within 3 business days)
- Share login credentials or activation link
- Send the quick-start guide (top 3 things to do in the first 24 hours)

**What to look for:**
- Did they read the message? (WhatsApp blue ticks)
- Did they click the login link?

**If off track:** No reply by end of Day 1 → call them. Don't assume the message landed.

---

### CS Task 2: Day 3 — First Login Check
**Timing:** Day 3, morning
**Channel:** WhatsApp

**What to check first:**
- Has the invoice been paid?
- Has there been at least one login?
- Is there at least one listing published?

**If all three are done:**
> "Hi [Name]! Nakita ko na-publish na ang listing mo — maganda! May tanong ka ba sa dashboard or sa pag-upload ng listings? Nandito lang ako."

**If invoice unpaid:**
> "Hi [Name]! Just checking in — kumusta na ang payment? Pwede sa GCash: [YOUR GCASH] o bank transfer: [BANK DETAILS]. Pag-confirm mo lang, i-activate na namin agad ang full account mo."

**If no login:**
> "Hi [Name]! Gusto ko lang siguruhing ok ang login link na natanggap mo. Napadala ko sa email at WhatsApp. Subukan mo na lang mag-click, tapos sabihin mo kung may issue. Tutulong ako."

**What to look for:** Any friction with the login or setup process. Surface it now — not at Day 7.

---

### CS Task 3: Day 7 — First Week Recap
**Timing:** Day 7, mid-morning
**Channel:** WhatsApp, offer a 15-min call if anything is off

**What to check:**
- Health score: what tier?
- Login days in past 7: ideally 4+
- Listings published: ideally ≥3
- First lead: received or not yet?

**If healthy (first lead received, listing published):**
> "Hi [Name]! Isang linggo na — great start! [X] listings na ang naka-publish mo, at [X] leads na ang natanggap mo. Sagutin mo lang ang mga leads nang mabilis — mas mataas ang chance na mag-convert. Kwentuhan mo naman ako kung may naging interested na buyer. 🔥"

**If no lead yet (listing published but no lead):**
> "Hi [Name]! [X] listings na — OK na ang setup! Usually nag-aappear ang unang leads sa Day 5–10. Habang hinihintay, pwede mo pang dagdagan ang detalye ng listings (mas maraming larawan, complete specs) para mas maraming maka-click. Gusto mo bang tumulong akong i-review ang mga listings mo?"

**If still not logged in:**
Stop scheduled outreach and call them immediately. Something is wrong — personal issue, technical issue, or cold feet. Find out which.

---

### CS Task 4: Day 14 — Two-Week Check-in
**Timing:** Day 14
**Channel:** WhatsApp + offer a 20-min call

**What to check:**
- Health score trend: improving, flat, or declining?
- Lead response rate: ≥70%? If not, why not?
- CRM usage: are they updating stages?
- Any support tickets or complaints?

**Script (healthy):**
> "Hi [Name]! Dalawang linggo na — checking in. Kumusta ang mga leads? May naging test drive or sale na ba? Gusto ko ring malaman kung may features na gusto mong pag-aralan — mayroon kaming CRM automation na makakatipid sa iyo ng oras."

**Script (Watch tier):**
> "Hi [Name], gusto ko lang mag-check in kasi nakita ko hindi masyadong nag-re-respond sa leads ngayong nakaraang linggo. Normal naman 'yan sa simula — baka busy ka. Pwede tayo mag-usap ng 15 minuto para makita kung paano ko mapapadali ang proseso para sa iyo?"

**What to do if lead response rate is <50%:**
- Ask directly: "Natutumanggap ka ba ng notification pag may bagong lead?"
- Check notification settings together
- Offer to walk through how to respond from WhatsApp

## 5. READING THE HEALTH SCORE

### Healthy (≥75) — Green
**What it means:** Dealer is active, responding to leads, using the CRM.
**Your job:** Reinforce the behavior. Send a positive message once a week. Build toward the case study.
**Do not:** Let a healthy dealer go silent. "No news is good news" is how you miss the early signs of drift.

### Watch (50–74) — Yellow
**What it means:** One or two key metrics are soft. Login frequency dropped, or lead response rate slipped.
**Your job:** Identify the single biggest drag metric. Address it directly. Don't send a generic check-in — name the specific issue.
**Time to act:** Within 48 hours of score dropping to Watch.

### At Risk (25–49) — Orange
**What it means:** Multiple metrics are declining. The dealer is disengaging.
**Your job:** Get on a call this week. Find the root cause. Is it product friction? Personal/business issue? Expectation mismatch?
**Time to act:** Same day. WhatsApp message immediately. Call within 24 hours if no response.

### Critical (<25) — Red
**What it means:** Near-zero engagement. If nothing changes, this dealer churns.
**Your job:** Call today. If no answer, call again tomorrow. Show up with a plan — not just a check-in. Bring a specific, concrete offer (e.g., 1-on-1 setup session, refreshed listings, free featured listing for 2 weeks).
**Time to act:** Today. No exceptions.

## 6. THE "NO ACTIVITY" PROTOCOL

When a dealer stops logging in, stops responding to WhatsApp messages, or both.

### Day 1 of Silence
**Action:** Send a low-pressure WhatsApp check-in.
> "Hi [Name]! Kumusta na? Nakita ko hindi pa naka-log in ngayong linggo. Busy ka siguro — ok lang. Nandito lang kami kapag kailangan mo. May bago kaming tips para sa mas maraming leads kapag may time ka."

**Do not:** Send a follow-up the same day. Let it breathe.

### Day 3 of Silence (No reply to Day 1 message)
**Action:** Call them. If voicemail, leave a message and follow up with:
> "Hi [Name]! Tinawagan kita kanina. Gusto ko lang tiyaking ayos ka at walang issue sa account. Mag-reply ka lang dito o tumawag — [YOUR PHONE]. Nandito kami."

**Also do:** Check if there's a billing issue (payment failed?), a technical issue (reported bug?), or a life event. Sometimes silence = crisis unrelated to the product.

### Day 7 of Silence (Still no reply)
**Action:** This is now a retention risk. Escalate.
- Try an alternate contact method (call on a different number if you have one, or ask a mutual contact)
- Send a final "we're here when you're ready" message — not a sales message, not a feature pitch
- Log this as At Risk in the CRM
- Prepare a re-engagement plan for when they surface

> "Hi [Name]. Nag-try akong makipag-ugnayan sa iyo ngayong nakaraang linggo. Account mo ay active pa rin. Kapag ready ka nang mag-continue, nandito kami — libre ang nag-set up session para matulungan kang magsimuli. Walang pressure."

**Do not:** Send 5 messages in a row. Filipino business culture values respect and not being puwersahin (forced). One check-in per 2-3 days maximum.

## 7. THE VALUE CONVERSATION

### The Month 1 ROI Script
Use this at the Day 30 review to frame what happened in terms of money, not metrics.

**Setup:** Before the call, calculate:
- Leads received in Month 1: [X]
- Assumed close rate: 15% (conservative)
- Assumed margin per car: ₱50,000
- Estimated revenue impact: X × 0.15 × ₱50,000

**Script (Taglish, conversational):**
> "Okay [Name], tingnan natin ang resulta. Sa loob ng 30 araw, [X] leads ang natanggap mo mula sa AutoBentaPH. Pag sinabi nating kahit 15% lang ang mag-close — which is conservative — that's roughly [X × 0.15] cars. Sa ₱50,000 margin per unit, that's ₱[amount] in potential gross profit.
>
> Iyong ₱3,599 na binayad mo? [Amount] times ROI sa unang buwan pa lang.
>
> Hindi pa namin kasama dito iyong mga leads na nasa pipeline pa at baka mag-close next month."

**Then ask:** "Alin sa mga leads na iyon ang pinaka-promising sa palagay mo?"

This shifts the conversation from "did it work?" to "how do we make it work even better?"

## 8. HANDLING COMPLAINTS

### Complaint #1: "Hindi ako nakakatanggap ng leads"
**Root causes (check in order):**
1. Listings not published or marked inactive
2. Listing quality poor (no photos, incomplete specs)
3. Notification settings not configured
4. Account not fully activated

**Script:**
> "Okay, tingnan natin ito ngayon. Pwede mo bang i-share ang screen mo? O pwede akong mag-log in at tingnan sa aking end. Usually ito ay matter of [most common root cause]. Aayusin natin ngayon mismo."

**Resolution SLA:** 4 hours for Founding Dealers (Mon–Sat 8am–6pm PHT).

---

### Complaint #2: "Ang hirap gamitin ng platform"
**Root causes:**
1. Not familiar with web-based CRM (older dealer)
2. Too many features shown at once during onboarding
3. Mobile experience issues

**Script:**
> "[Name], pasensya na. Gusto kong matulungan kang ma-simplify ang ginagamit mo. Ituro mo lang sa akin ang isang bagay na nakakalito — sisimulan natin doon. Hindi mo kailangang gamitin lahat ng features agad."

**Action:** Schedule a 30-minute 1-on-1 walkthrough. Focus on ONE workflow: log in → check leads → respond → update CRM stage.

---

### Complaint #3: "Mahal ito para sa dami ng leads"
**Do not:** Panic. Do not offer a discount on the Founding rate.

**Script:**
> "[Name], naiintindihan ko. Tingnan natin ang math. Kung [X] leads at kahit 1 lang ang mag-close sa halagang [average margin], [amount] na ang na-earn mo. Iyong ₱3,599 — [X] times mo na siyang na-recover.
>
> Pero kung pakiramdam mo hindi pa sapat ang value, sabihin mo lang — gusto kong malaman kung paano namin mapabilis ang results para sa iyo."

**Action:** Improve listing quality together. A dealer getting few leads almost always has a listing problem, not a platform problem.

**See:** `DAY_90_REVIEW.md` for the full script.

**The core ask:**

## 10. IF DEALER #1 CANCELS

### First: Don't react emotionally. This is data.

**Immediate steps:**
1. Thank them sincerely. No guilt-tripping, no last-minute discounting.
2. Send an offboarding survey (3 questions, 2 minutes, WhatsApp link).
3. Ask one open question directly: "Kung isa lang ang pagbabago para sana hindi ka umalis, ano iyun?"

### What to Learn
Ask yourself honestly:
- At what point did engagement drop? (Dashboard will show this)
- Was there a CS task that didn't happen on schedule?
- Did the dealer ever receive a qualified lead?
- Did the Time-to-Value sequence stall at a specific milestone?
- Was the complaint something you could have caught earlier?

### What to Fix (by root cause)
| Root Cause | Fix |
|------------|-----|
| Never received a lead | Improve listing SEO and quality at Day 3, not Day 30 |
| Too complex | Simplify onboarding to 3 steps maximum for first week |
| Not enough time | Offer a "done-for-you" listing service at onboarding |
| Expected instant results | Set expectations clearly in the welcome message |
| Competitor offer | Document the offer — price, features, terms |

### What NOT to Do
- Do not re-sell them the same day they cancel
- Do not offer a discount to win them back (you'll train all future dealers to cancel for a discount)
- Do not delete their data — keep it for 90 days in case they return
- Do not assume their feedback applies to all dealers — validate with Dealer #2

### The Upside
If Dealer #1 cancels, you have something most SaaS founders never get: early churn data with a real customer. Every detail they give you is a product insight. Document it, fix it, and use it to close Dealer #2 faster.
