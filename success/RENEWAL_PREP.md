## RENEWAL READINESS SCORE — EXPLAINED

The Renewal Readiness Score is a 0–100 composite of 6 factors. It tells you, at any point in the 60-day runway, how likely this dealer is to renew.

### The 6 Components

| Factor | What It Measures | Max Points | How to Increase |
|--------|-----------------|-----------|-----------------|
| **Health Review** | Is there a documented health review within the last 30 days? | 20 | Complete the Day 30 or Day 60 review on schedule |
| **Usage Review** | Is the dealer's current usage trend positive (login frequency, CRM updates)? | 15 | Address any usage drop within 48 hours of detection |
| **ROI Review** | Has the dealer seen a documented ROI calculation? | 20 | Present the ROI calculation at Day 30 and Day 90 |
| **Expansion Opportunity** | Has an expansion feature been introduced and discussed? | 15 | Introduce Featured Listings at Day 60 (only if Healthy) |
| **Proposal Sent** | Has a renewal proposal been sent? | 15 | Send proposal at Day 75 (15 days before renewal) |
| **Confirmed** | Has the dealer verbally confirmed intent to renew? | 15 | Close the renewal at Day 90 review |

**Score Thresholds:**
- **≥80:** On track. Continue the timeline. Renewal is highly probable.
- **60–79:** Needs attention. At least one factor is missing or weak. Address the gap within 1 week.
- **<60:** Intervention required. Stop and assess. Identify what is blocking each incomplete factor and create a recovery plan.

## DAY 60 BEFORE RENEWAL — Health Review Checklist

*This coincides with the Day 30 subscription milestone for a 90-day renewal cycle.*

Run this checklist after the Day 30 review:

- [ ] Day 30 review completed and logged
- [ ] Health score documented: ______
- [ ] Lead response rate documented: ______%
- [ ] Login frequency (30 days): ______ days
- [ ] Active listings count: ______
- [ ] At least 1 lead has progressed past "New" stage in CRM
- [ ] ROI calculation has been presented and discussed

**What "healthy enough to renew" looks like at this stage:**
- Health score ≥65 (allows time to improve before renewal)
- Lead response rate ≥60%
- Dealer is logging in at least 3× per week
- At least 1 lead has been actively followed up (any stage beyond "New")

**If not meeting the threshold:**
You have 60 days to close the gap. Start the Recovery Protocol now — don't wait. Document what's wrong, fix it, and re-check at Day 45 before renewal.

## DAY 15 BEFORE RENEWAL — Confirmation Call

**Purpose:** Confirm the renewal intent, address any remaining concerns, set expectations for the invoice.

**Duration:** 15 minutes maximum. This is not a review meeting.

**Script:**
> "Hi [Name]! 15 days na lang bago mag-renew ang account mo. Gusto ko lang mag-check in — nakita mo na ang renewal proposal na pinadala ko? Mayroon ka bang tanong o concerns bago mo gawin ang desisyon?
>
> Kung okay ka na, ipapadalhan kita ng invoice sa [DATE, 7 days before renewal]. GCash or bank transfer — whichever is easier para sa iyo."

**What to listen for:**
- "Oo, okay" → Confirmed. Note it. Send a reminder 7 days before.
- "Hmm, hindi ko pa nababasa" → Resend the proposal. Schedule a follow-up in 3 days.
- "May concern ako..." → Address it now. Do not let it sit.

**Log the outcome.** Update the renewal readiness score.

## IF RENEWAL IS AT RISK

Signs that renewal is at risk (any one of these is a warning):
- Health score <50 at Day 45 before renewal
- No response to the Day 15 confirmation call
- Dealer explicitly said "I'll think about it" at Day 15 and hasn't replied since
- Renewal readiness score <60 at Day 15

**Intervention checklist:**
- [ ] Schedule an emergency call — this week, not "when available"
- [ ] Pull the full CS task completion log: what was missed?
- [ ] Identify the single biggest unsolved problem the dealer has
- [ ] Prepare a concrete solution to bring to the call (not a generic offer — a specific fix)

**What to offer:**
- Free 1-on-1 setup session to rebuild their listings
- A 30-day performance review showing any results they may have missed
- A commitment to resolve their top complaint within 48 hours

**What NOT to offer:**
- A discount on the Founding Dealer rate. This rate is already ₱3,599/month — the lowest it will ever be. Discounting it trains future dealers to cancel for a discount and devalues your product.
- Free months. This sets a precedent you cannot sustain.

**The honest intervention script:**
> "[Name], gusto kong maging diretso sa iyo. Nakita ko na hindi masyadong aktibo ang account mo ngayong nakaraang buwan. Gusto kong malaman kung may concern ka na hindi ko pa natutugunan — at kung mayroon, ayusin nating ngayon bago mag-renew.
>
> Naniniwala pa rin akong may malaking halaga ang AutoBentaPH para sa negosyo mo. Pero kung may ginagawa akong mali, sabihin mo sa akin."

## THE RENEWAL PROPOSAL FORMAT

One page. Three sections. Send as PDF or clean WhatsApp message.

**Section 1 — Results (what happened in 90 days)**
```
Ang iyong 90-day results sa AutoBentaPH:
• [X] total leads received
• [X]% average lead response rate
• [X] active listings at peak
• Estimated value: ₱[amount] in potential pipeline
```

**Section 2 — The Plan (what we'll do in the next 90 days)**
```
Para sa susunod na tatlong buwan:
• [Specific improvement goal based on their situation]
• [Feature or workflow to introduce]
• [Lead volume or response rate target]
Monthly check-in reviews + 4-hour SLA on all support requests.
```

**Section 3 — Terms**
```
Founding Dealer Plan — Renewal
Monthly rate: ₱3,599 (locked for life — this rate will never increase)
Payment: GCash or bank transfer within 3 business days
Renewal date: [DATE]
```
