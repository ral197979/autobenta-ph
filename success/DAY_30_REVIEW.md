# DAY 30 SUCCESS REVIEW
## AutoBentaPH — Month 1 Review Template & Guide

**Meeting Type:** Video call (preferred) or phone | **Duration:** 30 minutes
**Who attends:** Founder + Dealer #1

---

## MEETING AGENDA (30 Minutes)

### Minutes 0–3: Warm-Up
Open with something personal. Ask about their business, not the platform.
> "Kumusta ang negosyo ngayon? Busy season ba ito para sa inyo?"

Let them talk for 1–2 minutes. This is relationship-building, not small talk.

### Minutes 3–10: Month 1 Metrics Presentation
Present the data. Be specific. Don't narrate — show.

> "Okay, tingnan natin ang naging resulta sa loob ng 30 araw."

Walk through:
1. **Leads received:** "[X] leads ang natanggap mo mula sa AutoBentaPH sa loob ng isang buwan."
2. **Lead response rate:** "[X]% ng mga leads ay na-respond mo — [great / pwede pa nating palakasin]."
3. **Listings:** "[X] listings ang naka-publish. [More/fewer] listings = [more/fewer] visibility."
4. **Login activity:** "Nag-log in ka ng [X] beses — [malaki ang tulong nito / gusto nating dagdagan ito]."
5. **Health score:** "[X] — [Healthy / Watch / At Risk]. Ibig sabihin [explain briefly]."

Pause after each. Ask: "Ok ba ito para sa iyo? May tanong ka ba?"

### Minutes 10–17: The Value Moment
This is the most important 7 minutes of the meeting.

> "Ngayon, pag-usapan natin ang value. [X] leads ang natanggap mo. Kung kahit 15% lang sa kanila ang mag-close — which is conservative sa used car sales — that's [X × 0.15] potential deals.
>
> Sa ₱50,000 na margin per car, that's ₱[amount] in potential gross profit mula sa leads na galing sa AutoBentaPH sa isang buwan pa lang.
>
> Iyong ₱3,599 na binayad mo? Roughly [ROI multiple]× ang return sa potential revenue.
>
> Hindi pa namin kasama dito iyong mga leads na nasa pipeline pa."

Then: "Aling leads ang pinakamaganda ang naging resulta para sa iyo?"

Let them talk. Listen for:
- A specific car they sold or almost sold from an AutoBentaPH lead
- A buyer interaction that felt promising
- Any frustration they haven't raised yet

### Minutes 17–25: The 5 Key Questions
Ask these verbatim. Take notes.

**Q1:** "Sa lahat ng features na nagamit mo, alin ang pinakamahalaga sa iyo?"
*(Identifies what's working — double down on it.)*

**Q2:** "May naguluhan ka ba o nahirapan ka na gamitin sa platform?"
*(Surfaces friction before it becomes churn.)*

**Q3:** "Kumusta ang mga leads sa kalidad? Seryoso ba ang mga nag-inquire?"
*(Lead quality check — if low, this is a listing issue.)*

**Q4:** "Kung mayroon kang isang bagay na gusto mong baguhin o idagdag sa platform, ano iyon?"
*(Product feedback. Log this regardless.)*

**Q5:** "Ano ang sukatan ng tagumpay para sa iyo sa susunod na 30 araw?"
*(Sets expectations for Month 2. Critical for the Day 60 review.)*

### Minutes 25–29: Action Items & Next Steps
Summarize the top 3 actions for the next 30 days:
1. ______ (dealer action)
2. ______ (your action)
3. ______ (optional: new feature to explore)

> "Okay [Name], ito ang gagawin natin sa susunod na 30 araw. [Summarize actions.] Sa susunod na buwan, mag-check in tayo ulit. Ang target natin: [specific metric from Q5]."

### Minute 29–30: Close
> "Salamat sa oras mo ngayon. Masaya akong makita ang progress mo. Nandito lang kami para sa anumang kailangan mo — WhatsApp lang anytime. Maayos pa ito!"

## RED FLAGS TO WATCH DURING THE CALL

| Signal | What It Means | What to Do |
|--------|---------------|------------|
| Dealer sounds distracted or rushed | Low engagement, might not see the value | Reschedule if needed; do not rush the value moment |
| Says "hindi masyadong nagamit" repeatedly | Usage is low; at-risk of churn | Ask why — friction or time? Address it directly |
| Doesn't remember how many leads they got | Not checking the platform regularly | Walk them through where to find the metric |
| Immediately asks about pricing | Price sensitivity emerging | Reinforce ROI before the renewal ask |
| Asks about a competitor | Active consideration of alternatives | Ask what the competitor offered. Don't panic. |

---

## IF THE DEALER IS UNHAPPY

**Signs:** Flat tone, short answers, not asking questions, raises a complaint

**De-escalation script:**
> "[Name], salamat sa honesty mo — importanteng malaman ko ito. Ang gusto ko ay matugunan ang concern mo ngayon mismo. [Restate their concern.] Tama ba iyong naiintindihan ko?
>
> Naniniwala ako na may solusyon tayo dito. Pwede ba nating tingnan ito ngayon, o gusto mo bang mag-set up ng separate na call para focus tayo doon?"

**Never say:**
- "Hindi kami responsible sa..."
- "Ganoon talaga ang platform..."
- "Pero maraming dealers ang..."

**After the call:** Follow up within 2 hours with a written summary of the specific issue you're resolving and your timeline for resolution.

---

## IF THE DEALER IS HAPPY

After the value moment, if they are clearly satisfied — ask for the case study.

**Case study ask script:**
> "[Name], sobrang laking tulong ng story mo para sa ibang mga dealers na gusto ring sumali. Pwede ba nating i-document ang results mo? Hindi kailangan ng maraming oras — 10 questions lang, mga 20 minuto. Gagamitin namin ito para ipakita sa ibang dealers kung paano gumagana ang AutoBentaPH sa totoong mundo.
>
> Of course, ipapakita namin sa iyo ang final version bago kami mag-publish ng kahit ano."

If they say yes: schedule the case study interview at Month 3. Note it now.
