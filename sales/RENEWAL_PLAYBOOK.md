# Renewal Playbook
## AutoBentaPH — Dealer Retention & Renewal System

**Purpose:** Ensure every Founding Dealer renews at months 1, 3, 6, and 12. Target: 100% retention in Year 1.

---

## Part 1: Why Early SaaS Customers Don't Renew

Understanding the failure modes is the first step to preventing them.

### The 5 renewal killers

**1. They forgot why they signed**
Early customers sign in a moment of enthusiasm. By Day 30, the memory of the demo fades and the monthly charge just feels like a cost — especially if they haven't seen concrete results yet.
- Prevention: Remind them of the "before" state. Show them their progress.

**2. They never got into the habit**
If the platform isn't part of their daily routine by Day 14, it probably won't be. Habit formation is a Day 1–14 problem.
- Prevention: Onboarding that ends with a clear daily action they repeat.

**3. They had an unresolved frustration**
A small bug, a confusing UI, a slow response — if it happened and wasn't acknowledged, it festers. They don't complain. They just don't renew.
- Prevention: Proactive check-ins that explicitly ask "what's frustrating you?"

**4. Budget pressure at month-end**
When business is slow, subscriptions get reviewed. If the dealer can't articulate the value to themselves or their accountant, it gets cut.
- Prevention: Quantify value in their language. Not "30 leads" — "₱30,000 in potential commissions."

**5. They never got a win they could feel**
If the first 30 days didn't produce at least one tangible result — a lead, a sale, a time saved — the dealer has no emotional anchor to the platform.
- Prevention: Engineering at least one visible win in the first 14 days.

## Part 2: The Renewal Health Check Framework

Run this review 30 days before every renewal date.

### Health check questions to answer before the renewal conversation

**Activity:**
- How many times did they log in this month?
- Did they update inventory in the last 14 days?
- Are they using the CRM (moving leads through stages)?

**Results:**
- How many leads did they receive this month?
- What is their lead response rate?
- Have they closed or progressed any leads from the platform?

**Relationship:**
- Did any support issues go unresolved?
- Did they express frustration in any channel (WhatsApp, call, email)?
- When was the last time you spoke with them?

**Financial:**
- Are invoices being paid on time?
- Any payment friction in the last 2 months?

### Health score categories
- **Healthy (75–100):** Active, getting leads, paying on time → renewal is straightforward
- **Watch (50–74):** Inconsistent usage or slow response to leads → proactive intervention needed
- **At Risk (25–49):** Low activity, no recent wins, possible unresolved issue → renewal conversation is critical
- **Critical (<25):** Near-zero activity, payment issues → save call required before renewal conversation

## Part 3: 60-Day Pre-Renewal — Success Review Meeting

### When to schedule
Book the meeting at Day 60 (Month 2 milestone), which is 30 days before the Month 3 renewal.

### Meeting agenda (30 minutes via Zoom or in-person)

**0–5 min: Warm-up**
- Ask about the business: "Kumusta ang dealership? Kumusta ang sales ngayong buwan?"
- Do not start with metrics — start with them

**5–15 min: Results review**
Walk through the numbers. Use simple language.
- "Nakatanggap kayo ng [X] leads mula nang nag-launch kayo."
- "Ang average response time ninyo ay [X hours] — maganda iyon."
- "Naka-move kayo ng [X] leads through the CRM — iyon ay [X] potential customers na actively nasa pipeline ninyo."

**15–20 min: What's working / what's frustrating**
Ask explicitly:
- "Ano ang mga bagay na okay para sa inyo sa platform?"
- "May bagay ba na frustrating o hindi gumagana nang gaya ng inaasahan ninyo?"
- "May feature ba na gusto ninyong makita na wala pa?"

Take notes. Act on frustrations within 48 hours.

**20–25 min: Upcoming renewal**
```
Gusto ko ring sabihin — ang inyong subscription ay mag-re-renew sa [date]. Same rate, ₱3,599/buwan, same Founding Dealer terms.

Bago dumating ang invoice, gusto ko munang malaman kung may concerns kayo o kung may bagay na kailangan nating ayusin bago ang next month. May katanungan kayo?
```

**25–30 min: Next month preview**
- "Ang plano namin para sa next month ay [X feature or improvement]"
- "May isa akong recommendation para sa inyo para mapahusay pa ang performance ninyo..."

### Key questions to ask during the meeting
1. "Sa scale ng 1–10, gaano kasiyahan kayo sa AutoBentaPH ngayon?"
2. "Kung may isang bagay lang na mababago ninyo sa platform, ano iyon?"
3. "Nagrerekomenda ba kayo ng AutoBentaPH sa ibang dealers na kilala ninyo?"
4. "Kumusta ang ROI — nararamdaman ninyo ba ang value ng subscription?"
5. "Ano ang pinaka-importanteng resulta na gusto ninyong makita sa next 30 days?"

## Part 5: 15-Day Pre-Renewal — Confirmation Call

### Objective
Brief 10-minute call to confirm renewal intent, address last-minute concerns, and make the payment easy.

### Script
```
Opening: "[First name], si [YOUR NAME] ito. Ilang araw na lang bago ang renewal — gusto ko lang i-confirm na okay kayo at wala kayong concerns bago dumating ang invoice."

Middle: "Kumusta ang platform ngayong buwan? May bagay ba na gusto ninyong ipag-alam sa akin?"

Address anything they raise. Don't dismiss.

Closing: "Okay, ang invoice ay darating sa inyong email sa [date] — ₱3,599 lang as usual. Pwede kayong mag-GCash sa [number] o bank transfer. Kung may tanong kayo sa invoice, i-WhatsApp lang ako agad."
```

## Part 6: Renewal Meeting Agenda (Full 30-Minute Format)

Use for Month 3, 6, and 12 renewals (not monthly renewals — those are handled by invoice + WhatsApp).

**0–5 min:** How's business? Personal check-in.

**5–12 min:** Metrics review. Show them the numbers. Be specific.

**12–18 min:** Celebrate a win. Identify the best thing that happened in the period. Name it explicitly: "Iyon ang magandang resulta — [specific outcome]."

**18–23 min:** Handle concerns. "May bagay ba na hindi naging okay? Gusto kong marinig."

**23–27 min:** Close the renewal. "Okay — ipapadala ko ang renewal invoice sa [date]. Same terms. May concerns pa kayo?"

**27–30 min:** Next period preview. One exciting thing coming up that's relevant to them.

## Part 9: At-Risk Renewal — Intervention Protocol

If health score drops below 50 within 30 days of renewal:

### Step 1: Emergency check-in call (within 24 hours of detecting risk)
```
"[First name], si [YOUR NAME] ito. Napansin namin na may ilang linggo na na hindi kayo aktibo sa platform. Nag-aalala ako — gusto kong malaman kung okay ang lahat at kung may magagawa ako para matulungan kayo."
```

### Step 2: Offer a hands-on session
```
"Kung gusto ninyo, pwede kaming mag-schedule ng 30-minute working session — magpu-pull kami ng screen share at sama-sama nating i-set up ang inventory o CRM para gumana ito para sa inyo."
```

### Step 3: Identify the real blocker
Ask directly: "Kung hindi kayo gumagamit ng platform, may dahilan iyon — ano ang pinaka-malaking obstacle?"

Common answers and responses:
- "Walang leads" → Show them the listing quality issues, help optimize
- "Hindi ko alam kung paano gamitin" → Schedule training immediately
- "Abalang-abala" → Offer lighter usage pattern (15 min/day routine)
- "Hindi gumagana ang feature X" → Fix it within 24 hours

### Step 4: Set a 7-day recovery goal
```
"Okay — ang goal natin sa susunod na 7 days ay makakita ng isang malinaw na resulta. [Specific goal: e.g., 'I-upload natin ang 10 listings at makita kung makakakuha ng leads']. Susundan kita araw-araw sa next 7 days para matiyak na gumagana ito."
```

### Step 5: Renewal conversation after recovery goal met
Only have the renewal conversation after they've had a win.
