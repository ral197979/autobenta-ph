## Response Cards

### "Too expensive / ₱5,999 is a lot"
**Root concern:** Unclear ROI — they don't see the return yet
**Response:**
"Oo, gets ko. Pero let me show you the math. If you're selling [X] cars a month at ₱[Y] gross each, and we help you close just 2 more leads a month — that's ₱[X×2×Y]. The platform costs ₱5,999. That's [ratio]× ROI. Does that change the calculation for you?"
**Evidence:** ROI calculator with their actual numbers
**Bridge question:** "What would the ROI need to be for this to make sense for you?"
**When this works best:** After demo, when they've seen the CRM in action
**When it doesn't work:** When budget genuinely doesn't exist (usually means wrong prospect)

---

### "We already use Facebook, it's free"
**Root concern:** They don't see the gap Facebook creates — missed leads, no follow-up, no tracking
**Response:**
"Facebook is great for reach — we actually integrate with it. The problem is what happens after someone messages you. If you have 3 salespeople and 1 inbox, who's following up? How do you know which lead bought? Facebook tells you none of that. Let me show you what a dealer's CRM looks like 30 days in — you'll see exactly where Facebook stops and where the money gets left on the table."
**Evidence:** Demo the CRM pipeline — show an unresponded lead aging out
**Bridge question:** "How many Facebook messages do you get a month that never turn into a sale?"
**When this works best:** Early in the demo before showing CRM
**When it doesn't work:** If they genuinely have a dedicated person managing the FB inbox well

---

### "We need to think about it"
**Root concern:** Either unclear value, fear of commitment, or needs partner/boss approval
**Response:**
"Of course — this is a real decision. Can I ask: what specifically do you need to think through? Is it the price, the setup, or do you need to loop in someone else first? I want to make sure I give you exactly the information that helps you decide."
**Evidence:** Not applicable — this is a discovery question
**Bridge question:** "What would you need to see or hear to feel confident moving forward?"
**When this works best:** Immediately — don't let them off the call without naming the specific blocker
**When it doesn't work:** If they're genuinely uninterested and just being polite

---

### "We don't have time to set it up"
**Root concern:** Fear of implementation burden — they picture weeks of work
**Response:**
"Setup takes less time than you think. We do it with you. Day 1: I send you credentials and we do a 30-minute call together. Day 2: we import your inventory — if you have an Excel list, it takes 15 minutes. Day 3: you're live. I've done this before — I won't let it drag. What day this week works for that first 30-minute call?"
**Evidence:** Reference the Day 1–3 onboarding plan from FIRST_CUSTOMER_SUCCESS_PLAN.md
**Bridge question:** "If setup only took 3 days and I handled most of it — does that change things?"
**When this works best:** Mid-conversation, after they've seen value in the demo
**When it doesn't work:** When they're using "time" as a proxy for "not interested"

---

### "Our team won't use a new system"
**Root concern:** Past implementation failures; resistance to change from sales staff
**Response:**
"That's the most common reason CRMs fail — the system is built for managers, not for salespeople. Ours is designed differently. The salesperson opens one screen, sees their leads, clicks to call or message. That's it. No training manual. I've seen teams adopt it in a week. Can we include one of your salespeople in the demo? It's harder to say no when they've tried it themselves."
**Evidence:** Demo the dealer-facing lead inbox — show simplicity
**Bridge question:** "Which person on your team is most resistant to new tools? Let me talk to them."
**When this works best:** When the DM is on board but worried about team adoption
**When it doesn't work:** If the DM themselves isn't bought in

---

### "You're too new / no track record"
**Root concern:** Risk aversion — they don't want to be a guinea pig
**Response:**
"Fair concern. Here's what I can offer: 14-day money-back guarantee. If you're not getting leads in 14 days, full refund — no questions. You're not betting on our track record; you're testing it with zero risk. The dealers who sign now also get the Founding Dealer price — ₱3,599/mo locked forever. That's the trade for being early."
**Evidence:** Founding Dealer terms; 14-day money-back policy
**Bridge question:** "If the risk is zero — 14-day refund — what's stopping you from trying it?"
**When this works best:** After the demo, when they're interested but hesitant
**When it doesn't work:** If they need established case studies (usually enterprise, not SME dealers)

---

### "We're happy with our current system"
**Root concern:** Status quo bias — they've adapted to the pain and don't see it as a problem
**Response:**
"I hear that. Can I ask — how are you tracking which leads got followed up versus which ones dropped off? And when a sale closes, how do you know which source it came from — Facebook, Philkotse, referral? Most dealers using Excel or Facebook are actually leaving 20–30% of leads un-followed-up without knowing it. I can show you in 10 minutes. If I'm wrong, I'll stop bothering you."
**Evidence:** Demo showing lead aging and source attribution
**Bridge question:** "What would have to be true about our platform for it to be worth switching?"
**When this works best:** When they're on Excel or paper — the gap is real and demonstrable
**When it doesn't work:** If they have an actual working CRM (rare in this segment)

---

### "Kailangan ko pang i-discuss sa partner"
**Root concern:** Needs buy-in from a co-owner or spouse — this is a legitimate blocker
**Response:**
"Sige po, tama naman. May I ask — ano yung pinaka-importanteng tanong ng partner mo? Gusto ko pong mag-prepare ng summary para sa kanya — isang page lang, yung price, yung ROI estimate, at yung guarantee. Para hindi mo na kailangang i-explain lahat mula sa umpisa. Kailan kayo magkasama — puwede ba nating i-schedule yung call na kasama na siya?"
**Evidence:** One-page proposal summary; ROI calculation with their numbers
**Bridge question:** "Kailan po available ang partner mo para sa isang 15-minute call?"
**When this works best:** When the DM is genuinely positive but partner is the gatekeeper
**When it doesn't work:** If "partner" means "I need more time" (probe to find out)

---

### "We tried CRM before and it failed"
**Root concern:** Specific past trauma — previous system was too complex, didn't get used, wasted money
**Response:**
"Tell me what happened — what system was it, and why did it fail?" [Listen fully.] "Got it. That's actually the most common story I hear. [Name the specific failure — usually: too complex, no onboarding, no one enforced usage.] We're built differently for exactly that reason. Setup is 3 days, not 3 weeks. And I personally do the onboarding with you — not a support ticket. If it's not working in 14 days, full refund."
**Evidence:** Onboarding plan; 14-day refund
**Bridge question:** "What would have made that previous system actually work for you?"
**When this works best:** After letting them fully vent about the past failure — then contrast
**When it doesn't work:** If the failure was their own internal discipline (be honest about that)

---

### "Wala pang budget ngayon"
**Root concern:** Either genuinely cash-constrained or budget isn't allocated yet
**Response:**
"I understand — timing matters. Can I ask: is it that there's literally no cash available right now, or that this isn't in the budget this month? If it's the latter — our Founding Dealer price is ₱3,599/mo, roughly ₱120/day. If you sell one extra car a month because of better lead follow-up, that covers the platform cost 10× over. The Founding Dealer spots are limited to 5 total — I'd hate for you to miss the locked price while we wait. When does your budget cycle reset?"
**Evidence:** Founding Dealer pricing; ROI with their numbers
**Bridge question:** "If the price were ₱1,999/mo, would you move forward today?" [Reveals if it's real or an excuse]
**When this works best:** When they're interested but genuinely timing-constrained
**When it doesn't work:** If it's a genuine cash crisis — wrong time, note for follow-up in 60 days
