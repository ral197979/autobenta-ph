# Customer Success Review Template
## AutoBentaPH — Monthly Dealer Review

**Use for:** Monthly success reviews with every active dealer
**Format:** 30-minute Zoom or in-person meeting
**Frequency:** Monthly (Month 1, 2, 3, 6, 12 and every month in between)

---

## Pre-Meeting Preparation (15 Minutes Before)

Before the call, pull together:
- [ ] Current health score and score breakdown
- [ ] Leads received this month (total)
- [ ] Leads responded to (and % response rate)
- [ ] Lead response time (average hours to first reply)
- [ ] Inventory: number of active listings, date of last update
- [ ] CRM stages used and leads in each stage
- [ ] Any support tickets or complaints this month
- [ ] Previous meeting notes and action items — which were completed?
- [ ] One specific win to celebrate
- [ ] One specific improvement opportunity to raise

---

## Meeting Agenda (30-Minute Format)

### 0–5 min: Personal check-in
Start with them, not with data.
- "Kumusta ang dealership? Kumusta ang buwan?"
- "May bagong stock ba kayong dumating?"
- "Kumusta ang pamilya?"

**Why this matters:** Filipino business relationships are personal first. A cold data-first opening signals you see them as a number. 2–3 minutes of genuine conversation sets the tone for honest feedback.

---

### 5–15 min: Metrics review

Present their numbers in plain language. Never show raw database fields. Translate everything into business terms.

**Template:**
```
"Okay, tingnan natin ang inyong [Month] results.

Leads this month: [X]
This means [X] buyers actively reached out about your inventory.

Lead response rate: [X]%
This means you responded to [X] out of [X] leads. [If below 80%:] "May [X] leads na hindi pa nare-respond — gusto ko nating pag-usapan ito."

Average response time: [X hours]
[If under 4h:] "Napakabilis — nasa guarantee range ninyo pa rin ng Founding Dealer SLA."
[If over 4h:] "May ilang leads na naghintay ng [X hours] — gusto naming tulungan kayong mapabilis ito."

Active listings: [X] listings
Last inventory update: [X days ago]
[If over 7 days:] "May mga listing kayo na medyo matagal nang hindi na-update — maaaring makaapekto ito sa views."

CRM stages: You have [X] leads in 'New', [X] in 'Contacted', [X] in 'Qualified'
This means [X] potential buyers are actively in your sales pipeline."
```

**Health score interpretation for non-technical dealers:**

| Score | What to say |
|-------|-------------|
| 85–100 | "Ang inyong score ay [X]/100 — meaning you're getting strong value from the platform. You're in the top tier of our dealers." |
| 70–84 | "Ang inyong score ay [X]/100 — maayos ito. Nakikita namin ang dalawang bagay na pwedeng i-improve para mapataas pa." |
| 50–69 | "Ang inyong score ay [X]/100 — may mga opportunities para mapabuti ito. Gusto kong ipag-usap natin kung paano." |
| Below 50 | "Ang score ninyo ay [X]/100 — ipinapakita nito na may ilang bagay na kailangan nating ayusin nang magkasama. Hindi ito judgment — gusto ko lang matulungan kayo makuha ang buong value ng subscription." |

---

### 15–22 min: Questions to ask

Ask these in order. Don't rush. Listen fully before responding.

**1. What's working?**
"Ano sa palagay ninyo ang pinaka-maayos na bagay na nangyari sa platform ngayong buwan?"

Wait. They might say "wala naman" — that's a signal. Dig deeper: "Kahit maliit na bagay — may napansin ba kayong positive?"

**2. What's frustrating?**
"May bagay ba na frustrating o hindi gumagana nang inaasahan ninyo?"

This is the most important question. Be silent after asking. Don't fill the pause. Dealers will only share frustrations if they feel safe. If they share something, write it down visibly (or say "isusulat ko ito").

**3. What do you wish the platform could do?**
"Kung may isang feature na pwede naming idagdag para gawing mas madali ang inyong trabaho, ano iyon?"

This is gold for your product roadmap and makes the dealer feel like a co-creator.

**4. How are your buyers finding you?**
"Sa mga leads na natanggap ninyo, paano nila kayo nakita — sa AutoBentaPH lang, o may iba pa?"

Understand their marketing mix. This helps you position value correctly.

**5. What does success look like for you next month?**
"Ano ang gusto ninyong mangyari sa susunod na buwan — isang specific na resulta na masasabi ninyong 'okay, nagtagumpay kami'?"

This sets the success criteria for the next review.

---

### 22–27 min: Action items

Capture 1–3 specific action items from the meeting. Maximum 3 — more than that and nothing gets done.

Format:
```
ACTION ITEMS

[Dealer first name] will:
1. Update inventory listings by [specific date]
2. Respond to the 3 pending leads in CRM by [tomorrow's date]

[YOUR NAME] will:
1. Fix [specific issue they raised] by [date]
2. Send [specific resource or feature guide] by [tomorrow]
```

Confirm each item verbally: "Okay ba ito sa inyo? Aayusin ko ang [issue] bago mag-[date]."

---

### 27–30 min: Expansion conversation (for Healthy dealers only)

Only raise this if health score is 75+ AND they've expressed satisfaction.

**Month 3 expansion:**
```
"May isa akong gustong i-share — optional ito, walang pressure. Ang aming Featured Listings add-on ay mag-re-release na sa [date]. Kasama doon ang 5 featured slots na nakikita ng mas maraming buyers. Gusto ninyong marinig ang detalye?"
```

If they ask: Price is ₱999/month. Show them the 3x views data.

## NPS Collection

**When to ask:** Month 1, Month 3, Month 6, and Month 12 (not every month — NPS fatigue is real).

**How to ask (at the end of the meeting):**
```
"Isang huling tanong bago tayo mag-wrap up. Sa scale ng 0–10, gaano kalamang na irerekomenda ninyo ang AutoBentaPH sa ibang dealers na kilala ninyo?

0 = hindi talaga
10 = tiyak na irerekomenda ko
```

**Score interpretation (for your records, not to share with them):**
- 9–10: Promoter — ask for a testimonial
- 7–8: Passive — good but not a referral source yet
- 0–6: Detractor — find out why, fix it

**If they score 9–10:**
```
"Napakagandang marinig iyon! May isang request ako — pwede ba kaming gumamit ng inyong experience bilang testimonial sa aming website? Isang sentence lang — kung ano ang pinaka-nagustuhan ninyo sa platform. Okay iyon sa inyo?"
```

## Success Story Capture

### When a win happens (any meeting)

When a dealer shares a success — a sale made, a lead converted, a time saved — document it immediately.

**What to capture:**
- What was the outcome? (e.g., "Nabenta ang isang 2019 Toyota Vios dahil sa AutoBentaPH lead")
- What did it mean for them? (e.g., "Iyon ang unang benta nila na directly trackable sa platform")
- In their exact words (quote, not paraphrase)

**Script for capturing the story:**
```
"[First name], iyon ay napakagandang resulta. Pwede ba akong magtanong — bago kayo sumali sa AutoBentaPH, paano kayo nag-ge-generate ng leads? At paano nagbago iyon ngayon?"
```

Let them tell the before/after story in their own words. Record (with permission) or take detailed notes.

## Review Scheduling Rules

- Month 1 review: Day 30
- Month 2 review: Day 60
- Month 3 review: Day 90 (full review — include renewal discussion)
- Months 4–12: Monthly, last week of each month
- Always schedule the next meeting at the end of the current meeting
- Send a calendar invite immediately after the meeting
