# AutoBentaPH — Dealer Objection Handling Guide

Sales-ready response scripts for the 12 most common dealer objections.

**How to use this guide:**
Each entry follows the same structure: understand the root concern first, deliver the response script, then use the bridge question to move forward. Never argue. Validate, reframe, and keep the conversation going.

---

## Objection 1: "We already use Facebook Marketplace"

**Root concern:** "I'm getting leads for free — why would I pay for something I don't need?"

**Response script:**

"Facebook Marketplace is a great place to advertise — and I'm not suggesting you stop. The problem isn't where your listings are. The problem is what happens after a buyer messages you.

Right now, when someone sends you a message on Facebook at 9 PM, what's your process? You probably see it the next morning, reply, and by then they've already bought from someone else. Facebook doesn't tell you how many people messaged you and never got a reply. It doesn't tell you which listings get the most inquiries. It doesn't tell you if your price is too high or your photos are weak.

AutoBentaPH doesn't replace Facebook — it works alongside it. Your Facebook leads can route directly into the CRM. You get an instant alert, a response timer, and a full lead history. You're still using Facebook. You're just not losing the leads you're generating there.

Most of our dealers who said this to us in the beginning now tell us Facebook is still their biggest lead source — they just stopped losing 60% of those leads before they could respond."

**Bridge question:** "When was the last time a buyer messaged you on Facebook and you didn't respond in time — do you have any idea how often that happens?"

## Objection 2: "We can't afford ₱5,999/month"

**Root concern:** "I don't see enough certainty of return to justify this expense."

**Response script:**

"I hear you — ₱5,999 is real money, and I wouldn't ask you to spend it if I couldn't show you the math.

Let me ask you this: what's your average gross profit per unit sold? [Wait for answer.] And how many cars are you selling per month right now? [Wait for answer.]

[Example — adjust with their numbers:] If you're clearing ₱50,000 per unit and selling 10 cars a month, your monthly gross is ₱500,000. The platform costs ₱5,999 — that's 1.2% of your current gross profit. And that's before you close a single extra unit.

Our conservative model shows dealers adding 5–10 additional units per month once they stop losing leads and start converting their existing pipeline more effectively. At ₱50,000 gross per unit, that's ₱250,000–₱500,000 in additional monthly gross profit against a ₱5,999 platform cost.

The question isn't whether you can afford ₱5,999/month. The question is whether you can afford to keep losing the leads you're already generating."

**Bridge question:** "Would it help if we built out a quick ROI estimate using your actual numbers — leads per month, current conversion rate, average gross per unit?"

## Objection 3: "Our Excel system works fine"

**Root concern:** "Change is disruptive and I'm skeptical the benefit is worth the hassle."

**Response script:**

"Excel is a perfectly reasonable tool — and a lot of our most successful dealers started exactly where you are. The question isn't whether Excel works. The question is what Excel can't do.

Excel can't send you an alert when a new lead comes in at 11 PM. It can't tell you that a lead has been sitting untouched for 18 hours. It can't show you which salesperson is converting and which one is losing deals in the negotiation stage. It can't automatically remove a sold unit from your Philkotse listing and your AutoBentaPH listing and your Facebook page at the same time. And it can't show a buyer a verified OR/CR badge that makes them trust your listing more than the one next to it.

Excel records what already happened. AutoBentaPH tells you what's happening right now and what you need to do next.

The migration from Excel is straightforward — we have a CSV import that pulls your existing inventory directly into the platform. Most dealers are fully switched over in under a day."

**Bridge question:** "What's the biggest thing you wish your current Excel system could do that it can't?"

## Objection 4: "We don't have an IT team to set this up"

**Root concern:** "This sounds like it requires technical expertise we don't have."

**Response script:**

"That's a completely fair concern — and honestly, it's one of the reasons we built AutoBentaPH the way we did. Most dealer software is designed for US franchise dealers who have an IT department. We're designed for Philippine dealers who don't.

Setup for most dealers takes less than 2 hours. Here's what's involved:

1. You sign up and we create your account — 10 minutes.
2. You upload your inventory via CSV (we provide the template) or we help you key in your first 5–10 units manually — 30–60 minutes.
3. We walk through the CRM with you and your sales team on a 30-minute call — that's it.

If you use CDK or Reynolds, the V8Atlas sync requires your API credentials from them — but that's one email to your DMS provider, not a technical project.

We've onboarded dealers who had never used any software beyond WhatsApp and Facebook. They were live and generating leads within 48 hours."

**Bridge question:** "Who handles your current technology setup — even just your phone or Facebook page? Because if they can manage that, they can manage AutoBentaPH."

## Objection 5: "We're already on Philkotse / OLX"

**Root concern:** "I'm already getting exposure on listing sites — what's different about AutoBentaPH?"

**Response script:**

"Philkotse and OLX are listing portals. Think of them like a bulletin board — you put your car up, buyers browse, and when someone is interested, they contact you. That's where their job ends.

AutoBentaPH is a dealer operating platform. It starts where Philkotse ends. When a buyer inquires on AutoBentaPH, that lead goes into your CRM automatically — with their contact info, the listing they were interested in, and a response timer. You can track where that buyer goes through your pipeline, who's handling them, and what it takes to close them.

And here's the thing: you don't have to choose. Dealers on AutoBentaPH still list on Philkotse. The difference is that leads from all sources — Philkotse, Facebook, AutoBentaPH, walk-ins — all flow into one dashboard. You stop managing five different inboxes and start managing one pipeline.

Beyond that: AutoBentaPH listings carry OR/CR verification and V8Atlas trust scores. Philkotse listings don't. Buyers who see a verified badge are significantly more likely to contact you — and more likely to show up when they do."

**Bridge question:** "How do you currently manage the inquiries that come in from Philkotse — is there one person handling that, or does it go to multiple people?"

## Objection 6: "What if we lose our Facebook followers?"

**Root concern:** "My Facebook page is an asset I've built — I'm worried about abandoning it."

**Response script:**

"You won't lose them — and you shouldn't leave Facebook. Your Facebook page is an asset. Keep posting, keep running your community, keep the followers you've built.

What AutoBentaPH does is work alongside Facebook, not replace it. You continue posting your listings on Facebook. But you also post them on AutoBentaPH, where buyers are specifically searching for verified, trustworthy used cars — not scrolling through food posts and news.

The difference is what happens when those Facebook followers message you. Right now, that message lands in your Facebook Messenger inbox, and it either gets answered quickly or it gets lost. With AutoBentaPH, you can route those Facebook leads directly into your CRM. Your followers stay on Facebook. Your lead management moves to a professional system.

Think of it this way: Facebook is your marketing channel. AutoBentaPH is your operations system. You need both."

**Bridge question:** "How many messages do you get on Facebook per day, and what percentage of those do you think your team responds to within the first hour?"

## Objection 7: "How do we know the leads are real buyers?"

**Root concern:** "I've wasted time on tire-kickers and lowballers from online platforms."

**Response script:**

"This is the right question — and it's exactly why we built the verification system the way we did.

On Facebook Marketplace, anyone can send an inquiry. There's no friction, no intent signal, no qualification. You get volume, but a lot of it is noise — people who are curious, who are three months away from buying, or who are just testing prices.

On AutoBentaPH, a few things filter for intent:

First, buyers who inquire on verified listings have already seen the OR/CR badge and trust score. They're not asking if the car is legitimate — they already know it is. That means their inquiry is about price and availability, not verification.

Second, our lead scoring system flags inquiry patterns. Leads from buyers who have completed profile verification, who have messaged multiple specific listings (not just browsing), and who have recent activity are scored higher. Your CRM shows you the score before you invest time in the conversation.

Third, over time, our platform data shows a clear pattern: buyers who inquire on verified listings with inspection badges are 2–3x more likely to schedule a test drive than buyers from unverified platforms.

We're not promising zero tire-kickers — no platform can do that. But we dramatically improve the ratio."

**Bridge question:** "On a scale of 1 to 10, how much of your current lead time do you think is wasted on people who were never going to buy?"

## Objection 8: "We tried a CRM before and the team didn't use it"

**Root concern:** "Software adoption in my team is a real problem — I've been burned before."

**Response script:**

"That's one of the most honest things a dealer owner can say, and I respect it. CRM adoption failure is the number one reason dealer software projects fail — and it almost always comes down to the same thing: the software was built for management, not for salespeople.

If your team has to do extra work — extra data entry, extra clicks, extra steps — to record a lead, they won't do it. The system has to make their day easier, not harder.

AutoBentaPH is designed the other way around. When a lead comes in, it's already in the system — the salesperson doesn't have to enter anything. When they move a lead from 'Contacted' to 'Qualified,' it's one drag-and-drop on a Kanban board. The response-time timer is visible to them — it creates accountability without requiring a manager to chase anyone.

The other issue: if management doesn't check the system, salespeople don't use it. We make it easy for you to look at one dashboard every morning and see exactly where every lead stands, who's handled it, and what the next step is. That visibility creates the habit on both sides.

We also do a team training session as part of onboarding. Not just setup — we walk through real scenarios with your salespeople so they know exactly what to do."

**Bridge question:** "When the previous CRM failed, was it more that the team didn't have time to enter data, or that they didn't see a benefit from using it?"

**Root concern:** "I don't want to build my operations on a platform that might disappear."

**Response script:**

"It's a fair question to ask of any SaaS platform, and you should ask it.

Here's what we can tell you about our stability:

AutoBentaPH is production-ready with a certification score of 90/100, built on top of V8Atlas — an established DMS infrastructure that supports CDK, Reynolds & Reynolds, and DealerSocket integrations. We're not a side project or a startup running on fumes.

On the data side: your inventory data is yours. You can export your full inventory and lead history as a CSV at any time. You're never locked in. If we ever stopped operating — which we're not planning — you'd have your data in a format you could move anywhere.

On the contract side: we don't ask for annual commitments upfront. Month-to-month billing means your exposure is limited. The Founding Dealer Program does lock in pricing, but the commitment is mutual — we guarantee your rate and you help us shape the product.

The honest answer is: no one can guarantee they'll never shut down. What we can guarantee is that your data is portable, your contract doesn't lock you in, and the platform is built on infrastructure that's been operating in the PH dealer space for years through V8Atlas."

**Bridge question:** "Besides data portability, what would give you confidence that a platform is built to last? We want to understand what matters most to you."

## Objection 10: "We want to wait and see"

**Root concern:** "I'm risk-averse — I want someone else to prove it works before I commit."

**Response script:**

"I understand the instinct — waiting is safe. But I want to make sure you're making that decision with full information.

Here's what 'waiting' costs in this specific case:

Every month you wait, leads arrive and leave without a system to capture them. If your current conversion rate is 12% and the platform adds 5 percentage points of conversion efficiency, that's roughly 4 extra cars per month at ₱50,000 gross each — ₱200,000 in gross profit you didn't capture. Over three months of waiting, that's ₱600,000. The platform would have cost ₱18,000 over those three months.

There's also the Founding Dealer Program angle: we have 5 spots at ₱3,599/month locked forever. When those spots fill, the price is ₱5,999. Waiting doesn't just cost you leads — it costs you ₱2,400/month permanently if you join after the program closes.

Finally: the risk of trying it is very low. Month-to-month billing, your data is always exportable, and we include a dedicated onboarding session to make sure you're actually using it effectively. If after 60 days it's not delivering results, you cancel and you're out ₱11,998. That's the cost of one half-unit sale at your margin."

**Bridge question:** "What specifically would you need to see in the next 30–60 days to feel confident this was the right decision?"

## Objection 11: "Can we negotiate the price?"

**Root concern:** "I want a better deal, or I'm testing to see if the price is real."

**Response script:**

"I appreciate you asking directly — it's a fair question.

The listed prices aren't negotiating anchors. ₱2,999 for Verified Dealer and ₱5,999 for Dealer Pro are the standard rates, and they're set where they are because the unit economics work at those prices for both sides.

That said, there is one legitimate way to get a better price: the Founding Dealer Program. Five dealers get 40% off Dealer Pro — ₱3,599/month locked permanently. That's a real discount with a real contract commitment from us. It's not 'ask and receive' — it's a limited program designed for dealers who are committing early and willing to engage with the product team.

If you're looking at the Verified Dealer plan at ₱2,999, that's already priced at roughly ₱100/day. It's designed to be accessible. Discounting below that would mean we're subsidizing your subscription, which doesn't create a sustainable relationship.

What I can offer instead: I'll make sure you get the full value of the plan you're on. That means a proper onboarding, team training, help setting up your first listings optimally, and a check-in call at 30 days to make sure you're getting ROI."

**Bridge question:** "Is the price concern about the absolute number, or is it about certainty of return? Because those are two different conversations."

## Objection 12: "We need to discuss with our partners first"

**Root concern:** "There are other decision-makers, and I'm not empowered (or not willing) to decide alone."

**Response script:**

"Absolutely — and I'd expect that for a business decision like this. Can I ask: who else is involved in the decision, and what are their biggest concerns likely to be?

The reason I ask is that I want to make sure you go into that conversation with the right information. If your partner's concern is cost, I want to make sure you have the ROI model. If it's implementation effort, I want to give you the onboarding guide. If it's risk, I want to make sure you can explain the data portability and month-to-month billing.

One thing that helps these conversations move faster: a 15-minute call with both of you together, so I can address both sets of questions directly. We're not a high-pressure close — I'd rather spend 15 minutes making sure both partners are genuinely confident than rush one person into a decision that creates friction internally.

If a joint call doesn't work, can we at least set a specific date by which you'll have the internal conversation done? 'We need to discuss it' without a timeline often means it doesn't happen. I want to respect your process — I just want to make sure this doesn't fall through the cracks."

**Bridge question:** "When do you expect to have that conversation, and would it help to include me on a brief call so I can answer questions directly?"
