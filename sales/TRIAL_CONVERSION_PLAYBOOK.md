# Trial Conversion Playbook
**Goal:** Convert every 90-day free trial dealer to a paying Founding Dealer at ₱3,599/month.  
**Owner:** Founder  
**Trigger:** Runs from the moment a dealer activates their free trial.

---

## The Conversion Goal

> **By Day 75, every trial dealer has received a personal conversion ask and has said yes — or given a clear objection you can work with.**

Don't wait until Day 89 to ask. By then you're chasing, not selling.  
The trial is the sales process. Use all 90 days.

---

## Day-by-Day Timeline

### Day 1 — Welcome and confirm access

**Goal:** Make them feel set up, not abandoned. Confirm they know what they have.

**Action:** Send the WhatsApp message below within 2 hours of signup. If they haven't listed a car yet, offer to help.

**WhatsApp template — Day 1:**
```
Uy [Name]! Welcome sa AutoBentaPH. 90 days libre ka — walang credit card, full access sa lahat.

Para makapagsimula:
1. I-list mo ang 1-3 cars mo
2. Buyers na naghahanap ng [make/model] ang makakakita agad

Kung kailangan mo ng tulong sa pag-list, sabihin mo lang — magagawa natin via WhatsApp, 15 minutes.

Anong car ang gusto mong i-list muna?
```

---

### Day 7 — First listing check

**Goal:** Make sure they've listed at least one car. If not, remove the friction now — before the habit dies.

**Action:** Check their account. If 0 listings — send the nudge. If 1+ listed — send the celebration.

**WhatsApp template — Day 7 (no listing yet):**
```
[Name], kamusta na? Nakita ko hindi pa naka-list ang cars mo.

Normal 'yan — busy ang mga dealer. Pero ganito: sabihin mo sa akin yung plate number o details ng isang kotse, gagawin ko 'yung listing para sa iyo. 10 minutes lang via WhatsApp.

Anong kotse ang gusto nating i-list muna?
```

**WhatsApp template — Day 7 (listing live):**
```
[Name], live na 'yung listing mo — buyers na pwedeng makakita.

May tanong ka ba sa platform o gusto mong ayusin sa listing? Andito lang ako.
```

---

### Day 14 — First lead or first milestone

**Goal:** Celebrate any win, no matter how small. First view, first favorite, first inquiry — lahat importante.

**Action:** Check their dashboard. Find something positive to highlight.

**WhatsApp template — Day 14:**
```
[Name], 2 weeks na kayo sa AutoBentaPH.

[Customize: "Nakakita na ng X buyers ng [car]. / May [X] nagpaka-favorite na. / Dumating na ang unang inquiry ninyo."]

Kung may questions ka o gusto mong i-optimize ang listing, tawagan natin ng 10 minutes. Kailan okay sa iyo?
```

---

### Day 30 — 30-day check-in call

**Goal:** Voice-to-voice conversation. Review results, answer questions, identify if they need help. This is also your first soft read on whether they'll convert.

**Action:** Book a 15-minute Viber/WhatsApp call. Don't skip this — dealers who get a 30-day call convert at a higher rate.

**Call agenda:**
1. "Ilang leads nakuha ninyo so far?"
2. "Anong kotse ang pinaka-maraming tinitingnan?"
3. "May improvements ba na gusto ninyo sa platform?"
4. Soft plant: "Sa Day 90, mag-oofferan ka ng Founding Dealer rate — ₱3,599/mo, locked for life. Just a heads up para hindi ka masurpresa."

**WhatsApp template — Day 30 (to book the call):**
```
[Name], isang buwan na kayo sa AutoBentaPH! Gusto ko sanang mag-check in — 15 minutes lang para i-review natin ang leads at listings.

Kailan okay sa iyo? Viber call or WhatsApp lang, kahit dito sa chat.
```

---

### Day 60 — Start the conversion conversation

**Goal:** Plant the conversion ask early. Don't surprise them on Day 89.

**Action:** Send the message below. Listen for objections — this is the time to address them, not Day 85.

**WhatsApp template — Day 60:**
```
[Name], 60 days na kayo sa trial — 30 days na lang.

Anung naisip mo sa platform so far? Nakakatulong ba ang leads? Gusto ko malaman bago matapos ang trial.

At saka — kapag tapos na ang 90 days, mag-oofferan kita ng Founding Dealer rate: ₱3,599/mo, locked for life. Isa sa mga unang dealers ka, kaya 'yan ang rate mo kahit mag-increase kami ng price. Worth it naman?
```

**If they raise an objection here — work it now.** Don't defer. See the Objections section below.

---

### Day 75 — Conversion ask

**Goal:** Get a yes or a clear objection. This is the primary close.

**Action:** Personal message. Reference specific results they've gotten. Ask directly.

**WhatsApp template — Day 75:**
```
[Name], 75 days ka na sa AutoBentaPH — 15 days na lang ang trial mo.

So far: [X leads, X inquiries, X listings na live — fill in actual numbers].

Gusto ko i-lock in ang Founding Dealer rate mo bago matapos ang trial: ₱3,599/mo, locked for life. Kahit mag-increase kami ng price next year, 'yan pa rin ang price mo.

Gusto mong i-continue? Sasabihan kita ng exact na steps para ma-activate — 5 minutes lang.
```

**If no response in 24 hours:** follow up with a voice note via WhatsApp. Personal > text.

---

### Day 85 — Urgency follow-up

**Goal:** Last real window to handle objections before the trial ends.

**Action:** If they haven't converted, send this. If they've raised objections, address them directly here.

**WhatsApp template — Day 85:**
```
[Name], 5 days na lang ang trial mo.

Kung gusto mong i-lock in ang ₱3,599/mo Founding Dealer rate — kailangan ko ng sagot mo ngayon. Kapag natapos na ang trial at nag-open ulit ka ng account, regular price na ang applicable.

Gusto mo pa ring ituloy? Sabihin mo lang — i-activate ko agad.
```

---

### Day 89 — Last day warning

**Goal:** One last personal touch. Not templated — write it yourself.

**Action:** Personal WhatsApp. No template. Reference something specific about their account, a car they listed, a lead they got. Make it feel like you actually know them, because by now you should.

**What to say (write your own, but cover these points):**
- Bukas na magtatapos ang trial
- Specific result na nakita mo sa kanilang account
- Ask: gusto pa bang ituloy?
- If yes: i-send ang payment link agad
- If no: thank them, ask for feedback, leave the door open

---

### Day 90 — Trial ends

**Outcome A — Converted:** Dealer is now active at ₱3,599/mo. Add to founding dealer tracker. Send a short thank-you message.

**Outcome B — Churned:** Send one final message:
```
[Name], natapos na ang trial mo ngayon. Salamat sa pagsubok sa AutoBentaPH.

Kung gusto mong bumalik — andito lang kami. Founding Dealer rate maaaring hindi na available, pero makikipag-usap kami.

Isang tanong lang bago ako umalis: bakit hindi naituloy? Feedback mo makakatulong sa amin na mapabuti ang platform.
```

## Objections and Responses

### "Magbabayad ba ako after 3 months?"
> "Ikaw ang magde-decide pagkatapos ng 3 months. Kung gusto mong ituloy — ₱3,599 lang per month. Isa lang na sale at nare-recover mo na ang isang taon. At Founding Dealer rate 'yan — locked for life kahit mag-increase kami ng price."

### "Wala pa akong leads na nakuha"
> "Okay — tingnan natin bakit. Ilang listings ka ba meron? Ilang reviews ang listings? Baka may kailangan nating i-adjust. Gusto mo bang mag-usap ng 15 minutes — bago mag-expire ang trial mo?"

### "Mahal pa rin ang ₱3,599"
> "Isa lang na sale — kahit ₱30,000 ang margin mo — nare-recover mo na ang buong taon. Kung wala kang makuhang sale mula sa Ryderr sa unang 3 months ng paid subscription, sagutin ko personally. Fair ba 'yan?"

### "Hindi ko pa kailangan ngayon"
> "Gets ko. Pero ganito — 'yung Founding Dealer rate na locked for life, available lang 'yan habang early stage pa kami. Kapag nagtaas na kami ng price, hindi ko na maibibigay ang rate na 'to. Kung gusto mong i-lock in ngayon, sagutin mo lang — kayang-kaya."

### "Pag-isipan ko pa"
> "Sige. Kailan okay mag-usap ulit? Gusto ko lang malaman kung may concern ka na hindi ko pa nasasagot."
