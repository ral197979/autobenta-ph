# Dealer Onboarding Playbook

**Purpose:** Step-by-step process for onboarding a dealer from the moment they pay through their first 30 days. Every step is assigned to you (the founder) unless noted.

---

## Day 0 — Same Day as Payment

Payment confirmed. Start the clock. Everything below must happen today.

- [ ] Send Founding Dealer welcome email (use `FOUNDING_DEALER_OFFER` template — modify for "welcome" tone since they already signed)
- [ ] Create their dealer account manually: Admin Panel → Dealers → Invite Dealer → enter their email
- [ ] Send login credentials via WhatsApp (more reliable than email for first-time login)
  - Include URL: [your-domain]/dealer/login
  - Include their email and temporary password
  - Include: "Mag-log in na kayo anytime — nandoon na ang account ninyo"
- [ ] Set up Success Plan: Admin → Success Plans → Create → assign to their dealer account
- [ ] Log payment in CRM, move stage to `onboarding`
- [ ] Send a brief WhatsApp: "Kumusta [Name]! Welcome sa AutoBentaPH. Napadala ko na ang login details ninyo. Bukas naman tayo mag-onboarding call para masimulang mag-upload ng listings — anong oras okay sa inyo?"

---

## Day 1 — First Login

Goal: They have at least 5 listings live by end of Day 1.

**Schedule a 30-minute onboarding call or screenshare.** Async video works if they can't do live.

During the call:
1. Walk them through /dealer/onboarding wizard step by step
2. Help them import first listings — preferred method depends on their situation:
   - **They have a spreadsheet:** Use CSV import. Walk them through the template format.
   - **They have Philkotse/Facebook listings:** Import manually together. Do the first 2-3 with them.
   - **No existing data:** Create 2-3 listings together manually during the call
3. Verify first listing is live: go to the marketplace listing URL and confirm it shows up
4. Walk through the CRM dashboard: show them the 8 stages, explain what each means
5. Confirm they can see leads coming in (if any already)

**After the call:**
- [ ] Log call in CRM: what was covered, what's still pending
- [ ] Set Day 7 check-in reminder
- [ ] Send quick WhatsApp recap: "Nandoon na ang [X] listings ninyo — live na! Bukas or mamayang gabi, dagdag pa ng higit pa. Nandito ako pag may questions."

---

## Week 1 Targets

By end of Day 7, the dealer should have:

- [ ] 10+ listings live (help them if needed)
- [ ] CRM dashboard accessed at least once
- [ ] At least 1 inquiry received OR 1 lead created manually (to see how it works)
- [ ] At least 1 activity logged in CRM (even if you help them log it together)

**If they haven't hit these by Day 5:** Call them. Don't wait. Unactivated dealers churn.

**Daily check-in format (WhatsApp, Day 2-7):**
- "Kumusta [Name]! Ilang listings na? May tanong ba kayo?"
- Keep it short. You want them to feel supported, not watched.

---

## Week 2–4

**Days 8–14 (Daily check-in, WhatsApp):**
- Ask: "Anong CRM stage pinaka-maraming leads ngayon?"
- Ask: "May first inquiry na ba? Anong nangyari?"
- If no inquiries by Day 10: help optimize a listing together (title, price, photos)

**Day 14:**
- [ ] Send NPS survey: "Sa scale na 0-10, gaano ka likely na i-recommend ang AutoBentaPH sa ibang dealer?" — send via Google Form or Typeform
- [ ] Log NPS score in CRM
- [ ] If score 0-6: escalate to founder call within 24 hours (it already is you — call them same day)
- [ ] If score 7-8: ask "Ano ang magpapataas ng score ninyo?"
- [ ] If score 9-10: ask for testimonial and referral

**Days 15–30:**
- [ ] Help them log their first closed lead in CRM
- [ ] Run analytics check together: response time, listing views, inquiry count
- [ ] Check listing quality — are photos good? Titles descriptive? Prices competitive?
- [ ] Day 25-27: Proactive renewal conversation — confirm they're renewing, answer any concerns
- [ ] Day 30: Second NPS survey before renewal date

## Success Milestones (Update DealerSuccessPlan)

Track these in `/admin/success-plans` for each dealer. Check off as they happen.

1. ☐ Application submitted / payment confirmed
2. ☐ Account created + verification complete (Day 0)
3. ☐ First listing live (Day 1)
4. ☐ 10+ listings live (Target: Day 7)
5. ☐ First lead received
6. ☐ First lead responded within 2 hours
7. ☐ First stage move in CRM
8. ☐ First sale reported in CRM
9. ☐ Subscription renews (Day 30)

---

## Risk Signals — Trigger Proactive Outreach Immediately

These are early warning signs of churn. Act fast. Don't wait for them to complain.

| Signal | Action |
|--------|--------|
| No login in 3 days after account creation | Call immediately. "Natry na ninyo yung account?" |
| 0 leads after 7 days of listings being live | Help optimize listings — title, photos, price. Review together. |
| CRM never opened after 14 days | Schedule a 20-min CRM training call. Make it easy. |
| NPS score < 7 at Day 14 | Personal call within 24 hours. Find the real problem. |
| No activity logged in CRM after 14 days | WhatsApp: "Nakikita ko na nandoon ang leads ninyo — gusto ko ipakita kung paano i-track sa CRM. 15 minuto lang?" |

**Rule:** If you see a risk signal, call. Don't email. Don't wait.
