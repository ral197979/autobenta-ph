# First 10 Dealers Playbook
**Goal:** 10 paying dealers in 60 days.  
**Owner:** Founder  
**Time required:** 1 focused hour per day

---

## The Only Metric That Matters Right Now

> **Dealers on free trial — then converted to paid.**

Phase 1: 10 dealers on the 90-day free trial.  
Phase 2: Convert them to paying at day 75 — before the trial ends.

Not demos booked. Not leads in the pipeline. Not messages sent.  
Trial activations first, then ₱3,599/month paying dealers. Those are the two numbers.

---

## Who You're Looking For

### Profile of Dealer #2–10
- Independent used car dealer (not a franchise)
- 5–50 cars in inventory
- Currently posting on Facebook Marketplace, Philkotse, or OLX
- Has a phone number visible on their listings
- Responds to messages (their listings show recent activity)
- Based in Metro Manila first, then Cebu/Davao

### Green flags
- Posts multiple listings per week
- Replies to buyer comments on Facebook
- Has a branded Facebook page (not personal account)
- Currently paying for Philkotse ads (they already see value in online listings)
- Mentions "direct owner" or "dealer" in their bio

### Red flags
- Last post was 3+ months ago
- Only 1–2 cars listed
- No phone number visible
- Franchise dealer (different sales process, longer cycle)

---

## Where to Find Them

### 1. Facebook Marketplace (highest volume)
Search: `used cars for sale [city]` — Metro Manila, Quezon City, Pasig, Caloocan, Las Piñas  
Look for: accounts with 10+ listings, active in the last 30 days  
Action: Note their name, phone number, inventory count, what platforms they use

### 2. Philkotse.com
Go to `philkotse.com/dealers` — lists all registered dealers with contact info  
Filter by Metro Manila  
Note: if they're paying Philkotse (~₱1,500/listing), they're already spending on online ads

### 3. OLX Philippines
Search `used cars` → filter by seller type: dealer  
Dealers with "See all listings" links have real inventory

### 4. AutoMart.ph
Browse dealer listings — smaller platform, easier to stand out

### 5. Referrals from Dealer #1
The fastest path to Dealer #2.  
Ask directly: *"Sino pang dealer ang kilala mo na kailangan ng mas maraming buyer?"*

## The Daily Routine (1 Hour)

**Monday — Research Day (60 min)**
- Find and qualify 5 new prospects from the sources above
- Add them to your CRM (`/admin/founding-dealers`) with: name, phone, platform, inventory count
- Goal: always have 20+ prospects in the pipeline

**Tuesday–Thursday — Outreach Days (60 min each)**
- Send 5 first-touch messages (Messenger or Viber — see scripts below)
- Follow up with anyone who hasn't responded in 2 days
- Reply immediately to anyone who responded
- Goal: 1 demo booked per week minimum

**Friday — Follow-Up Day (60 min)**
- Review the full pipeline
- Move stale prospects forward or mark as dead
- Write 3 lines in your notes: what happened this week, what's next, what's blocking you

---

## The Message Sequence

### Touch 1 — First message (Day 1)
Send on Messenger or Viber. See the full scripts in `templates/MESSENGER_VIBER_SCRIPTS.md`.  
Keep it short. One compliment, one problem, one question.  
Do NOT pitch features. Do NOT send a link.

### Touch 2 — Follow-up (Day 3 if no reply)
Different angle. Still short. Reference something specific about their listing.

### Touch 3 — Last attempt (Day 7 if still no reply)
Honest and direct. "Last message, I promise."

### Touch 4 — Demo (if they reply yes)
Book within 48 hours. Use `/book-demo` or just pick a time directly.  
Send the confirmation message from `templates/DEMO_CONFIRMATION.md`.

### Touch 5 — After demo
Send the proposal same day. Don't wait.  
Use `templates/PROPOSAL_EMAIL.md` adapted for WhatsApp.

### Touch 6 — Follow-up after proposal (Day 2 after demo)
One message. "Anong naisip mo?"

### Touch 7 — Close or learn (Day 5 after demo)
If still no answer: ask for a yes or a no. Explain why you need to know.

## Pricing to Offer

| Offer | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **3-month free trial** | ₱0 for 90 days | Default offer — lead with this always |
| Founding Dealer (after trial) | ₱3,599/mo | Locked for life — limited slots |
| Annual prepay | ₱35,990/year | Offer when they ask for discount after trial |

**Always lead with the free trial.** Never open with the price.  
**Never discount the monthly price.** Offer annual prepay instead.

## Handling the Most Common Responses

### "Magkano?" (How much?)
> "₱3,599 lang per month — pero founding dealer rate 'to, locked for life kahit mag-increase kami ng price. Karamihan ng dealers namin nami-recover nila 'yun sa isang sale lang."

### "May libre ba?" (Is there a free trial?)
> "Oo! 3 months libre, walang credit card, full features lahat. Saka ka lang mag-decide kung gusto mong ituloy pagkatapos ng trial. Pwede ba bukas o sa makalawa tayo mag-setup?"

### "Magbabayad ba ako after 3 months?"
> "Ikaw ang magde-decide pagkatapos ng 3 months. Pero kung gusto mong ituloy — ₱3,599 lang per month. Isa lang na sale at nare-recover mo na ang isang taon. At founding dealer rate 'yan — locked for life kahit mag-increase kami ng price."

### "Facebook na lang ako, libre naman 'yan"
> "Tama, libre nga. Pero Facebook leads — buyers yung nagtatanong sa madaming seller sabay-sabay, di pa ready bumili. Sa Ryderr, buyer na naghahanap ng specific kotse ang nagcocontact — mas seryoso. Tsaka ikaw may dashboard para malaman mo kung aling kotse ang pinaka-maraming tinitingnan."

### "Busy muna ako"
> "Sige, gets ko. Kailan okay para mag-usap ng 15 minutes lang? Kahit Viber call na lang para di na kayo lumabas sa opisina."

### "Hindi ako marunong sa technology"
> "Ako na bahala doon — setup ko mismo lahat para sa inyo. 30 minutes lang, tapos running na. Kasama rin support kung may tanong kayo after."

### "Mahal naman"
> "Hindi mo kailangang mag-bayad ngayon — libre muna ng 3 months, walang credit card. Subukan mo muna, tingnan natin kung kumikita. Pagkatapos ng 3 months, ₱3,599 lang per month — isa lang na sale, nare-recover mo na."

## The Demo Script (15 minutes)

**Minutes 0–2: Their situation**
"Ilang kotse meron kayo ngayon? Saan kayo nag-aadvertise? Ilang leads kayo nakukuha per week?"

**Minutes 2–5: Show the listing page**
"Ganito lalabas 'yung kotse ninyo sa Ryderr. Buyers na naghahanap ng [make/model] ang makakakita nito."

**Minutes 5–10: Show the leads dashboard**
"Kapag may nagtype ng inquiry, dito lalabas — name, number, kung anong kotse ang gusto nila. May status din para malaman mo kung na-follow up na."

**Minutes 10–13: Show the analytics**
"Makikita mo kung ilang tao ang nagtingin sa listing mo, ilang nagpaka-favorite, ilang nag-inquire. Hindi mo ito makita sa Facebook."

**Minutes 13–15: Close**
"Dalawang tanong lang: May kotse ka bang gusto mong i-list ngayon? At pwede ba natin simulan ang libreng 3-month trial mo ngayon?"

Stop selling and start listening.

Ask each dealer:
1. "Ano 'yung pinaka-naitulong sa inyo ng Ryderr?"
2. "Ano 'yung gusto mong baguhin o idagdag?"
3. "Sino pang dealer ang kilala mo na baka kailangan nito?"

Their answers become your pitch for dealers 11–50.  
Their referrals become your cheapest leads.  
Their results become your case study.
