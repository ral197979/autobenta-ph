## Detailed Competitive Narratives

### Facebook Marketplace

Facebook Marketplace is the dominant lead-generation tool for Philippine used-car dealers. It has massive reach, it's free, and nearly every PH dealer uses it. The problem is not that Facebook is bad — it's that Facebook is an advertising channel, not an operations platform.

When a buyer messages a dealer on Facebook, the lead enters Facebook Messenger — a consumer chat application with no lead tracking, no response-time monitoring, no pipeline management, and no visibility for the dealer owner. Leads get lost in notification overflow, handled inconsistently across multiple salespeople's personal accounts, and never aggregated into data a dealer can act on. Facebook does not tell you your conversion rate, your response time, or which listings drive the most serious buyers.

AutoBentaPH captures leads from all sources — including Facebook — and routes them into a professional CRM. Facebook remains a valuable advertising channel. AutoBentaPH becomes the system that ensures those Facebook leads are actually converted.

### Philkotse

Philkotse is the largest dedicated used-car listing portal in the Philippines. It provides real exposure to in-market buyers, and many dealers see meaningful lead volume from it. The limitation is structural: Philkotse is a listing aggregator, not a dealer tool.

When a buyer inquires on Philkotse, the dealer receives an email notification. There is no CRM to capture that inquiry, no pipeline to manage the conversation, no analytics to show which listings perform, and no DMS integration to keep inventory current. Dealers who list on Philkotse still manage the resulting leads manually — in WhatsApp, in Excel, or in their heads.

AutoBentaPH complements Philkotse rather than replacing it. Dealers can continue listing on Philkotse while using AutoBentaPH as the CRM and analytics layer that Philkotse doesn't provide. Over time, AutoBentaPH's verified listings and trust scoring drive higher-intent buyers who require less qualification time.

### Carsome

Carsome is a vehicle acquisition and wholesale platform, not a dealer SaaS product. Its business model involves buying used cars from consumers and dealers and reselling them through its own channels. Dealers who work with Carsome are selling inventory to Carsome — they are not using Carsome as a tool to sell to their own buyers.

AutoBentaPH and Carsome are not in the same category. A dealer who sells units to Carsome wholesale is exiting the retail margin in exchange for convenience. A dealer who uses AutoBentaPH is building a retail operation that captures the full margin. The two can coexist — a dealer might use Carsome for aged inventory they want to liquidate while using AutoBentaPH to grow their retail pipeline.

### CDK Global

CDK Global is the market-leading DMS for North American franchise dealerships. It is feature-rich, highly configurable, and designed for dealers who process hundreds of transactions per month with dedicated finance, service, and parts departments. In the Philippine context, CDK represents ₱200,000–₱540,000 per year in licensing fees, plus implementation costs that typically require an IT team and a dedicated project manager.

For the 95%+ of PH used-car dealers who are independent operators with 10–200 units, CDK is architecturally designed for a different business. The onboarding timeline alone (typically 3–6 months) eliminates it as a viable option for dealers looking to solve operational problems now. AutoBentaPH delivers the CRM, analytics, and DMS sync capabilities that matter for PH dealer operations at a fraction of the cost and with a 2-hour setup timeline.

### Reynolds & Reynolds

Reynolds & Reynolds is the second-largest DMS provider in North America, with a similarly enterprise-grade product profile. Pricing starts at approximately ₱500,000 per year for basic configurations and scales significantly from there. There is no dedicated PH support infrastructure, no PH-market localization (OR/CR verification, LTO database integration, GCash compatibility), and implementation requires months of IT project management.

Like CDK, Reynolds is designed for large franchise dealer groups in Western markets. The product is not wrong — it's simply the wrong product for the PH independent dealer market. AutoBentaPH was built from the ground up for the market Reynolds is not designed to serve.

### DealerSocket

DealerSocket serves the US and Australian dealer markets with a CRM and DMS platform that has strong reputation in those geographies. It has no PH market localization, no LTO or OR/CR integration capabilities, no GCash or local payment awareness, and no PH support team. Pricing is in USD and typically not disclosed publicly, but quotes to PH dealers who have inquired have ranged from ₱25,000–₱60,000/month.

The setup complexity is comparable to CDK and Reynolds — weeks of implementation, IT requirements, and a learning curve designed for Western dealer operations workflows. For PH dealers, DealerSocket represents cost and complexity that delivers no PH-specific value.

### Excel / Manual Systems

Excel and manual systems (WhatsApp threads, physical logs, spreadsheets, personal notebooks) represent the most common "competitor" in the PH dealer market today. They are free, familiar, and infinitely flexible — which is why dealers use them.

The limitations are not about capability — Excel can hold data. The limitations are about real-time operations: Excel doesn't alert you when a lead arrives at 9 PM. It doesn't track response time. It doesn't prevent two salespeople from double-working the same lead. It doesn't update a Philkotse listing automatically when a car sells. And it provides no aggregate analytics to show the owner where the business is actually performing.

The transition from Excel to AutoBentaPH is not a rip-and-replace. AutoBentaPH has a CSV import specifically because most dealers store their inventory in a spreadsheet. The migration takes less than a day.
