# Payment Collection Playbook
## AutoBentaPH — Dealer Billing Operations

**Purpose:** Collect ₱3,599/month reliably from every active dealer. Cover every scenario from invoice generation to overdue escalation.

---

## Part 1: Invoice Timing and Format

### When to generate the invoice
- **First invoice:** Generate within 2 hours of receiving the signed Dealer Agreement
- **Recurring invoices:** Generate on the **25th of each month** (for the following month's service)
- Never generate an invoice before the agreement is signed

### Invoice file naming
Format: `INV-[DealerName]-[YYYY-MM].pdf`
Example: `INV-ManilaAutoSales-2026-06.pdf`

### Required invoice fields

```

[YOUR REGISTERED BUSINESS ADDRESS]
TIN: [YOUR TIN] (required for BIR compliance)
Email: [YOUR EMAIL]

Date Issued: [Date]
Due Date: [Date + 5 days for first invoice; 1st of month for recurring]

[Dealer Business Name]

[Dealer TIN if available]

## Part 4: GCash Collection Flow

### Step-by-step process

1. **Share your GCash number** in invoice and follow-up messages
   - Format: 09XX-XXX-XXXX
   - Always include the registered name so they can verify before sending

2. **When payment screenshot arrives:**
   - Check the sender name matches the dealer's name or business
   - Verify the amount (₱3,599.00)
   - Check the date and transaction reference number
   - Screenshot or save the confirmation yourself

3. **Immediate confirmation message (send within 5 minutes):**
   ```
   Natanggap! Salamat [First name] — confirmed ang payment ng ₱3,599.
   
   Ire-issue ko ang Official Receipt sa email ninyo within 3 business days. I-a-activate na rin ang account ninyo ngayon.
   
   Handa na kayong mag-onboard — sino ang best time para sa Zoom call? 😊
   ```

4. **Log in spreadsheet:** Date, Dealer Name, Amount, GCash ref number, Status = Received

5. **Issue OR within 3 business days**

### If GCash name doesn't match
```
[First name], natanggap ang payment — salamat! Isang clarification lang: ang GCash reference na natanggap ko ay nakapangalan sa [name on GCash]. Tama ba ito? Para ma-match ko ang records namin nang tama.
```

## Part 5: BDO/BPI Bank Transfer Flow

### What to include in the invoice
```
Bank Transfer Details:
Bank: [BDO or BPI]
Account Name: [YOUR NAME or BUSINESS NAME]
Account Number: XXXX-XXXX-XXXX
Branch: [Branch Name, City]
```

### Confirming receipt
1. Wait for dealer to send bank transfer screenshot/confirmation slip
2. Verify: account number matches, amount is ₱3,599, date is recent
3. Cross-check against your online banking if possible (same business day)
4. Send confirmation WhatsApp (same as GCash flow above)

### If you don't receive confirmation
- Check your bank account on Day 3 and Day 5 after invoice due date
- If funds appear without confirmation, message the dealer: "May natanggap kaming transfer na ₱3,599 — kayo ba ito? Gusto ko lang i-confirm para ma-issue ang receipt."

## Part 7: Refund Policy Script

AutoBentaPH's policy: No refunds after account activation and onboarding call has occurred. Pro-rata refund if account is cancelled before onboarding.

**If dealer asks for a refund (first 30 days):**
```
[First name], naiintindihan ko ang concern. Hayaan ninyong tiyakin ko ang sitwasyon.

Kung hindi pa kayo naka-onboard at hindi pa namin nai-activate ang lahat ng features ninyo, pwede nating pag-usapan ang partial refund.

Pero bago tayo pumunta doon — pwede ba nating alamin kung ano ang specific na problema? Karamihan ng concerns ay naaayos namin sa isang tawag, at gusto ko talagang matiyak na nakukuha ninyo ang value ng subscription bago tayo mag-discuss ng refund.

Pwede ba tayong mag-usap ngayon o bukas?
```

**If the problem is unresolvable:**
```
Naiintindihan ko. Para sa refund request: pag-aaralan namin ito at magbibigay kami ng sagot sa loob ng 3 business days. Kailangan namin ng:
1. Invoice number
2. Date of payment
3. Reason for refund request

I-send ito sa [YOUR EMAIL] at aasikasuhin namin agad.
```

## Part 8: Payment Dispute Handling

**If dealer claims they paid but you have no record:**
```
[First name], naiintindihan ko ang frustration. Para matugunan natin ito agad:

1. Pwede ba ninyong i-send ulit ang payment screenshot o reference number?
2. I-check din natin ang bank/GCash records ninyo — minsan nagta-take ng 1-2 business days ang bank reconciliation

I-aayos natin ito sa araw ding ito pagkita ng proof of payment.
```

Never assume bad faith in the first message. Always request documentation first.

## Part 9: Renewal Collection Sequence

Renewals run on a monthly cycle. Invoice goes out on the **25th**, due on the **1st**.

### 60 days before renewal (Month 4 milestone)
Send a success review — not a billing message. Make them feel good before the invoice arrives.

### 30 days before renewal
**WhatsApp (1st of prior month):**
```
[First name], kumusta? Isang buwan na lang bago ang inyong renewal.

Gusto ko munang sabihin — ang [specific metric: e.g., "12 leads na nakuha ninyo ngayong buwan"] ay kahanga-hanga. Nagtatrabaho ang sistema.

Ipapadala ko ang renewal invoice sa [25th] — same rate, ₱3,599, same terms. No surprises.

Kung may gustong baguhin o gustong pag-usapan bago ang renewal, sabi lang.
```

### Day 25 (Invoice sent)
Use same Day 0 flow above but update the messaging:

**WhatsApp:**
```
[First name], attached sa email ang renewal invoice para sa [next month]. ₱3,599 lang — same Founding Dealer rate ninyo.

Pag nakabayad na, i-screenshot at i-send sa akin. Official Receipt within 3 days, as always.

Salamat sa inyong tiwala — excited pa rin akong makita ang growth ng inyong dealership ngayong buwan!
```

### Day 27 (if not yet paid)
```
[First name], quick reminder lang — renewal invoice para sa [month] ay due na sa [date].

Pag nakabayad, i-screenshot at i-WhatsApp sa akin. 🙏
