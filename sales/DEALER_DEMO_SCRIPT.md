# AutoBentaPH — 30-Minute Live Demo Script

**Purpose:** Walk a prospective dealer through the platform live, connect features to their specific pain points, and move toward a close.

**Format:** Video call (Zoom/Google Meet) or in-person. Works for owner/GM audiences. Adapt tone based on whether the prospect is technical or operations-focused.

---

## Pre-Demo Checklist

### 24 Hours Before

- [ ] Confirm the meeting and send calendar invite with Zoom link
- [ ] Research the dealer: Google them, check their Facebook page, note their inventory size and vehicle types (SUV-heavy? Sedans? Commercial?)
- [ ] Check if they're on Philkotse or OLX — screenshot any listings you find
- [ ] Note if they use CDK, Reynolds, or DealerSocket (check their website or ask in confirmation email)
- [ ] Pull their approximate lead volume if known from pre-call qualification

### 30 Minutes Before

- [ ] Open AutoBentaPH demo environment (use demo account, not production)
- [ ] Stage the CRM with 8–10 realistic leads at various pipeline stages
- [ ] Have a sample verified listing ready (SUV preferred — highest volume in Metro Manila)
- [ ] Open the analytics dashboard — make sure charts show meaningful data
- [ ] Have the pricing page ready in a separate tab
- [ ] Close all unrelated tabs and notifications on your screen
- [ ] Test screen share before the call
- [ ] Have the ROI calculator spreadsheet ready (or open the in-platform version)

### Materials to Have Ready

- [ ] This demo script (on a second screen or printed)
- [ ] Objection handling guide (open, not visible to prospect)
- [ ] Pricing slide or plan comparison page
- [ ] A sample CSV inventory file (to show import capability)
- [ ] Contact card / follow-up email template

## Minute-by-Minute Demo Guide

---

### 0:00 – 2:00 | Welcome, Agenda, Qualifying Question

**Goal:** Set expectations, establish rapport, learn one key fact about their business before the demo starts.

**Script:**

"[Name], thanks for making time. Before I get into the demo, I want to make sure this is the most useful 30 minutes possible for you.

I'll walk you through the platform in three areas: how leads come in and get managed, the analytics and reporting you'd see day-to-day, and how pricing works. At the end, we'll have a few minutes to talk about next steps — no hard close, just making sure you have what you need to make a good decision.

Before I share my screen — quick question: right now, when a buyer reaches out about one of your cars, what does that process look like? Where does the message go, and who handles it?"

**Listen for:**
- "Facebook Messenger" — note it, reference it throughout the demo
- "We have a salesperson who handles it" — ask how many
- "It depends / we all pitch in" — flag as an organization problem to address later
- "We use [X software]" — note for competitive positioning

**Key question:** "And what happens if that message comes in at 9 PM on a Saturday?"

*Their answer will define the entire demo narrative. If they say "we see it the next morning," you have your hook.*

---

### 2:00 – 7:00 | Problem Setup — Ask About Their Current Process

**Goal:** Get the prospect to articulate their pain in their own words. Don't describe their problem for them — guide them to describe it themselves.

**Script:**

"Let me ask you a few more questions before I show you anything — I want to make sure what I show is relevant to you specifically.

Walk me through a typical month. Roughly how many inquiries do you get across all your channels — Facebook, Philkotse, walk-ins, wherever?"

*[Wait for answer. Write it down visibly — it signals you're tracking their numbers.]*

"And out of those, how many do you think actually turn into a closed sale?"

*[Wait. If they say "I don't know," that's a pain point itself.]*

"So if I understand correctly — [X inquiries] per month, [Y] turning into sales — that's roughly [Z%] conversion. Does that feel about right?"

*[Calculate on the spot. Even if rough, this makes the ROI conversation real.]*

"One more: how do you track which of your salespeople is handling which leads right now?"

*[If they say "we don't really" or "WhatsApp" or "Facebook DMs" — pause here.]*

"That's actually really common. Most dealers we talk to have the same setup — the leads come in through multiple channels, someone handles it informally, and there's no single place to see where every deal stands at any moment. That's exactly the problem AutoBentaPH solves. Let me show you."

*[Start screen share now.]*

### 7:00 – 12:00 | Homepage + Listings Tour

**Goal:** Show the buyer-facing experience so the dealer understands what their buyers see, then connect it to trust and conversion.

**Script:**

"I'm going to show you this from two angles — first what your buyers see, then what you see on the backend.

This is the AutoBentaPH marketplace. [Navigate to homepage.] When a buyer lands here, they're seeing a curated feed of verified used-car listings. Not Facebook Marketplace where the car is next to a used ref and a second-hand iPhone. This is specifically buyers who came here looking for a used car.

[Navigate to a featured listing.] Let me show you one of our sample listings. Notice these three things:

**One — the OR/CR verified badge.** [Point to badge.] This tells the buyer that the LTO records check out. Buyers in the PH market are trained to be skeptical about documentation. When they see this, they stop asking 'is this car legitimate?' and start asking 'what's the price?' — and that's a much better conversation for you.

**Two — the V8Atlas trust score.** [Point to score.] This is pulled from VIN history and ownership chain data. Buyers understand what it means — higher score, cleaner history.

**Three — the 5-point inspection badge.** [If present.] This means the dealer has completed a physical inspection checklist covering engine, transmission, exterior, interior, and electrical. It tells the buyer the dealer stands behind what they're selling.

Now — [navigate to listings page and find a 'featured' listing] — this is a featured listing. It appears at the top of search results and on the homepage rotation. When you pay for a featured slot, you're not just getting more views. You're getting views from buyers who are already further along in the decision process — they're not browsing, they're comparing."

**Key questions to ask during this section:**
- "Does your current listing on [Philkotse/Facebook] have anything equivalent to the verified badge? How do buyers verify the OR/CR today?"
- "When you get a walk-in who saw your car on Facebook — how much time do you spend in the first conversation just establishing that the car is legitimate?"

### 12:00 – 18:00 | Lead CRM Demo — 8 Stages, Activity Log

**Goal:** This is the core value demonstration. Make it feel like their specific problem is being solved.

**Script:**

"Now let me show you what happens when a buyer clicks 'Inquire' on your listing.

[Navigate to CRM — Kanban view.] This is your lead pipeline. Every inquiry that comes in — from AutoBentaPH, from Facebook if you set up the integration, from direct links you share — lands here as a new card.

[Point to 'New' column.] When this comes in, two things happen automatically: you get a notification on your phone, and a response-time clock starts ticking. The clock is visible to you and to your salesperson — so everyone knows if a lead is sitting too long without a response.

[Drag a card from New to Contacted.] When your salesperson makes first contact, they drag it here. That's it — one move. No data entry, no form to fill out. The system logs who moved it and when.

[Walk through the 8 stages:]

- **New** → lead just arrived
- **Contacted** → first outreach made
- **Qualified** → you've confirmed budget and timeline
- **Test Drive Scheduled** → appointment set
- **Negotiating** → active price discussion
- **Closed Won** → deal done, car sold, inventory auto-updated
- **Closed Lost** → didn't close — reason captured for your analytics
- **On Hold** → buyer needs more time, automatic follow-up timer set

[Click into a specific lead card.] This is the activity timeline. Every interaction is logged here — when the inquiry came in, who replied, what was said, what listings they looked at, when the test drive was scheduled. If your salesperson is out sick and someone else needs to pick up the lead, they see everything in 30 seconds. No more 'I don't know where this lead is at.'"

**Tactical move — personalize it:**

"You mentioned earlier that [specific pain point they mentioned, e.g., 'you don't know how many leads get dropped']. Let me show you exactly how that gets solved. [Point to response-time metric in card.] This timestamp shows the lead came in at [time] and the first response was at [time]. That gap is your response time for this lead. In your analytics dashboard, you'll see your average response time for the whole month — and you'll see where leads are going cold."

**Key questions:**
- "How many salespeople do you have handling leads right now? [Show salesperson assignment dropdown.] Each lead can be assigned to a specific person — so you always know who owns what."
- "Have you ever had a situation where two salespeople were both following up on the same buyer? [Show activity log.] This prevents that."

### 18:00 – 22:00 | Analytics Dashboard + Dealer Scorecard

**Goal:** Show that the platform gives the owner/GM visibility they've never had before.

**Script:**

"Now let me show you what you see as the owner or GM — not the salesperson view, the management view.

[Navigate to analytics dashboard.] This is your control panel. These are the numbers I want to show you:

**Lead volume:** [Point to chart.] This shows how many leads came in this month vs. last month, broken down by source. You can see which channel is working — AutoBentaPH marketplace, featured listings, Facebook, direct links. If you're spending money on Facebook ads, you can see if they're actually generating leads or just impressions.

**Conversion funnel:** [Point to funnel chart.] This shows you where you're losing deals. If you're losing 40% of leads between 'Contacted' and 'Qualified,' that's a training problem — your salespeople are contacting but not qualifying. If you're losing 30% between 'Test Drive' and 'Closed,' that's a negotiation problem. Without this data, you have no idea where the leak is.

**Response time:** [Point to metric.] This is your average first-response time vs. the platform average. Platform data shows dealers who respond within 1 hour close at 3x the rate of dealers who respond the next day. If your number is high, you know exactly where to focus.

[Navigate to Dealer Scorecard.] This is your monthly scorecard. Dealers are graded A through D based on response time, conversion rate, listing quality, and buyer feedback.

A-rank dealers get search boost, homepage rotation, and priority placement — that's not a paid feature, it's earned. It creates a flywheel: better performance → better ranking → more visibility → more leads → better performance."

**Key questions:**
- "Right now, do you have any visibility into your conversion rate — do you know what percentage of inquiries turn into sales?"
- "If I told you that you had 10 leads stuck in 'Negotiating' for more than 7 days, would that be useful to know?"

### 22:00 – 25:00 | V8Atlas Sync

**Goal:** If they use CDK or Reynolds, this section is critical. If they don't use a DMS, make it relevant anyway.

**Qualifying gate:** "Earlier I asked about your DMS — are you currently using CDK, Reynolds, or any other dealer management system?"

**If YES (CDK/Reynolds/DealerSocket):**

"Then this section is especially relevant for you. [Navigate to V8Atlas sync settings.] AutoBentaPH runs on top of V8Atlas, which has native integrations with CDK, Reynolds, and DealerSocket. What that means practically: when you mark a unit as sold in your DMS, it's automatically removed from your AutoBentaPH listings. When you add a new unit to your DMS, it automatically appears on AutoBentaPH. When a lead comes in on AutoBentaPH, the data can route back into your DMS.

You stop managing inventory in two places. You stop the 'car is already sold but still listed' problem. And your salespeople work in whatever system they're already comfortable with — the sync handles the rest.

Setup requires your API credentials from your DMS provider. It's one email to your CDK/Reynolds rep. We handle the rest."

**If NO (no DMS):**

"Even without a DMS, the inventory management inside AutoBentaPH replaces the need for one at your scale. You enter a unit once — photos, specs, price, OR/CR details — and it's live on the marketplace, tracked in your CRM, and linked to your analytics. When it sells, you archive it and the listing comes down automatically. For dealers who are managing inventory in spreadsheets or manually, this alone is worth the subscription."

**Key question:** "How long does it currently take you to add a new unit to all the places you list? And when a car sells, how many places do you have to manually update?"

## Common Demo Questions + Answers

**"Can I import my existing inventory?"**
Yes — CSV import is included on all plans. We provide a template. Most dealers with 20–50 units complete the import in under an hour.

**"Does it work on mobile?"**
Yes — AutoBentaPH has a mobile-responsive web interface. The CRM Kanban, lead alerts, and listings management all work on your phone. A dedicated app is on the roadmap.

**"What if I'm already using a salesperson's personal Facebook to post listings?"**
We can generate a shareable link for any AutoBentaPH listing that posts to Facebook. Your salespeople can still share from their personal pages — the link just routes the inquiry into the CRM instead of to their personal Messenger.

**"Can buyers pay through the platform?"**
AutoBentaPH facilitates introductions and lead management. The actual transaction — payment, contract, vehicle transfer — happens outside the platform between dealer and buyer, the same as today. GCash integration for reservations is on the roadmap.

**"What happens to my data if I cancel?"**
You can export all lead history and inventory data as a CSV at any time. Your data is yours. There's no lock-in.

**"How does the OR/CR verification actually work?"**
You submit the OR/CR details when creating a listing. AutoBentaPH cross-references with LTO records. Verification typically completes within 24 hours. Verified listings carry the green badge.

**"Do you have references — other dealers I can talk to?"**
Yes — we can connect you with existing dealers on the platform. These are real operators, not paid endorsers. Ask for this and we'll facilitate an intro.

**"What support do I get if something breaks?"**
Verified Dealer: best-effort response via email/ticket. Dealer Pro: 8-hour response SLA. Founding Dealer: 4-hour response SLA with a dedicated support contact.
