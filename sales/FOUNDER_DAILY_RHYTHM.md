# Founder Daily Rhythm — Dealer Acquisition Sprint
*Simple, repeatable. Total time: ~90 minutes/day. Sprint goal: Dealer #1 signed within 14 days.*

---

## Morning Block (30 min) — 9:00–9:30am

**9:00 — Pipeline review (10 min)**
Open `/admin/founding-dealers`
Answer: Which prospect is closest to closing today?
Answer: Who has a follow-up due today?
Answer: Any demo scheduled today?

**9:10 — Priority calls list (5 min)**
Write 3 names to contact today (in priority order):
1. ___
2. ___
3. ___

Prioritize: Hot tier first → referrals → prospects with open proposals → warm follow-ups

**9:15 — Pre-call prep (5 min)**
For the first call: open their entry in `DEMO_PREP_WORKSHEET.md`
Know before you dial: their pain, their current system, what mattered in the last interaction
Do not call cold — 5 minutes of prep changes the outcome.

**9:20 — Log yesterday in CRM (10 min)**
For every contact from yesterday: stage updated? Next follow-up date set?
Rule: no prospect leaves the CRM without a next action date
If a prospect is stuck at the same stage for 3+ days: change the approach, don't just wait

---

## Midday Block (45 min) — Flexible timing, based on your schedule

**Calls and demos**
Use `DEMO_PREP_WORKSHEET.md` before each demo
Fill in `DEMO_SCORECARD.md` within 15 minutes of hanging up — memory fades fast

**WhatsApp follow-ups**
Use templates from `sales/templates/`
Personalize at least one line per message — don't send identical messages to multiple prospects
Priority order: open proposals → post-demo follow-ups → first-contact outreach

**Objection logging**
When you hear a new objection or a variant: update `OBJECTION_TRACKER.md` immediately
If you found a response that worked: write it down before the next call

**Referral check-in**
For Cruz Vehicles, Soriano Motor Works, Southside Auto Group: have you confirmed the referrer's name?
Referrals close faster when you can say "Pinarefer ka ni [name]" — don't skip this step.

---

## Evening Block (15 min) — Before 6pm

**Log all today's contacts in `/admin/founding-dealers`**
Stage updated for everyone contacted today?
Next follow-up date set for every active prospect?

**Set tomorrow's follow-ups (minimum 3)**
Which 3 prospects need contact tomorrow?
Write their names in your morning prep slot now — don't decide fresh tomorrow.

**Update `DEALER_1_PLAYBOOK.md`**
For any prospect with news today: update Stage, Last Contact, Next Action, Known Objections

**Score any demos from today**
Fill in `DEMO_SCORECARD.md` — don't leave it until tomorrow

**One question to answer before closing:**
Am I closer to Dealer #1 than I was yesterday?
If no: what's one thing I'll do differently tomorrow?

---

## Weekly Rhythm (Friday, 20 min)

**Review `OBJECTION_TRACKER.md`**
Any new patterns this week? Which objections are you hearing most?
Update Best Response column if you found something that worked.

**Review `MESSAGE_TESTING_LOG.md`**
Kill WhatsApp variants with <20% response rate
Double down on variants performing above average

**Review `WAR_ROOM_DAILY_TRACKER.md`**
On pace for the week? What needs to accelerate?
Are the right prospects in the right stages?

**Check `/admin/growth`**
Are the weekly goals turning green?
What's the single biggest bottleneck right now?

---

## Sprint Milestone Checkpoints

**Day 3 (June 3):** All 3 referrals contacted (Cruz, Soriano, Southside). At least 1 demo booked.
**Day 5 (June 5):** At least 2 demos completed. At least 1 proposal sent.
**Day 7 (June 7):** At least 1 prospect at Proposal or Negotiation stage.
**Day 10 (June 10):** Verbal yes from at least 1 prospect. Pilot offered if needed.
**Day 14 (June 14):** Dealer #1 signed, account created, onboarding started.

If Day 7 passes with no prospect at Proposal stage: re-assess the outreach approach, not just the follow-up cadence.

---

## Green Flags (you're on track)
- Referrals respond within 24 hours of first WhatsApp
- Demo-to-proposal conversion rate ≥ 50%
- At least 1 prospect scores 45+ on the DEMO_SCORECARD
- You're logging objections — you're learning from each call

## Red Flags (intervene immediately)
- 3 days without a new prospect contacted
- Proposal sent but no follow-up within 48 hours
- Prospects stuck at "Contacted" for more than 4 days
- You're avoiding the CDK/Reynolds prospects because the pitch feels harder
