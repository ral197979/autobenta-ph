## Program Overview and Rationale

### What It Is

The AutoBentaPH Founding Dealer Program is a limited cohort of 5 dealers who join the platform at launch in exchange for an extraordinary pricing commitment, direct product influence, and accelerated onboarding. The program is a one-time offer — once the 5 spots are filled, the program permanently closes.

**Core offer:** Dealer Pro at ₱3,599/month, locked forever (40% off the standard ₱5,999 rate), in exchange for product engagement, honest feedback, and early commitment.

### Why We're Doing This

**Reason 1: Product feedback at the earliest stage matters most.**
The first 5 paying dealers will reveal what the product gets right and what it misses in ways no amount of internal testing can match. Founding Dealers interact with the platform every day in real business conditions. Their experience shapes every major product decision in the first 6–12 months.

**Reason 2: Reference customers and social proof.**
A new platform asking for ₱5,999/month from a skeptical Philippine dealer needs proof that it works. Five real dealers using it, getting results, and willing to say so publicly are worth more than any marketing spend.

**Reason 3: Revenue certainty in the early phase.**
Even at the discounted rate, five Founding Dealers at ₱3,599/month generates ₱17,995/month in committed recurring revenue — and more importantly, it establishes a financial relationship that motivates our continued investment in the platform.

**Reason 4: Market learning.**
Founding Dealers will be operating across different regions (Metro Manila, Cebu, Davao priority) and different inventory profiles (sedans, SUVs, commercial vehicles). The diversity of their feedback gives us a product that works for the full spectrum of PH dealers, not just one type.

### Why It Works as a Sales Offer

The Founding Dealer pitch is differentiated from a discount because it's framed as a partnership, not a promotion. Dealers who join aren't "getting a cheap deal" — they're being selected as partners who will help build the platform. That framing attracts the kind of operator we actually want: engaged, serious, and willing to give feedback.

The 5-spot limitation is real, not manufactured urgency. When spots are gone, they're gone.

## Ideal Founding Dealer Profile

### Hard Criteria (All Must Be Met)

- **Inventory size:** 20–200 active units in inventory at any given time
- **Sales staff:** At least one dedicated salesperson (not just the owner managing everything alone)
- **Current digital presence:** Active on Facebook Marketplace, Philkotse, OLX, or some combination — they already have a digital lead flow we can improve
- **Decision-maker access:** Owner or GM is available and willing to be involved (product feedback requires someone with authority to evaluate the platform holistically)
- **Vehicle focus:** Primarily selling used passenger cars and light commercial vehicles (no parts-only dealers, no heavy equipment dealers)

### Preferred Criteria (Not Required, But Positive Signals)

- Located in Metro Manila, Cebu, or Davao (highest concentration of online buyers; easiest for us to support directly)
- Has had at least one prior experience with a CRM or lead management tool (understands what good looks like, can give informed feedback)
- Owner/GM is growth-oriented — asking questions like "how do I sell more?" not just "how do I manage what I have?"
- Currently experiencing pain around lead loss, slow response times, or inventory tracking — not content with the status quo
- Willing to be named as a reference customer (logo, quote, case study) after 90 days if results are positive
- Uses CDK, Reynolds, or DealerSocket (V8Atlas sync opportunity makes the value prop clearer and the integration story more compelling)

### Disqualifying Signals

- Owner who is resistant to any technology adoption (will not engage, will not train team, will expect results without input)
- Single-person operations where the owner is also the only salesperson — insufficient scale to reveal CRM value
- Dealer whose primary concern is "what's the cheapest option" rather than "what gets me results"
- Dealer in active financial distress — pricing pressure will make them difficult partners even at the discounted rate
- Dealer with fewer than 5 active units — the platform's value compounds with volume; too-small operations will see weak results and attribute it to the platform

## Benefits Package — Full Detail

### 1. Permanent Price Lock at ₱3,599/Month

**What it means:** The standard Dealer Pro price is ₱5,999/month. Founding Dealers pay ₱3,599/month. This rate is locked in their written contract — it cannot increase regardless of future price changes to the platform. If standard Dealer Pro goes to ₱7,999/month in two years, Founding Dealers still pay ₱3,599.

**Why it matters to the dealer:** This isn't just a discount today. It's guaranteed protection against future price increases. A dealer who joins now and operates for 3 years saves ₱2,400/month × 36 months = ₱86,400 compared to joining at standard pricing.

**How to explain in sales conversation:** "This price is locked in your contract. Not 'locked for a year' — locked permanently. As we grow and add features, the standard price will increase. Your price won't."

### 2. Founding Dealer Badge

**What it means:** A visual badge displayed on the dealer's AutoBentaPH profile and on all their listings, identifying them as one of the original AutoBentaPH Founding Dealers.

**Why it matters to the dealer:** In the Philippine used-car market, trust is a key purchase driver. Being identified as a founding partner of a platform signals early adoption, legitimacy, and commitment to professional standards. As AutoBentaPH grows, the Founding Dealer badge becomes a mark of distinction — buyers know this dealer was there from the beginning.

**How to explain in sales conversation:** "As the platform grows and more buyers use AutoBentaPH to find their next car, your Founding Dealer badge tells them you've been here since day one. That's a trust signal you can't buy later — you can only earn it now."

### 3. Direct Product Influence

**What it means:** Monthly 30-minute call with AutoBentaPH's product team (product manager or founder level, not customer support). Founding Dealers' feature requests go to the top of the backlog. They see feature proposals before public release and provide feedback that shapes the final product.

**Why it matters to the dealer:** The platform will be built around how they actually work. Features that annoy them get fixed first. Features they need get prioritized. They're not adapting to a foreign product — they're co-creating one that fits their operation.

**How to explain in sales conversation:** "You get a direct line to the people building the product. When you tell us something isn't working or something's missing, it goes directly onto the roadmap. You're not filing a support ticket — you're talking to the people who can change it."

### 4. Priority Support — 4-Hour Response SLA

**What it means:** Founding Dealers receive a guaranteed 4-hour response to any support request, including weekends. They have a dedicated support contact (not a general ticketing queue).

**Standard comparison:** Dealer Pro is 8 hours; Verified Dealer is best-effort.

**Why it matters to the dealer:** If something breaks — a listing isn't publishing, a lead notification isn't firing — a dealer can't wait 24 hours for a fix. 4-hour SLA means the platform is operationally dependable.

**How to explain in sales conversation:** "You get a specific contact you can message directly. When something needs attention, you don't wait in a queue — you reach out and you hear back within 4 hours, any day of the week."

### 5. Beta Feature Access

**What it means:** Founding Dealers get access to all new features before they release to the general platform. This includes features still in testing, new DMS integrations, AI tools, mobile app beta, and any features added based on Founding Dealer feedback.

**Why it matters to the dealer:** They stay ahead of competitors who join later. When a new feature launches that drives more buyer inquiries, Founding Dealers have had it for weeks already.

**How to explain in sales conversation:** "Every new feature we build — AI tools, new integrations, marketplace improvements — you get them first. When everyone else gets access, you've already been using it for a month."

### 6. Dedicated Onboarding Support

**What it means:** A dedicated onboarding session (not generic documentation) including: inventory CSV import help, V8Atlas sync setup (if applicable), team walkthrough of the CRM, listing optimization review, and a 30-day check-in call.

**Why it matters to the dealer:** The difference between a dealer who gets ROI and one who doesn't is almost always onboarding quality. Founding Dealers don't just get access — they get set up properly.

## Onboarding Timeline

### Day 1 — Account Setup and Inventory Migration

**Actions:**
- Account created and credentials delivered
- Dealer invited to onboarding call (schedule within 48 hours of signup)
- CSV inventory template sent — dealer completes and returns
- V8Atlas sync credentials collected (if applicable)
- Billing confirmed

**Success criteria:** Account active, at least 5 listings live or in draft before Day 1 ends

### Day 2–3 — Onboarding Call

**What happens on the call (60 minutes):**
- Walk through the CRM with the owner and any salespeople who will use it
- Import inventory CSV together and review first listings
- Set up first verified listing (OR/CR submission)
- Configure lead notifications (phone alerts, email)
- Show the analytics dashboard and explain the scorecard
- Answer all team questions live

**Success criteria:** At least one salesperson can independently add a listing, move a lead through the pipeline, and find their response-time metric without help

### Week 1 — First Leads and First Wins

**Goals:**
- First inquiry received and handled through the CRM
- First listing receiving views
- Owner/GM checks the dashboard at least once

**Our job:**
- Check in with dealer on Day 5 via WhatsApp or call
- Resolve any friction points immediately (response: within 4 hours)
- Review listing quality and suggest improvements if needed

### Month 1 — First Product Feedback Call

**What happens:**
- 30-minute call with AutoBentaPH product team
- Review: what's working, what's frustrating, what's missing
- Dealer shares their specific feature priorities
- We share our roadmap and confirm what's being built next

**Success criteria:** At least one actionable piece of product feedback captured and added to backlog

### Month 1 — Scorecard Review

- Share the dealer's first monthly scorecard with them
- Review response time, conversion metrics, lead volume
- Set goals for Month 2
- Ask whether the ROI is materializing — if not, diagnose and fix

## Talking Points for Sales Conversations

**Why only 5 spots?**
"We're deliberately keeping it small. We want genuine product partners, not a large early cohort that we can't support properly. Five dealers means we can give each one direct attention, actually implement their feedback, and make sure the first cohort succeeds before we open wider."

**Why ₱3,599 specifically?**
"It's 40% off the standard Dealer Pro rate of ₱5,999. The discount reflects the partnership trade — you're giving us feedback, product input, and the credibility of being an early adopter. We're giving you a permanently lower rate and priority treatment. It's a fair exchange."

**Is the founding price really permanent?**
"Yes — and that's in the written contract. Not 'for the first year' or 'until we change it.' Permanently locked. If you stay with the platform, you keep ₱3,599/month forever, regardless of what happens to standard pricing."

**What exactly is 'product influence'?**
"Every month we have a 30-minute call with you specifically — not a group webinar, not a support call. We share what we're building, you tell us what you need, and your feedback goes directly to the backlog. Several features on our current roadmap exist because founding dealers told us they mattered."

**What if I'm not ready to commit right now?**
"Totally fair. The honest answer is: I don't know when the last founding spot fills. We have active conversations with qualified dealers right now. If it fills before you're ready, the next available option is standard Dealer Pro at ₱5,999 — which is still a great platform. The founding deal just isn't available at that point. I'd rather you take the time you need than feel pressured. What would help you feel confident enough to move forward?"

## Escalation Path If Founding Dealers Churn

### Why Founding Dealer Churn Is a Critical Signal

A Founding Dealer who cancels is not a normal churn event. They have the highest support priority, the most direct feedback channel, and the lowest price point. If they leave, the reason is almost certainly a product failure or a serious expectation mismatch — and both are correctable if caught early.

### Warning Signs (Act Before Cancellation)

- Response time on product calls drops (stops showing up to monthly calls)
- CRM activity goes quiet (no lead movement for 7+ days — detectable via platform data)
- Support tickets increase in frequency and frustration level
- Monthly scorecard grade declining for two consecutive months

### When a Founding Dealer Signals They Might Leave

**Step 1: Escalate immediately.** Any indication of cancellation intent goes directly to the founder/CEO level. Do not route to standard support.

**Step 2: Schedule an emergency call within 24 hours.** Not a ticket — a real conversation. Goal: understand the specific failure, not defend the platform.

**Step 3: Diagnose honestly.** Is this a product problem? (Fix it.) Is this an adoption problem? (Re-onboard.) Is this an ROI problem? (Diagnose the conversion metrics.) Is this a business problem on their side? (Understand before acting.)

**Step 4: Recovery offer.** If the platform failed them, offer a one-month credit as acknowledgment — not as a bribe to stay. The goal is to solve the real problem.

**Step 5: If they still leave:** Exit interview required. Document every reason, every failure point, every unmet expectation. This goes directly into the product backlog as a priority item, not into a spreadsheet.

### Post-Churn Protocol

- The vacated founding spot is not re-offered to another dealer. The program stays at the original number to preserve the credibility of "5 spots."
- The churn reason must be shared with the product team within 48 hours of cancellation.
- A follow-up contact at 90 days to see if circumstances changed (not to sell, to learn).

## How Founding Dealers Feed the Product Roadmap

### The Feedback Loop

Each Founding Dealer monthly call produces two types of input:

**1. Friction reports** — Things that don't work as expected, add unnecessary steps, or cause confusion. These go into the bug/UX backlog immediately.

**2. Feature requests** — Capabilities the dealer wishes existed. These go into the feature backlog with a priority vote from the founding cohort.

### Prioritization Process

After each round of monthly calls (all 5 dealers in the same calendar month), the product team synthesizes feedback into a prioritized list:

- Features requested by 3+ founding dealers → top of backlog
- Features requested by 2 founding dealers → second tier
- Features requested by 1 founding dealer → third tier, evaluated for broader applicability

### What Founding Dealers Have Already Shaped (Example Categories)

*(To be populated as the program runs — examples of what to track:)*

- "Three dealers requested bulk edit for listing prices → bulk price update tool added in Sprint X"
- "Two dealers said the response-time timer was causing salesperson anxiety → added 'snooze' option for leads in On Hold stage"
- "All five dealers wanted mobile push notifications instead of email-only → mobile notification system prioritized"

### Communicating Roadmap Impact Back to Founders

Founding Dealers should know when their feedback shipped. At the start of each monthly call: "Since our last conversation, we shipped [X] based on your feedback. Here's how it works." This closes the loop and reinforces that their input is genuinely valued — which keeps them engaged and keeps the feedback coming.
