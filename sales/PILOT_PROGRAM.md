# Pilot Program
*Use ONLY when a prospect is genuinely interested but has a specific, named blocker. This is not a standard offer — it is a close tool for high-probability prospects with removable friction.*

---

## When to Offer

Use the pilot when ALL of the following are true:

- [ ] Demo score was 35–49 (Warm — interested but not closing)
- [ ] You can name the specific blocker (not just "they said they'll think about it")
- [ ] Prospect explicitly said something like: "Can we try it first?" / "I need to show my team" / "Hindi pa ako sure sa ROI"
- [ ] They are the decision maker (or DM is reachable within 48 hours)

## When NOT to Offer

Do not offer the pilot if:

- Budget genuinely doesn't exist right now (note for 60-day follow-up instead)
- You haven't confirmed you're talking to the decision maker
- They're just browsing — no specific pain articulated in the demo
- The blocker is "need to talk to partner" — solve that first (get the partner on a call)
- Demo score was <35 — this prospect needs more nurturing, not a faster path

## Pilot Terms

| Term | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Duration | 30 days |
| Price | ₱2,999 (Verified plan pricing) — not free |
| Listings | Up to 20 active listings |
| Included | Inventory import assistance (1 session, ~30 min) |
| Included | CRM setup call (1 hour, Day 1 or Day 2) |
| Included | Weekly check-in (30 min × 4 weeks) |
| Support | Direct WhatsApp line, 4-hour response |

**Success criteria (agree before pilot starts):**
- 10+ listings live by Day 14
- 3+ leads received by Day 30
- CRM used at least weekly (leads moved through pipeline)

**Exit terms:**
- Success criteria met → convert to Founding Dealer at ₱3,599/mo locked (Founding Dealer price available during pilot window only)
- Success criteria not met → full ₱2,999 refund, no questions asked
- Dealer decides to exit early → ₱2,999 refund prorated

## Pilot Pitch Script

*Use word-for-word, or adapt to your natural voice:*

"I understand you want to see it work before committing fully — that's fair. Here's what I suggest: let's do a 30-day pilot at ₱2,999. That's the Verified plan price.

Here's what you get: I personally handle your setup — inventory import, CRM training, and a check-in every week for a month. We agree upfront on what success looks like: 10 listings live, 3 leads received, CRM being used. If that doesn't happen, I refund you in full.

If it works — and I expect it will — you convert to the Founding Dealer plan at ₱3,599/month, locked forever. That price goes away once all 5 founding spots are taken.

So the risk is ₱2,999 for 30 days. The upside is a platform that pays for itself from the first additional lead. Does that work for you?"

**Then stop talking. Wait for their answer.**

## Pilot Success Plan (Week by Week)

**Week 1 — Foundation**
- Day 0: Credentials sent, WhatsApp intro
- Day 1: Setup call — first login, inventory import starts
- Day 3: 5+ listings live — send them a screenshot of how their first listing looks
- Day 7: Check-in call — review what's live, confirm CRM is set up

**Week 2 — First Leads**
- Day 8–10: Platform exposure increases as listings index — expect first inquiries
- Day 10: Check-in — show them their first lead in the CRM inbox
- Day 14: Mid-pilot review — 10 listings target check; if behind, spend 30 min adding together

**Week 3 — Engagement**
- Day 15–21: At least 3 leads should be in the pipeline
- Day 21: Check-in — show them the analytics dashboard (listings viewed, leads by source)
- If 0 leads by Day 21: Review listing quality — title, price, photos. Fix together on the call.

**Week 4 — Conversion**
- Day 25: Pre-close call — "Paano naman ang experience mo so far?"
- Day 28: Present conversion offer: "Success criteria are met. Ready to lock the Founding Dealer price?"
- Day 30: Final review — convert or refund, no pressure

---

## Conversion Trigger

**What success looks like:**
- 10+ listings are live with photos and correct pricing
- At least 3 leads received (visible in CRM inbox)
- Dealer has logged into the CRM at least twice in the last 2 weeks

**How to ask for the full commitment:**

"You've hit the criteria we agreed on — 10 listings live, leads coming in, CRM being used. The Founding Dealer price is ₱3,599/month, and it's locked for you as long as you stay on the platform. There are [X] spots left. Should I convert your account now?"

**If they hesitate:** "Is there something specific that still feels unresolved? Let me address it now."
