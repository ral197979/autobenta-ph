## Slide 2: The Problem

# Three Problems Killing Dealer Profits Today

### Problem 1: Leads Disappear Before You Can Respond

A buyer messages you on Facebook at 10 PM. By morning, they've already bought from someone else. You had no system to alert you, no way to track it, and no way to know it happened.

**Philippine market reality:** 73% of used-car buyers contact 3+ dealers before deciding. The first to respond with a real price wins.

---

### Problem 2: No Visibility Into What's Working

You're posting on Facebook, listing on Philkotse, and running walk-ins. You have no idea which source brings your best buyers, which listing gets the most inquiries, or which salesperson closes the most deals.

**Philippine market reality:** Most dealers operate on gut feel. That means spending money on what feels right, not what actually converts.

---

### Problem 3: Inventory Lives in 5 Different Places

Your cars are on Facebook, on Philkotse, in a spreadsheet, on a whiteboard, and in your head. When a unit sells, you update three of the five — and a buyer wastes their time coming in for a car that's already gone.

**Philippine market reality:** Duplicate and stale listings damage trust. Trust is the #1 purchase driver for used-car buyers in the Philippines.

## Slide 7: Verified Listings

# Trust Is Your Most Valuable Asset

### The Philippine Buyer Trust Problem

Used-car fraud is a real concern for PH buyers. Doctored OR/CRs, rolled-back odometers, flood-damaged units with repainted panels — buyers are skeptical, and rightfully so.

**The result:** Buyers haggle harder, take longer to decide, and ask more questions. Every hour they spend verifying legitimacy is an hour not spent closing.

---

### How AutoBentaPH Verification Works

**Step 1: OR/CR Verification**
Dealer submits OR/CR details. AutoBentaPH cross-references against LTO database. Verified units get a green checkmark on the listing.

**Step 2: V8Atlas Trust Score**
V8Atlas assigns a trust score based on VIN history, ownership chain, and reported incidents. Displayed as a 1–100 score on each listing.

**Step 3: 5-Point Inspection Badge**
For dealers who complete the physical inspection checklist (engine, transmission, exterior, interior, electrical), listings carry a 5-point inspection badge.

---
