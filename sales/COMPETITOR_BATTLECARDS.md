# Competitor Battlecards

**Purpose:** Quick-reference cards for handling competitive conversations during demos and sales calls. Read these before any prospect meeting. The goal is not to trash competitors — it's to redirect the conversation to your strengths.

---

## Facebook Marketplace

**When you hear:** "Gumagamit na kami ng Facebook — libre naman at maraming buyers doon."

**Their strength:** Massive organic reach, free, familiar to both buyers and sellers, everyone in the Philippines already uses it.

**Their weakness:** Zero lead management. Every inquiry lives in Facebook Messenger and disappears into the noise. No follow-up system, no history, no way to know which leads are hot. Dealers lose 20-30% of leads simply because messages get buried.

**Your response:** "Facebook is great for reach — we're not replacing Facebook. But what happens after someone messages you? Do you know which messages you've already followed up on? How many leads slipped through last month because they got buried in Messenger? AutoBentaPH takes those same leads and organizes them so nothing falls through."

**Proof point:** 8-stage Kanban CRM captures every inquiry — from Facebook, Philkotse, or directly from AutoBentaPH — in one dashboard. No more lost leads in Messenger.

**Bridge question:** "Ngayon, pag may nag-message sa inyo sa Facebook, nasaan iyon? Excel? Messenger lang? Anong mangyayari pagkatapos ng 3 araw na walang reply?"

## Philkotse

**When you hear:** "Naka-list na kami sa Philkotse." / "Nagte-try na kami ng Philkotse."

**Their strength:** Established brand in the PH used-car market. Buyers know it. Some dealer trust already built.

**Their weakness:** Pure portal model — ₱1,500 per listing, no CRM, no lead management, no analytics. You pay per listing even if the car doesn't sell. Costs scale badly as inventory grows.

**Your response:** "Philkotse is a known portal and we respect that. But here's the math — if you have 20 cars listed, that's ₱30,000/month in listing fees with no CRM, no follow-up tools, and no analytics. AutoBentaPH Dealer Pro is ₱5,999/month for unlimited listings plus full CRM plus analytics. And you still get buyers — because our marketplace is also buyer-facing."

**Proof point:** AutoBentaPH gives you unlimited listings on the Dealer Pro plan. At ₱5,999/month vs. ₱1,500/listing, the math shifts dramatically at 5+ cars.

**Bridge question:** "Sa Philkotse, pagkatapos mag-inquire ng buyer, saan napupunta? Nai-track ba ninyo kung sino ang nag-follow up?"

## OLX Philippines

**When you hear:** "Nag-po-post din kami sa OLX."

**Their strength:** Large general classifieds audience. Free basic listings. Many Filipinos browse for deals there.

**Their weakness:** General classifieds — not automotive-specific, no dealer tools, no CRM, no Verified badge system, no trust infrastructure. Buyers associate OLX with informal transactions, not professional dealers.

**Your response:** "OLX is useful for volume, but it's the same problem as Facebook — no CRM, no follow-up, no trust signals. Buyers on AutoBentaPH see a Verified OR/CR badge, professional photos, and dealer credibility markers — they're more serious buyers, not bargain hunters. And every inquiry goes into your CRM automatically."

**Proof point:** Verified OR/CR badge on AutoBentaPH listings — buyers know the car's documentation is confirmed by the platform. OLX has no equivalent.

**Bridge question:** "Anong klaseng buyers ang nakakakuha kayo sa OLX? Seryoso ba sila o maraming paligoy-ligoy?"

## Carsome

**When you hear:** "Kilala ko ang Carsome." / "Carsome din ba kayo?"

**Their strength:** Strong brand, B2C buying experience, inspection process, financing.

**Their weakness:** Carsome buys cars directly from individual owners — they are not a dealer platform. They are a competitor to the dealers themselves. They are not a tool for dealers; they are a buyer.

**Your response:** "Carsome and AutoBentaPH are completely different. Carsome buys cars from individuals to resell — they compete with dealers. AutoBentaPH is a platform for dealers like you to sell your own inventory directly to buyers, with a CRM to manage your leads. We help you grow your business, not replace you."

**Proof point:** AutoBentaPH is dealer-centric — dealers own their listings, own their leads, and own their relationships with buyers.

**Bridge question:** "Gusto ba ninyong ibenta ang inventory ninyo sa Carsome, o gusto ninyong mag-sell directly sa buyers at mapanatili ang margin?"

## CDK Global

**When you hear:** "Narinig ko na ang CDK." / "May mas established na DMS systems diba?"

**Their strength:** Comprehensive DMS, proven in large dealerships globally, extensive features.

**Their weakness:** ₱200,000+/year cost. Minimum 6-month implementation. Built for large franchise dealers with 200+ unit sales/month. Massive overkill for an independent PH dealer selling 10-50 cars/month. No PH-specific support.

**Your response:** "CDK is a serious platform built for franchise dealerships doing hundreds of units per month. If you're a 10-50 car/month dealer, you'd pay ₱200K+ per year for a system that's 80% irrelevant to your operation. AutoBentaPH is built specifically for independent Philippine dealers — you're up and running in a day, not 6 months."

**Proof point:** AutoBentaPH setup takes 1 day. First listing live within the hour. No implementation fee. No long-term contract.

**Bridge question:** "Magkano ang budget ninyo para sa tools ngayon? Ilang buwan bago kayo makapag-start sa CDK?"

## Reynolds & Reynolds

**When you hear:** "May iba pang DMS systems ba maliban sa ganito?"

**Their strength:** Legacy DMS leader, deeply integrated, trusted by large US dealers for decades.

**Their weakness:** ₱500,000+ implementation cost. No Philippine presence, support, or localization. Built for US dealer compliance workflows — completely inapplicable to LTO, OR/CR, and Philippine registration processes.

**Your response:** "Reynolds & Reynolds doesn't operate in the Philippines — there's no local support, no peso pricing, and the system is built for US dealer compliance, not LTO and OR/CR workflows. AutoBentaPH is built from the ground up for Philippine used-car dealers — in Filipino, in pesos, with Philippine vehicle documentation in mind."

**Proof point:** AutoBentaPH Verified badge checks OR/CR documentation — a specifically Philippine requirement that no US DMS handles.

**Bridge question:** "Ano ang pinaka-importante sa inyo — isang system na globally proven pero built for US, o isang system na specifically designed para sa PH market at suportahan kayo ng lokal na team?"

## DealerSocket

**Their strength:** Comprehensive US dealer CRM, strong feature set, widely used.

**Their weakness:** Not available in the Philippines. No pricing in Philippine pesos. No LTO/OR/CR documentation support. No PH customer support.

**Your response:** "DealerSocket isn't operating in the Philippines. Even if you signed up, you'd have no local support, no peso billing, and none of the Philippine-specific workflows you need. AutoBentaPH is the only dealer CRM built specifically for the PH market."

**Proof point:** AutoBentaPH is Philippines-first — local support, peso pricing, Philippine-specific features.

**Bridge question:** "For PH dealers, ano ang pinaka-important: international brand, o may taong makakatawag kapag may problema?"

## Excel / Manual Tracking ("We just use spreadsheets")

**When you hear:** "Excel lang kami." / "Notes lang sa phone." / "Alam ko naman kung sino yung customers ko."

**Their strength:** Free. Familiar. No learning curve. No monthly cost.

**Their weakness:** No real-time updates, no follow-up reminders, no lead routing, no shared visibility if you have staff, breaks down completely as volume scales past 10 leads/month, and zero buyer-facing discovery.

**Your response:** "Excel works when you're small and have time to manage it manually. But the moment you're handling 20+ leads/month, things fall through. You miss a follow-up, someone buys from your competitor, and you never know. The CRM isn't just about organization — it's about not losing money when you're too busy to remember every lead."

**Proof point:** 8-stage Kanban CRM with follow-up reminders — you can see every lead, every stage, and every next action in one view. It takes less time than maintaining a spreadsheet, and it doesn't miss leads.

**Bridge question:** "Sa isang buwan, ilang leads ang hindi na-follow up dahil nakalimutan o nawala sa notes? Kahit isa lang, magkano ang halaga noon?"

## "We Built Our Own System" (In-House)

**When you hear:** "May ginawa na kaming sariling sistema." / "Custom system kami."

**Their strength:** Tailored to their exact workflow. No external dependency.

**Their weakness:** Expensive to maintain and update. No dedicated product team. No buyer-facing marketplace — their custom system doesn't bring them new buyers. Usually falls apart when the person who built it leaves.

**Your response:** "That's impressive — most dealers don't have that. The question isn't whether your internal system works — it probably does for operations. The gap is the buyer-facing side: does your custom system bring you new buyers who don't already know you? AutoBentaPH is a marketplace — your listings are discoverable by buyers actively searching for cars right now."

**Proof point:** AutoBentaPH marketplace generates new buyer discovery that no internal system can replicate. Your custom CRM manages existing leads; AutoBentaPH generates new ones.

**Bridge question:** "Yung custom system ninyo — nagdadala ba ito ng bagong buyers na hindi pa nila kilala ang dealership ninyo? O para lang sa management ng existing leads?"
