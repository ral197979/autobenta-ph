# Follow-Up #1 Template

**When to use:** 4 days after cold outreach with no reply. This is your first follow-up.

**Channel:** Same channel as first message (WhatsApp preferred). If they haven't opened or read the first message, try a different time of day.

**Goal:** Get a reply — any reply. Even "not interested" is better than silence because it lets you move on or ask why.

**Tone:** Friendly, low-pressure. You're not chasing — you're checking in with something new to say.

---

## WhatsApp / SMS Version

**Option A — New angle (ROI focus):**

```
Hi [First Name], si [Your Name] muli ito mula AutoBentaPH. 

Quick question: sa isang buwan, ilang leads ang hindi na-follow up dahil nawala sa Messenger o nakalimutan? Kahit 2-3 leads lang iyon, sa average na ₱20,000 profit per car, iyan ay ₱40,000–₱60,000 na potential na nawala.

Iyan lang ang gusto kong ipakita sa inyo sa 15-minute demo. Pwede ba tayo this week?
```

**Option B — Competitor pain angle:**

```
Hi [First Name]! Si [Your Name] ito. 

Nakita ko na [naka-list kayo sa Philkotse / actively posting kayo sa Facebook]. Para sa dealers na kayo, alam ko na ang pinakamalaking problema: maraming inquiries, pero hindi lahat nai-close dahil walang system para i-follow up.

May time ba kayo this week para makita kung paano ayusin iyon?
```

**Short version (SMS):**

```
Hi [First Name], [Your Name] ulit mula AutoBentaPH. Gusto ko lang ipakita kung paano makakakuha ng mas maraming benta mula sa existing leads ninyo. 15 min — pwede ba this week?
```

## Email Version

**Subject:** Re: Dealer tools para sa [their city] — isa pang bagay na gusto kong ipakita

**Body:**

```
Hi [First Name],

Nagpadala ako ng mensahe ilang araw na ang nakalipas — baka busy kayo.

Gusto ko lang magdagdag ng isang konkretong bagay:

Ang average dealer na walang CRM ay nawawalan ng 20–30% ng leads dahil hindi na-follow up — nababaon sa Messenger, hindi naibabalik ang tawag, o nakalimutan.

Para sa dealer na nagbebenta ng 10 cars/month sa ₱20,000 avg margin, iyan ay ₱40,000–₱60,000 bawat buwan ang potential na nawawala.

AutoBentaPH ay ₱2,999/month. Kahit isa lang ang karagdagang benta, nag-cover na.

May 15-minute Zoom o tawag ba kayo this week? Gusto kong ipakita ang platform gamit ang inyong actual numbers.

[Your Name]
AutoBentaPH
[Phone]
```

## Notes

- **Try a different time of day** than your first message. If you sent at 10am before, try 2pm or 5pm.
- **Don't apologize** for following up. You're not bothering them — you're offering something useful.
- **If still no reply after this:** Move to FOLLOW_UP_2 (Day 10) — different angle, shorter message.
- **If they reply "not interested":** Log reason in CRM, thank them warmly, ask if you can check in again in 3 months.
- **Max follow-ups for cold outreach:** 3 (Day 1, Day 4, Day 10) + Breakup (Day 20). After that, archive.
