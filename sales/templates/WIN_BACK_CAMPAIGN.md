# Win-Back Campaign Template

**When to use:** For dealers who cancelled, churned, or went unresponsive after a demo or proposal. Also for dormant prospects who showed early interest but went cold 60+ days ago.

**Channel:** WhatsApp primary (personal feel). Email as backup for churned paying customers (they have an email record).

**Goal:** Re-engage, understand what changed, offer a reason to take another look.

**When NOT to use:** Within 30 days of a clear rejection ("hindi talaga kailangan"). Give it time. Win-back at 60–90 days.

---

## Version 1: Dormant Prospect (60+ Days, Never Paid)

### WhatsApp Version

```
Hi [First Name]! Si [Your Name] ito mula AutoBentaPH. 

Matagal na kami nag-usap — [X months] na pala! Gusto ko lang mag-check in.

Ilang bagong features ang na-launch namin simula noon:
- [Feature 1 — e.g., "AI listing wizard para mas mabilis ang upload"]
- [Feature 2 — e.g., "V8Atlas DMS sync para automatic na ang inventory update"]
- [Feature 3 — e.g., "Mas maraming buyers na — growing na ang marketplace"]

Ang tanong ko lang: still using Facebook/Excel for leads? O nagbago na ang setup ninyo?

Kung gusto ninyong makita ang updated platform — 15 minuto lang, libre pa rin.
```

## Version 2: Churned Paying Customer (Was on a Plan, Cancelled)

### WhatsApp Version

```
Hi [First Name]! Si [Your Name] ito mula AutoBentaPH.

Nakita ko na nag-cancel kayo ng subscription. Naiintindihan ko — may dahilan lahat.

Gusto ko lang malaman: anong nangyari? 

Hindi sales call ito — gusto ko talagang malaman kung ano ang hindi gumana para mapabuti namin ang platform para sa mga dealers tulad ninyo.

Kahit 5 minuto lang — pwede ba akong tumawag this week?
```

### Email Version (Churned Paying Customer)

**Subject:** [First Name] — matagal na, gusto ko lang makinig

```
Hi [First Name],

Nakita namin na nag-cancel kayo ng AutoBentaPH subscription. Salamat sa nagdaang [X months] — alam ko na hindi palaging perpekto ang lahat.

At kung balang araw gusto ninyong subukan uli — nandito kami. Walang masamang loob.

[Your Name]
AutoBentaPH
[Phone]
```

## Version 3: Re-Engagement Offer (For High-Value Churned or Near-Close Prospect)

**Use only if:** They previously showed strong interest, churned or stalled due to a specific reason (budget, timing, feature gap), and enough time has passed that the reason may have changed.

### WhatsApp Version

```
Hi [First Name]! Si [Your Name] ito.

Naaalala ko na nag-usap tayo noong [month] — gusto ninyo noon pero hindi pa tamang oras [o: may budget concern].

Gusto ko lang i-share: [relevant update — e.g., "nagdagdag na kami ng [feature they wanted]" / "nagbago ang pricing namin" / "mas maraming dealers na sa platform namin"].

Kung baka nag-bago na ang situation ninyo, pwede ba tayong mag-usap ulit ng 15 minuto? Walang pressure — gusto ko lang i-update kayo sa kung saan na kami.
```

## Version 4: Breakup Message (Day 20 of Cold Cadence — Last Message)

**Use:** For any cold prospect who hasn't replied to 3 messages over 20 days. This is the final message before archiving.

### WhatsApp Version

```
Hi [First Name], huling mensahe na ito mula sa akin.

Tatlong beses na akong nagpadala — naiintindihan ko na baka hindi talaga ang tamang oras ngayon, o baka hindi talaga relevant ang AutoBentaPH sa inyo.

Hindi ko na kayo aabalahin pagkatapos nito.

Kung balang araw nagbabago ang isip ninyo o gusto ninyong makita ang platform, nandito pa rin kami: [your-domain]

Ingat kayo, [First Name] — at sana maayos ang negosyo ninyo.

— [Your Name], AutoBentaPH
```

## Personalization Fields

| Field | What to Put |
|-------|------------|
| `[First Name]` | Their first name |
| `[Your Name]` | Your name |
| `[X months]` | How long since last contact |
| `[month]` | Month of previous conversation |
| `[Feature 1/2/3]` | Actual features shipped since last contact |
| `[relevant update]` | Specific reason you're reaching out now |
| `[your-domain]` | Your actual domain |

## Notes

- **Win-back timeline:** Wait at least 60 days after churn or last contact before reaching out. Anything sooner feels desperate.
- **Churn feedback call:** Even if they don't come back, a 5-minute "what went wrong" call is valuable. Don't skip it — it's product research.
- **Never offer a discount as the first move.** Ask what happened first. If price was the issue, then you can discuss options — but only offer a 1-month extension (not a permanent price reduction).
- **The breakup message often gets replies.** People respond to closure. If they come back from a breakup message, treat it like a warm lead.
- **For churned paying customers:** The emotional tone should be understanding and curious, not sales-y. They already know the product. You need to know the real reason they left.
