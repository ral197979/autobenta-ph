# Cold Outreach Template

**When to use:** First contact with a dealer who doesn't know you yet. They were found through Facebook Marketplace, Philkotse, OLX, or referral research.

**Channel:** WhatsApp or SMS first. Email only if you have their email and no phone number.

**Key rules:**
- Always personalize with their name and something specific about their business
- Don't pitch features — pitch the problem you solve
- Keep the first message short. Under 5 lines.
- Always end with a question or clear call to action

---

## WhatsApp / SMS Version

**For dealers found on Facebook Marketplace:**

```
Hi [First Name]! Nakita ko ang listings ninyo sa Facebook — [specific car model or "maganda ang lineup ninyo"]. 

Ito si [Your Name] mula AutoBentaPH — nagtatayo kami ng CRM + marketplace para sa mga used-car dealers sa Pilipinas. Para hindi na mawala ang leads sa Messenger, at mas maraming buyers ang makakakita ng listings ninyo.

May 15-20 minuto ba kayo this week para makita ang platform? Free demo, walang obligasyon.
```

**For dealers found on Philkotse:**

```
Hi [First Name]! Nakita ko ang [specific car or "dealer profile ninyo"] sa Philkotse.

Ito si [Your Name] mula AutoBentaPH. CRM at marketplace kami para sa dealers — para hindi na kayo magbayad ng ₱1,500 per listing at ma-organize ang lahat ng leads ninyo sa iisang lugar.

Pwede ba kayo makipag-usap ng 15 minuto this week?
```

**Short version (SMS — under 160 chars):**

```
Hi [Name]! [Your Name] ito mula AutoBentaPH — CRM + marketplace para sa car dealers. 15-min demo, libre. Interesado? 😊
```

## Email Version

**Subject:** Dealer tools para sa [their city / "Metro Manila"] — AutoBentaPH

**Body:**

```
Hi [First Name],

Nakita ko ang [dealer page / Philkotse listing] ninyo — maganda ang lineup ng sasakyan.

Ito si [Your Name] mula AutoBentaPH. Nagtatayo kami ng platform para sa used-car dealers dito sa Pilipinas — specifically para sa problemang ito: maraming buyers ang nag-i-inquire sa Facebook o sa portals, tapos nawawala sa Messenger bago matapos ang deal.

Ang AutoBentaPH ay:
- CRM na nag-o-organize ng lahat ng leads mula sa Facebook, Philkotse, at aming marketplace
- 8-stage Kanban para malaman kung nasaan ang bawat buyer
- Verified OR/CR listings para mas mapagkatiwalaan ng buyers
- ₱2,999/month — walang per-listing na bayad

Kung [X] cars ang binesenta ninyo sa isang buwan at isa lang ang additional sale dahil sa mas mabilis na follow-up, nag-cover na ang cost.

May 15-20 minuto ba kayong this week para makita ko? Libre, walang obligasyon.

[Your Name]
AutoBentaPH
[Phone number]
```

## Personalization Fields

| Field | What to Put |
|-------|------------|
| `[First Name]` | Their first name only — Filipinos are informal |
| `[specific car model]` | E.g., "yung Honda CR-V" or "yung Vios lineup ninyo" — shows you actually looked |
| `[their city]` | Metro Manila, QC, Makati, etc. |
| `[X cars]` | Estimate based on their active listings — "10 cars" or "20+ cars" |
| `[Your Name]` | Your first name |

---

## Notes

- **WhatsApp is far more effective than email** for Philippine dealers. Use it first.
- **Send between 9am–6pm on weekdays** (GMT+8). Avoid Friday afternoons.
- **Don't send a wall of text.** If it looks long on mobile, cut it in half.
- **If no reply in 4 days:** Use FOLLOW_UP_1 template — not this one again.
- **Mention something specific** about their business in the first line. Generic messages get ignored.
