# Follow-Up #2 Template

**When to use:** Day 10 after your initial cold outreach — no reply to the first two messages. This is your last "normal" follow-up before the breakup message.

**Channel:** WhatsApp preferred. Try calling if you haven't yet.

**Goal:** Get any response. If still nothing after this, send the breakup message at Day 20, then archive.

**Tone:** Confident and direct. Short. No apology. Provide one new piece of value.

---

## WhatsApp / SMS Version

**Option A — Social proof angle (use when you have a Founding Dealer signed or demo completed):**

```
Hi [First Name], si [Your Name] ito mula AutoBentaPH — huling beses lang.

Na-onboard na namin ang aming unang Founding Dealers ngayong buwan. Ang sabi nila: "Hindi ko alam na ganito karaming leads ang nawawala ko."

5 spots lang ang Founding Dealer rate (₱3,599/month, 40% off, locked for life). [X] na lang ang natitira.

Gusto mo bang makita bago maubos? 15 minuto lang.
```

**Option B — No social proof yet:**

```
Hi [First Name], si [Your Name] ito mula AutoBentaPH — huling follow-up na ito bago ko kayong huwag nang abalahin. 

Gusto ko lang malaman kung: may dahilan ba na hindi ito tamang oras? Budget? Hindi priority ngayon? Naiintindihan ko — gusto ko lang malaman para alam ko kung kailan okay na bumalik.

Kung interesado pa rin kayo kahit konti, ang Founding Dealer program ay [X] spots na lang. Magsasara na ito soon.
```

**Short version (SMS):**

```
Hi [First Name], [Your Name] ito — [X] Founding Dealer spots na lang ang natitira sa ₱3,599/mo. Kung gusto pang makita, ngayon na. Kung hindi pa oras, okay lang — babalik ako sa [month].
```

## Email Version

**Subject:** [X] Founding Dealer spots na lang — [First Name]

**Body:**

```
Hi [First Name],

Dalawang beses na akong nagpadala — baka hindi lang tamang oras.

Gusto ko lang sabihin itong isa bago ako tumigil:

Ang Founding Dealer program ng AutoBentaPH ay 5 spots lang, at [X] na lang ang natitira. Sa ₱3,599/month (locked for life, 40% off sa regular na ₱5,999), ito ang pinaka-mababang presyo na makukuha ninyo — ever. Kapag naubos na ang spots, babalik sa regular rate.

Kung hindi tamang oras ngayon, okay lang — babalik ako in [2 months]. Pero kung gusto ninyong ma-lock ang rate na ito, kailangan nating mag-usap this week.

May 15 minuto ba kayo?

[Your Name]
AutoBentaPH
[Phone]

P.S. Kung hindi na talaga interested, okay lang sabihin — iuunsubscribe ko kayo sa follow-ups at hindi na kayo aabalahin.
```

## Personalization Fields

| Field | What to Put |
|-------|------------|
| `[First Name]` | Their first name |
| `[Your Name]` | Your first name |
| `[X] spots` | How many Founding Dealer spots remain |
| `[month]` | 2 months from now — e.g., "August" |

---

## Notes

- **If they haven't called you back yet:** Try calling first before sending this WhatsApp. A live voice breaks through noise.
- **If they reply with an objection:** That's a win. Engage it directly using DEALER_OBJECTION_HANDLING.md.
- **If no reply after Day 20:** Send the breakup message (a short "closing the loop" note), then mark as `dormant` in CRM and schedule a 60-day re-ping.
- **Scarcity must be real.** Only use "[X] spots remaining" if it's true. Don't fabricate urgency — it destroys trust.
