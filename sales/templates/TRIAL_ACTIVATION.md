# Trial Activation Template

**When to use:** When a dealer signs up but hasn't logged in or activated their account yet. Also used proactively after sending login credentials to make sure they actually get started.

**Channel:** WhatsApp (primary). Call if no reply after 24 hours.

**Goal:** Get them to log in, see the platform for the first time, and complete at least one action (upload a listing, view the CRM).

**Key insight:** The first login is the most critical moment. If a dealer doesn't log in within 48 hours of getting credentials, churn risk spikes significantly.

---

## Version 1: Login Credentials Delivery (Day 0, Right After Account Created)

### WhatsApp Version

```
Hi [First Name]! Welcome sa AutoBentaPH! 🎉

Na-create na ang account ninyo — nandito ang login details:

🔗 [your-domain]/dealer/login
📧 Email: [their email]
🔑 Temporary password: [temp password]

I-change ang password pagka-login ninyo.

Gusto ko kayong sabayan sa onboarding call bukas o sa [date] — ilang minuto lang para ma-set up ang unang listings ninyo. Kailan kayo available?

Nandito ako pag may tanong. Welcome to the team!
— [Your Name]
```

## Version 3: 48-Hour Non-Activation Follow-Up (High Risk — Call First)

### Call Script (Before WhatsApp):

"Hi [First Name], ito si [Your Name] mula AutoBentaPH. Gusto ko lang i-check — nakatanggap ba kayo ng login credentials? Gusto ko sanang sabayan kayo sa first login ngayon o bukas — 20 minuto lang. Kailan okay sa inyo?"

### WhatsApp (If Call Unanswered):

```
Hi [First Name]! Tinawagan ko kayo kanina.

Pag mayroon kayong 20-30 minuto kahit kailan this week, tara na. Ang listings ninyo ay pwedeng maging live today. 

Anong oras okay sa inyo?
```

---

## Notes

- **Never paste passwords in email** if you can avoid it — WhatsApp is encrypted end-to-end and more appropriate for credentials.
- **48-hour rule:** If no login in 48 hours, call. Don't wait. This is your clearest churn signal in Month 1.
- **Celebrate the first listing.** A small acknowledgment goes a long way in keeping a new dealer motivated.
- **If they have trouble logging in:** Troubleshoot immediately. Don't let a technical friction point kill the activation. Admin panel → Dealers → resend invite if needed.
