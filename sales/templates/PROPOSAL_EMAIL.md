# Proposal Email Template

**When to use:** Within 24 hours after completing a demo. Send the same day if possible — while the demo is still fresh.

**Channel:** Email (primary, so they have something to refer back to) + WhatsApp notification that the email is sent.

**Goal:** Confirm value shown in demo, present the right plan, show their specific ROI, make it easy to say yes.

---

## WhatsApp Notification (Send First)

```
Hi [First Name]! Salamat sa oras ninyo kanina — maganda ang demo. 

Nagpadala ako ng email sa [their email] na may summary ng ipinakita namin at ang pricing para sa inyo. Check ninyo pag may time — tapos magusap tayo kung may tanong.
```

## Personalization Fields

| Field | What to Put |
|-------|------------|
| `[First Name]` | Their first name |
| `[Dealer Name]` | Their dealership name (for subject line) |
| `[their email]` | Their email address |
| `[X] cars` | Cars/month they told you |
| `[Y]` | Their avg profit per car |
| `[Z]` | ~20% of their monthly leads |
| `[2-3]` | Conservative recovery estimate |
| `[plan price]` | The plan you're recommending |
| Summary bullets | List only what you actually showed in the demo |
| Recommended plan | Highlight the right one — don't show all three equally |

---

## Notes

- **Send within 24 hours of the demo.** The first 24 hours have 80% of the buying energy — don't waste it.
- **Calculate their actual ROI numbers** before sending. Generic numbers feel lazy. Their numbers feel personal.
- **Recommend one plan clearly.** Don't make them choose without guidance. Say "Based on [X cars/month], I recommend Dealer Pro because..."
- **For Founding Dealer prospects:** Always include the Founding Dealer option prominently. The 40% locked discount is a powerful closer.
- **Follow up on this email** within 3 business days if no reply. Use a brief WhatsApp: "Nakita mo na ba ang email? May tanong ba?"
