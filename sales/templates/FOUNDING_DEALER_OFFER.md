# Founding Dealer Offer Template

**When to use:** Three situations:
1. **Prospecting** — to introduce the Founding Dealer program to a warm or cold prospect
2. **Post-demo close** — to push a demo-completed prospect over the line with the Founding Dealer angle
3. **Welcome** — when a dealer has just paid, send a warm welcome that reinforces their decision

**Channel:** WhatsApp (prospecting/close) + Email (post-demo, welcome)

**Key urgency driver:** 5 spots, limited time, rate locked for life. Never manufacture false scarcity — only use these if they're true.

## Personalization Fields

| Field | What to Put |
|-------|------------|
| `[First Name]` | Their first name |
| `[Your Name]` | Your name |
| `[X] spots` | Exact number of remaining Founding Dealer spots |
| `[X cars/month, current system]` | Their specifics from the demo |
| `[their email]` | Their email address |
| `[your-domain]` | Your actual domain |
| `[time]` | Specific time you're proposing for onboarding |

---

## Notes

- **The "locked for life" angle is your strongest closer.** Price anchoring against ₱5,999/month makes the savings feel real and permanent.
- **Only use "[X] spots remaining" if accurate.** The moment this feels manufactured, trust breaks. Keep it honest.
- **Welcome message should feel celebratory but not over-the-top.** One exclamation point is fine. Not every sentence.
- **Send credentials separately from the welcome message** — never include passwords in the same message as marketing content.
- **Set onboarding call within 24 hours.** Momentum from signing must carry straight into setup.
