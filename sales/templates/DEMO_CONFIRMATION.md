# Demo Confirmation Template

**When to use:** Two situations:
1. **Booking confirmation** — right after they agree to a demo, to confirm the time
2. **Pre-demo reminder** — 2 hours before the scheduled demo

**Channel:** WhatsApp first. Email as backup or if they gave you an email.

---

## Version 1: Booking Confirmation (Right After They Agree)

### WhatsApp Version

```
Hi [First Name]! Confirmed — demo tayo sa [Day], [Date] sa [Time] (PH time). 

Gagamitin natin ang [Zoom/Google Meet/phone call — whichever you agreed on]. [Link if applicable]

Para mas personalized ang demo, pwede bang sabihin ninyo:
1. Ilang sasakyan ang binesenta ninyo last month?
2. Anong system ang ginagamit ninyo ngayon para sa leads — Facebook Messenger? Excel? Wala pa?

Excited na akong ipakita sa inyo! 🚗
— [Your Name]
```

### Email Version

**Subject:** Demo confirmed — [Day], [Date] sa [Time]

```
Hi [First Name],

Salamat sa oras — excited na!

Demo Details:
📅 [Day], [Date]
⏰ [Time] (Philippine Standard Time, GMT+8)
📍 [Zoom/Google Meet link] o tawag sa [your phone number]

Para mas maging useful ang demo, may dalawang tanong lang:
1. Ilang sasakyan ang binesenta ninyo sa isang buwan sa average?
2. Ano ang ginagamit ninyo ngayon para sa leads at follow-up?

Hindi required — pero kapag alam ko, mas matutukoy ko kung aling parte ng platform ang pinaka-relevant sa inyo.

Hanggang [Day]!

[Your Name]
AutoBentaPH
[Phone]
```

## Version 2: Pre-Demo Reminder (2 Hours Before)

### WhatsApp Version

```
Hi [First Name]! Demo natin ngayong [time] — 2 hours na lang!

Kumpirmahin lang: okay pa ba kayo?

[If Zoom/Meet]: Link natin: [link]
[If phone call]: Tatawagin ko kayo sa [their number] sa [time].

Pag may problema, i-message lang ako agad — madaling mag-reschedule. See you soon!
```

### SMS Version (if WhatsApp not confirmed read)

```
Hi [First Name]! Demo natin ngayong [time] — kumpirma lang kung okay pa kayo. [Your Name] / AutoBentaPH
```

---

## No-Show WhatsApp (After Waiting 10 Minutes)

```
Hi [First Name]! Demo natin kanina sa [scheduled time] — naghihintay ako. Okay ba kayo?

Kung may nangyari, okay lang mag-reschedule — kailan available kayo ngayon o bukas?
```

---

## Personalization Fields

| Field | What to Put |
|-------|------------|
| `[First Name]` | Their first name |
| `[Day]` | e.g., "Miyerkules" |
| `[Date]` | e.g., "June 5" |
| `[Time]` | e.g., "2:00 PM" |
| `[Zoom/Google Meet/phone call]` | Whichever you agreed on |
| `[Link]` | Your Zoom or Meet link |
| `[Your Name]` | Your name |
| `[their number]` | Their number if you're calling them |

---

## Notes

- **Always ask the two pre-demo questions** (volume, current system). Even rough answers help you tailor the demo.
- **If no reply to the confirmation within 1 hour:** Call them. Unconfirmed demos are high no-show risk.
- **Use their local language cues** — if they write to you in Tagalog, reply in Tagalog. If in English, match that.
- **The no-show WhatsApp tone is warm, never frustrated.** They're busy. Make it easy to come back.
