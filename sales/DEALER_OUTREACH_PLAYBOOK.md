# Dealer Outreach Playbook

**Purpose:** Daily operating manual for prospecting and outreaching to Philippine used-car dealers. Written for a single founder running all sales.

---

## Prospecting Sources (Ranked by Quality)

### 1. Inbound — /for-dealers or /book-demo (Highest Priority)
Someone visited the site and raised their hand. Treat this like gold.
- **Response target:** Within 2 hours, always. No exceptions.
- **First action:** Phone call. If no answer, WhatsApp immediately after.
- **Why it matters:** Inbound intent converts 3-5x higher than cold. Every hour of delay loses deals.

### 2. Referral from Existing Contact
Someone vouched for you. Trust is already established.
- **Response target:** Same day. Same hour if possible.
- **First action:** Phone call. Open with the referrer's name in the first sentence.
- **Script:** "Hi [Name], ito si [Your Name] mula AutoBentaPH — si [Referrer] nagbigay ng contact mo, sabi niya baka interesado ka sa aming CRM para sa dealers."

### 3. Cold Outreach — Known Metro Manila Dealers
Dealers you've identified through research. They don't know you yet.
- **Sources:** Facebook Marketplace dealer pages, Philkotse, OLX, word of mouth
- **Response target:** No urgency — but don't let them sit in your list more than 3 days before first contact.
- **First action:** WhatsApp intro message (see templates). Follow cadence below.

### 4. Facebook Marketplace Dealer Pages (Manual Research)
Search "used cars [city]" on Facebook. Look for accounts posting 10+ cars.
- **Signals of a good prospect:** Multiple posts per week, responds to comments, has a branded page name (not personal account)
- **Contact method:** Message their Facebook page OR find their phone number in the listing

### 5. Philkotse / OLX Dealer Listings
Search Philkotse for dealers with multiple active listings. Check OLX for dealers with "View All Listings" links.
- **Contact info:** Often listed on the profile. Note their phone number.
- **Context to note:** Which platform they're paying to advertise on (Philkotse listings cost ₱1,500 each — use this in your pitch)

### 6. Event / Trade Show Contacts
CAMPI, CATS (Car Auction), auto expos — collect business cards, add to CRM within 24 hours.
- **Follow-up:** Within 48 hours while memory is fresh. Reference where you met.

## Prospect Qualification Criteria (BANT for PH Dealers)

Use this to decide whether to invest time in a prospect or move on.

### Budget
**Question:** Can they afford ₱2,999–₱5,999/month?
**Signal:** More than 10 cars per month in inventory
**Check:** How many active listings do they have on Facebook or Philkotse right now?
- 10+ active listings = budget likely there
- 3-5 listings = maybe, ask about volume
- 1-2 listings = low priority unless they're growing fast

**If unsure, ask directly:** "Ilang sasakyan kayo nagbebenta sa isang buwan?" (How many cars do you sell per month?)

### Authority
**Question:** Are you talking to the owner or decision maker?
**Signal:** They say "ako ang may-ari" or use "kami" when describing the business
**Red flag:** "Ikukuha ko si boss" after you've already pitched — you pitched the wrong person
**Fix:** Ask early: "Ikaw ba ang nag-decide sa mga tools na ginagamit ng business?" (Are you the one who decides on tools used by the business?)

### Need
**Question:** Do they have real pain with their current process?
**Pain signals to listen for:**
- "Nawawala na ang messages sa Messenger"
- "Hindi ko alam kung sino na ang nag-follow up"
- "Excel lang kami, madalas may mali"
- "Gusto ko ng mas maraming buyers pero di ko alam san hanapin"

**If they have no pain**, they are browsing. Deprioritize. Come back in 30 days.

### Timeline
**Question:** Are they actively shopping for a solution right now?
**Hot:** They asked for a demo, mentioned a specific problem, or recently had a bad experience
**Warm:** They're interested but "thinking about it"
**Cold:** "Baka sa susunod" / "Hindi pa masyado" — note and follow up in 60 days

## Outreach Cadence

### Inbound Leads (from /book-demo or /for-dealers)
| Day | Action |
|-----|--------|
| Day 1 (same day, <2 hrs) | Phone call. If no answer: WhatsApp immediately. |
| Day 2 | Email if no response to WhatsApp. Try a different time of day. |
| Day 5 | Final follow-up — WhatsApp. If no reply, mark as "unresponsive" and schedule a 30-day re-ping. |

### Cold Outreach
| Day | Action |
|-----|--------|
| Day 1 | WhatsApp/SMS intro message (see COLD_OUTREACH template) |
| Day 4 | Follow-up #1 — WhatsApp. Reference their specific listing or platform. |
| Day 10 | Follow-up #2 — try a different angle (ROI, competitor pain) |
| Day 20 | Breakup message — "Last na ito, gusto ko lang malaman kung interesado pa rin." Then archive if no reply. |

### Referral
| Day | Action |
|-----|--------|
| Day 1 | Phone call. Mention referrer name in first sentence. If no answer, WhatsApp immediately with referrer name. |
| Day 3 | Follow-up WhatsApp if no response |
| Day 7 | Final follow-up. If no reply, inform referrer and move on. |

## Research Checklist (Before First Outreach)

Do this in under 10 minutes per prospect. Note findings in their CRM record.

- [ ] Find their Facebook page or Philkotse dealer profile
- [ ] Count their active listings (inventory size estimate)
- [ ] Note price range of their inventory (entry, mid, premium)
- [ ] Note their current system: Facebook-only? Philkotse? Excel? Nothing visible?
- [ ] Test their Facebook response time: send a dummy inquiry on Messenger and see how long it takes to reply (or if they reply at all)
- [ ] Note their location: single lot or multiple branches?
- [ ] Check if they have a website (rare in PH independent dealers, but notable if yes)
- [ ] Look for any reviews, complaints, or reputation signals on their page

**Record all of this in the CRM prospect record before first contact.**

## Phone Script — Cold Call Intro (30 seconds)

Use this word-for-word until you can improvise comfortably.

**Opening (5 seconds):**
"Hello, si [Name] ba ito? Hi, ito si [Your Name] mula AutoBentaPH."

**Value hook (10 seconds):**
"Nagtatayo kami ng platform para sa mga used-car dealers dito sa Pilipinas — para hindi na kayo mawalan ng leads sa Messenger, at makita ng mas maraming buyers ang mga sasakyan ninyo."

**Qualifying question (10 seconds):**
"Ilang sasakyan kayo nagbebenta sa isang buwan? At Facebook lang ba ang ginagamit ninyo ngayon para sa leads?"

**Close for demo (5 seconds):**
"Pwede ba kayong makita ng 20 minuto this week para ipakita ko ang platform? Libre, walang obligasyon."

**If they say they're busy:**
"Sige, anong best time para kayo tawagan? Mag-se-set lang ako ng reminder."

## CRM Discipline — Required Daily Actions

These are non-negotiable. Without this, your pipeline becomes noise.

**Every contact attempt must be logged:**
- Who you called/messaged, when, what was said
- Their response (or no response)
- What's the next step

**Every active prospect must have a next follow-up date set.**
If you hung up the phone and didn't set a follow-up date, you didn't finish the task.

**Stage must be updated immediately after each interaction:**
- First contact made → `contacted`
- Demo scheduled → `demo_scheduled`
- Demo done → `demo_completed`
- Proposal sent → `proposal_sent`
- Waiting on decision → `negotiating`

**7-day rule:** No prospect should sit in any stage for more than 7 days with zero activity. If 7 days pass with no action logged, either follow up today or move them to a dormant list.

**Daily minimum:**
- Morning: Review all prospects with follow-up due today. Action every single one.
- Evening: Log every interaction from the day. Set every next follow-up date.
