# Dealer Closing Playbook
## AutoBentaPH — Founding Dealer Acquisition

**Purpose:** Close Dealer #1 (and Dealers #2–5) at ₱3,599/month. This is a relationship close, not a feature sell.

**Success criteria:** Signed dealer agreement + first invoice paid.

---

## Part 1: The Psychology of the Filipino Dealer Buyer

### What Filipino dealers actually buy

Filipino dealers do not buy software. They buy:
1. **Trust in you** — they need to believe you will be there when something goes wrong
2. **Respect** — they want to feel valued, not sold to
3. **Proof** — they want to know it worked for someone like them
4. **Control** — they want to feel like they made the decision, not that they were pushed

The features are the justification. The relationship is the sale.

### What kills deals in Filipino business culture

- Moving too fast before rapport is established
- Being transactional ("So are you ready to sign?") before they feel comfortable
- Making them feel like a number, not a partner
- Disrespecting their decision-making process (family input, business partner, "kausapin ko muna ang aking asawa")
- Pushy urgency tactics that feel manipulative — they will ghost you instantly

### What accelerates deals

- Consistency: following up exactly when you said you would
- Generosity: sharing useful info even when it's not a sales touch
- Directness with warmth: "Gusto ko lang malaman kung kumusta kayo at kung may katanungan kayo"
- Social proof from someone they already know or respect
- A clear, fair deal they can explain to their family/partner

### The founder advantage

You are not a sales rep. You are the founder. Use that. It signals:
- They are getting direct access to the person who built this
- You care personally about their success
- They can call you when something is wrong
- This is a partnership, not a vendor relationship

Always say: "Ako mismo ang magsi-siguro na maayos ang lahat para sa inyo."

## Part 2: The 9-Stage Closing Pipeline

### Stage 0: Identified
**What it means:** Name is on the list. No contact yet.
**Done when:** Decision-maker's WhatsApp number confirmed and first message sent.

### Stage 1: Contacted
**What it means:** First outreach sent (WhatsApp, call, referral intro).
**Done when:** They responded — even just "sino ito?" counts.

### Stage 2: Qualified
**What it means:** Confirmed they are a used car dealer, have inventory to list, and have some openness to digital tools.
**Done when:** You know their lot size, approximate monthly sales volume, and current marketing method.

### Stage 3: Demo Booked
**What it means:** They agreed to a live demo (Zoom or in-person).
**Done when:** Date, time, and meeting link/location confirmed via WhatsApp.

### Stage 4: Demo Complete
**What it means:** Demo happened. They saw the platform.
**Done when:** Demo ended, you sent the follow-up deck + ROI summary same day.

### Stage 5: Verbal Yes
**What it means:** They said "sige, gusto ko ito" or equivalent. Not signed yet.
**Done when:** You confirmed what they said back to them: "Okay, so we're moving forward — I'll send the agreement today."

### Stage 6: Agreement Sent
**What it means:** Dealer Agreement PDF or link sent to them.
**Done when:** They confirmed they received it.

### Stage 7: Agreement Signed
**What it means:** Agreement signed and returned (PDF signature or wet signature photo).
**Done when:** You have a signed copy and sent them a confirmation.

### Stage 8: Invoice Sent
**What it means:** First invoice for ₱3,599 generated and sent.
**Done when:** They confirmed receipt of invoice.

### Stage 9: Payment Received — CLOSED
**What it means:** ₱3,599 received via GCash or bank transfer.
**Done when:** Official Receipt issued, account activated, onboarding scheduled.

## Part 3: Stage-Specific Talk Tracks

### After Demo Complete (Stage 4 → 5)

Send within 2 hours of demo ending. WhatsApp first, email as backup.

**WhatsApp message:**
```
[First name], salamat sa oras ninyo kanina! Enjoyed namin ang demo.

Ipinadala ko na sa email ang summary ng lahat ng napag-usapan namin, kasama ang ROI estimate para sa inyong dealership.

Isa pang bagay — dalawa na lang natitira sa Founding Dealer slots namin sa Metro Manila. Sa ₱3,599/buwan, nalilocked ang rate na iyon habang buhay ng subscription ninyo — kahit mag-raise kami ng presyo sa ibang bagong dealers.

Kung okay kayo na mag-move forward, pwede ko na i-send ang agreement ngayong araw din. Ano sa palagay ninyo?
```

**If they ask questions about the demo:** Answer directly and specifically. Don't redirect to "when can we sign" — let them ask until they're satisfied.

### At Verbal Yes (Stage 5 → 6)

When they say yes, resist the urge to celebrate. Stay calm and professional. Confirm and act immediately.

**What to say:**
```
Napakagandang balita! Okay, para ma-formalize natin — ipapaabot ko sa inyo ang Dealer Agreement ngayong araw din. 

Dalawang bagay lang ang kailangan:
1. I-review ang agreement (isang pahina lang ang important sections)
2. I-sign at i-send pabalik — pwedeng i-photo ang signature at WhatsApp sa akin

Pagkatapos nun, mag-ge-generate ako ng invoice at pagkabayad ninyo, i-a-activate na ang account ninyo. Kasama sa activation ang isang 60-minute onboarding call namin — ako mismo ang mag-gu-guide sa inyo.

I-send ko na ba ngayon?
```

They will almost always say yes. Send within 30 minutes.

## Part 4: Stall Patterns and How to Break Them

### "Mag-iisip muna ako" (I'll think about it)

**What it really means:** They're not sure if it will work for them, or they need permission from someone else, or they don't want to say no directly.

**Wrong response:** "Sige po, mag-follow up nalang ako bukas."

**Right response:**
```
Okay lang po iyon. Para matulungan ko kayo sa pag-iisip, may tanong lang ako — ano ang pinaka-malaking bagay na gusto ninyong maging sigurado bago kayo mag-move forward?
```

Then listen. The real objection will surface. Address that specific thing.

If they still say "mag-iisip pa," set a specific time: "Sige — kailan pwedeng makausap kayo ulit? Wednesday ng 10am okay sa inyo?"

---

### "Kailangan ko munang kausapin ang aking partner/asawa/kapareha"

**Never fight this.** Respect it. Use it.

```
Ganun talaga dapat — importante ang input ng inyong partner sa business decisions. 

Para matulungan ninyo silang mag-desisyon, pwede ba akong mag-prepare ng isang-pahina na summary para sa kanila? Kasama na ang presyo, kung ano ang kasama, at ang ROI estimate? Para hindi na kayo kailangang mag-explain ng matagal.

At kung gusto ng inyong partner na makausap din kami directly o makita ang platform, bukas kami sa iyon.
```

Then follow up: "Kailan kayo pwedeng makausap ng partner ninyo? Para alam ko kung kailan mag-fo-follow up."

---

### "Magmahal ba ito?" (Is this going to get expensive?)

This is a price anchoring concern, not a budget problem.

```
Tanong na marami ang nagtatanong, at naiintindihan ko kung bakit. Hayaan ninyong maging direkto ako:

₱3,599 bawat buwan — iyon ang Founding Dealer rate, at nakalock iyon habambuhay ng subscription ninyo. Kahit mag-dagdag kami ng features o mag-raise ng presyo para sa bagong dealers, hindi mag-babago ang rate ninyo.

Para mag-maka-sense: kung makapag-generate kayo ng isang extra sale bawat taon dahil sa platform — kahit isang kotse na ₱200,000 — ang ROI ay 46x ang subscription cost. Hindi kami nagbebenta ng software; nagbebenta kami ng leads.

Sabi ng ibang dealers, ang pinaka-malaking expense nila ngayon ay ang Facebook ads na walang tracking, walang CRM, walang sistema. Ito ang sistema.
```

---

### "Subukan ko muna libre" (Can I try it for free first?)

**Do not offer a free trial.** It signals the product isn't ready to bet on.

```
Naiintindihan ko kung saan nanggagaling ang tanong na iyon. Ang totoo, hindi kami nag-o-offer ng free trial — at may dahilan iyon.

Ang mga dealers na nag-co-commit — kahit maliit na subscription — sila ang talagang ginagamit ang platform. Sila ang nakakakita ng results. Ang mga nag-try nang libre? Karamihan ay hindi nag-se-setup ng maayos, hindi na-upload ang inventory, at pagkatapos ay nagsasabing "hindi gumana."

Pero ito ang offer namin para sa inyong peace of mind: 30 days. Kung pagkatapos ng 30 days ay hindi kayo nasiyahan sa platform at wala kayong natatanggap na leads, kausapin natin. Hindi namin gusto ang unhappy customers — mas gusto naming ayusin ang problema o ibalik ang pera kaysa panatilihin kayo sa isang bagay na hindi gumagana para sa inyo.

Iyon ang commitment namin sa inyo bilang Founding Dealer.
```

---

### "Hindi ko pa kailangan ngayon, baka next month na lang"

This is a delay, not a no. Use scarcity — but only the real kind.

```
Completely understand — timing is important.

Isang bagay lang ang gusto kong i-share: ang Founding Dealer rate na ₱3,599/buwan ay available lang habang may slots na open. Wala na kaming dalawa pagkatapos ninyo — at pagnakuha na ng ibang dealers ang remaining slots, ang susunod na tier ng pricing ay magiging ₱4,999/buwan.

Hindi ko kayo pinapressure — kung talagang hindi pa kayo handa, okay lang iyon. Pero gusto ko lang matiyak na alam ninyo na may time pressure dito na hindi ko kontrolado.

Kung mas gusto ninyong mag-move next month at tanggapin ang mas mataas na rate, naiintindihan ko rin iyon. Ano ang mas komportable sa inyo?
```

---

### "Mabagal ang internet namin / hindi kami bihasa sa technology"

This is a fear-of-failure objection, not a real technical barrier.

```
Naiintindihan ko — at iyan mismo ang dahilan kung bakit ako mismo ang mag-gu-guide sa inyo sa buong onboarding.

Hindi mo kailangang maging tech-savvy para gamitin ang AutoBentaPH. Ang pinaka-maraming ginagawa ng aming dealers araw-araw ay ito: mag-check ng leads at mag-update ng inventory. Ginagawa nila iyon sa phone. Ganoon kadali.

Para sa internet — basta may data ka, gumagana ito. Hindi ito isang mabigat na sistema.

At kung may mangyaring problema, ako mismo ang matatawagan ninyo. 4-hour response guarantee — iyon ang aming commitment bilang Founding Dealer ninyo.

Pwede bang i-try natin ngayon mismo na mag-log in? Para makita mo kung gaano kadali?
```

## Part 5: The 5-Day Urgency Sequence After Verbal Yes

The clock starts the moment they say yes. Every day without a signature is a day the deal can die.

### Day 0 (same day as Verbal Yes)
- Send agreement within 30 minutes of verbal yes
- Send WhatsApp confirming you sent it
- Follow up in 2 hours if no response: "Natanggap ba ang agreement?"

### Day 1 (if not signed)
**WhatsApp:**
```
[First name], kumusta? Nandoon ba kayo ngayon? Gusto ko lang matiyak na natanggap ninyo ang agreement na ipinadala ko kahapon. May katanungan kayo tungkol sa content?

Handa na kami i-activate ang inyong account pagkasign ninyo.
```

### Day 2 (if still not signed)
**WhatsApp (morning):**
```
[First name], good morning! 

Quick reminder lang — ang Founding Dealer agreement ay naghihintay pa rin sa inyong signature. 

May isang bagay akong gustong i-share: isang dealer sa Quezon City ang nag-activate ng account nila kahapon. Dalawa na lang talaga ang slots na natitira.

Kung may katanungan kayo sa agreement, pwede rin tayong mag-usap — call o Zoom, kahit 10 minutes lang.
```

### Day 3 (direct call)
Call them. Don't WhatsApp. Voice call.

**Opening:** "Good morning [First name], ito si [YOUR NAME] mula sa AutoBentaPH. Paumanhin sa tawag — gusto ko lang malaman kung may concerns kayo sa agreement bago kayo mag-sign."

**Listen for the real objection.** Address it. End the call with a specific ask: "So pwede ba naming asahan ang signature ninyo ngayong araw o bukas?"

### Day 4 (last attempt before formal follow-up)
**WhatsApp:**
```
[First name], huling reminder po ako bago ko i-hold ang inyong slot.

Kung may nag-bago sa inyong desisyon o kailangan ninyo ng additional information, sabihin ninyo sa akin at aayusin natin. Pero kung hindi ko marinig mula sa inyo ngayon, kailangan ko na i-release ang slot sa ibang dealer.

Nandito lang ako kung kailangan ninyo ako.
```

### Day 5 (slot release notice)
**WhatsApp:**
```
[First name], naiintindihan ko kung abalang-abala kayo. Dahil wala pa akong marinig, i-re-releasing ko na ang Founding Dealer slot at ii-offer ito sa ibang dealer.

Kung mag-babago ang isip ninyo, makikita ninyo pa rin ang platform — pero sa susunod na pricing tier na ng ₱4,999/buwan.

Salamat sa oras ninyo. Kung may magbabago, nandito lang ako.
```

Note: If they come back after Day 5, honor the ₱3,599 rate if a slot is still available. The goal is to close, not to punish.

## Part 6: The Founding Dealer Scarcity Play

You have 5 Founding Dealer slots. This is real scarcity. Use it honestly.

**Rules:**
1. Never claim fewer slots than you actually have available
2. Update the count as you close dealers (if you've closed 2, say "3 slots remaining")
3. Never fake urgency — Filipino dealers will find out and you will lose the relationship permanently

**The framing:**
```
Ang Founding Dealer program namin ay may 5 slots lang. Bakit 5? Dahil gusto naming mabigyan ng personal na atensyon ang bawat isa. Hindi kami naghahanap ng daan-daang dealers ngayong buwan — naghahanap kami ng 5 na tamang dealers na magiging bahagi ng aming success story.

Ang mga Founding Dealers ay makakakuha ng:
- ₱3,599/buwan na nakalock habang buhay ng subscription
- Direktang access sa akin bilang founder
- 4-hour response SLA (ibang dealers ay 24 hours)
- Input sa product roadmap — ang inyong feature requests ay priority

Dalawa na lang ang natitira. [Or however many are actually remaining.]
```

**When to use it:** After demo, at verbal yes, and in any follow-up. Never use it in the first outreach — it feels fake before they know the product.

## Part 7: Signature Ask Scripts

### The direct ask (use after verbal yes)
```
Okay, para ma-formalize natin ang partnership — i-send ko na ang agreement ngayon. Isang pahina lang ang kailangan ninyong basahin at pirmahan. Pwedeng i-photo ang inyong signature at i-WhatsApp sa akin, o pwede rin kayong mag-sign online sa link na ipapadala ko. Alin ang mas komportable sa inyo?
```

### The urgency ask (use on Day 2-3)
```
[First name], ang slot ninyo ay nakahold pa rin para sa inyo. Ang kailangan ko lang ay ang inyong signature sa agreement para ma-confirm natin ang partnership. Pwede ba ito ngayong araw?
```

### The concern-clearing ask (use when they have questions)
```
Naiintindihan ko. Ano ang specific na bagay sa agreement na gusto ninyong i-clarify? Para ma-address ko ito directly, at pagkatapos ay pwede na nating i-finalize.
```

### The final ask (use on Day 4)
```
Isang tanong lang — may dahilan ba kung bakit hindi pa nila na-sign ang agreement? Dahil kung may concerns kayo, gusto kong marinig ito at ayusin. At kung wala namang concerns, pwede ba nating i-wrap up ito ngayong araw?
```

## Part 8: What to Do If the Deal Dies

### Definition of "dead deal"
- No response after Day 5 urgency sequence
- Explicit "hindi na" or "hindi pa muna"
- Agreement sent but no signature after 14 days with follow-ups

### Immediate action: Mark stage and note reason
Update your CRM: record the last stage reached, the stall reason, and the date. Do not delete — these are resurrection candidates.

### The respectful close-out message
```
[First name], okay lang — naiintindihan ko na hindi pa ito ang tamang oras. Salamat sa inyong oras at sa pagbibigay ng pagkakataon na ipakita ang AutoBentaPH.

Kung magbabago ang inyong sitwasyon o gusto ninyong makita kung paano nagbago ang platform, nandito lang ako. Wala akong reklamo.

Ingat po kayo palagi.
```

### The resurrection sequence

**Day 30 re-engage:**
```
[First name], kumusta na? Si [YOUR NAME] ito mula sa AutoBentaPH.

May bago kaming feature na inilabas last week na akala ko ay interesting para sa inyong dealership — [specific feature relevant to their business]. Ayaw ko lang kayong hindi mapabalam.

Wala akong inaasahan — basta gusto ko lang ibahagi. Kumusta ang negosyo?
```

**Day 60 re-engage:**
```
[First name], kumusta ang dealership? 

Isang dealer namin sa [nearby area] ay nag-generate ng [X leads] last month gamit ang AutoBentaPH. Naalala ko kayo nung nakita ko ang kanilang results.

Kung gusto ninyong makita kung paano ito naaangkop sa inyong setup, bukas pa rin ako para sa isang quick demo or call. Walang pressure.
```

**Day 90 re-engage:**
```
[First name], third quarter na — paano ang sales ngayong Q? 

May bago kaming pricing structure — ang Founding Dealer program ay natapos na, pero nag-co-consider pa rin kami ng isang bagay para sa mga dealers na dating nag-express ng interes. Kung gusto ninyong malaman ang detalye, i-message lang ako.
```

### Rules for resurrection
- Never be resentful — they can hear it even in text
- Always bring new information or a genuine reason to re-engage
- Maximum 3 resurrection touches (30/60/90) — after that, quarterly only
- If they say "hindi talaga," respect it. Move on.

## Part 9: Closing Metrics to Track

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Demo-to-Verbal-Yes rate | >50% |
| Verbal-Yes-to-Signed rate | >80% |
| Signed-to-Paid rate | >95% |
| Average days from Demo to Paid | <10 days |
| Stall recovery rate | >30% |

If your demo-to-verbal-yes rate is below 50%, the demo needs work.
If your verbal-yes-to-signed rate is below 80%, the urgency sequence needs work.
If your signed-to-paid rate is below 95%, your payment process needs work.
