# AutoBentaPH Founding Dealer Program

## The Short Version

We're offering 5 Philippine used-car dealers the chance to join AutoBentaPH before we open to the public — at 40% off, locked for 24 months, with direct influence over the product roadmap.

This is not a discount offer. It is a partnership offer.

## Program Overview

AutoBentaPH is a production-certified dealer CRM and marketplace platform built specifically for Philippine used-car dealers. We are not a directory. We are not a classifieds site. We are an end-to-end sales operating system: inventory management, lead pipeline, buyer communication, DMS sync, analytics, and Verified listings — all in one platform.

We are acquiring our first 5 paying dealers through direct relationships rather than advertising. These 5 dealers become Founding Dealers — a permanent designation that signals trust, early adoption, and product partnership.

**What makes this different from a standard subscription:**
- Permanently lower price (₱3,599/mo vs ₱5,999/mo, locked 24 months)
- Founding Dealer badge on all listings — visible to buyers
- Named input into product roadmap (quarterly sessions, not just a feedback form)
- Priority support (4-hour SLA vs 24-hour standard)
- Direct WhatsApp line to the founding team

This offer closes when all 5 spots are filled. There is no deadline date — only the spot count.

## The 5 Spots: Why Limited, Who Qualifies, What the Urgency Is

### Why only 5

Our onboarding capacity at founding stage is intentionally limited. We want to give each founding dealer hands-on attention: a live onboarding session, a customized success plan, and weekly check-ins for the first 30 days. Scaling that to 20 dealers at launch would mean doing it poorly. We are choosing to do it extremely well for 5.

Once the founding cohort is active and we have their feedback, we will open to general availability — at standard pricing, without the founding benefits.

### Who qualifies

You are the right fit if:
- You sell used cars in the Philippines (Metro Manila priority, but all regions considered)
- You move 5–50 vehicles per month
- You are the business owner or primary decision-maker (not a hired manager)
- You currently manage inquiries through Facebook Messenger, Philkotse, or informal tools
- You do not have an existing CRM or DMS (V8Atlas users are welcome — integration is built-in)
- You are willing to give honest feedback, including when things are not working

You are not the right fit if:
- You are a large franchise group looking for enterprise features (that plan is not yet available)
- You need extensive custom development before going live
- You need white-label branding

### The urgency

There is no artificial countdown. The urgency is real: 5 spots, filled sequentially. The first dealer to sign gets 5 years of at-₱3,599 pricing (locked 24 months + existing subscriber rate protections). The fifth dealer to sign still gets the same deal, but the spot count visible on our site will show 4 taken.

When a spot is taken, we update the counter. When all 5 are filled, the Founding Dealer program closes permanently.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1: Is there really a 14-day money-back guarantee?**
Yes. If you activate, run the platform for up to 14 days, and decide it is not right for you, we refund your first month's payment in full. No paperwork, no negotiation.

**Q2: What happens when all 5 spots are filled — can I still get the platform?**
Yes. The Verified Dealer plan (₱2,999/mo) and Dealer Pro plan (₱5,999/mo) will remain available. You just will not have the Founding Dealer price lock, badge, or roadmap sessions.

**Q3: Do I have to use V8Atlas?**
No. V8Atlas DMS sync is optional. The platform works fully without it. If you already use V8Atlas, the integration eliminates double-entry entirely — but it is not a requirement.

**Q4: We already use Philkotse / Carmudi. Why switch?**
You do not have to switch — you can run AutoBentaPH alongside your existing listings. The difference is that Philkotse and Carmudi are listing sites; they have no CRM, no pipeline management, and no analytics. AutoBentaPH manages what happens after the inquiry arrives.

**Q5: We have 3 salespeople. Can they all use the platform?**
Yes. The Pro plan includes up to 5 user seats. Additional seats are ₱299/seat/month. All 3 salespeople can manage leads, update vehicle status, and view their own pipeline from mobile.

**Q6: What if I want features you do not have yet?**
Tell us. That is the point of the quarterly roadmap sessions. As a Founding Dealer, your requests carry more weight than general user submissions. If 3 of the 5 founding dealers want the same thing, it goes to the next sprint.

**Q7: Is my lead data safe?**
Yes. The platform is production-certified (90/100 security score) with PostgreSQL data isolation, encrypted credentials, and no shared data between dealerships. Your leads are yours; we do not share or monetize them.

**Q8: What if my business slows down — can I pause the subscription?**
Currently we do not offer a pause option, but you can cancel anytime with no fee. If you resubscribe within 90 days, we will honor your original Founding Dealer rate for the remainder of your lock period.
