## 8. Conversion Optimization Notes

### Urgency Elements

1. **Scarcity banner** (sticky top or above form heading):
   `Only 5 Founding Dealer spots available at ₱3,599/mo. 0 claimed. When they're gone, regular pricing applies.`
   - Update the "0 claimed" number manually as spots are taken
   - Do NOT use a countdown timer unless a real deadline exists

2. **Form submit button microcopy:**
   - Primary: `Book My Free Demo →`
   - Below button: `Founding Dealer pricing discussed at the end of your demo — only if it's a fit.`

3. **Social proof near the CTA:**
   `Join dealers across Metro Manila already on the waitlist.`

### Trust Signal Placement

- Trust signals should appear: (1) hero section, (2) directly above the form, (3) in the right panel
- Never cluster more than 3 trust signals in one place — it reads as defensive
- "Production-certified" and "Philippine dealers" are the two highest-converting trust phrases — use at least one within 200px of every CTA

### Framing Guidance

- Frame the demo as "see it on your numbers" not "let me pitch you" — dealers in PH are skeptical of software pitches
- Avoid the word "free trial" on this page — it implies effort to cancel; "free demo" is lower commitment
- The form asks for inventory volume and current tools *before* the calendar — this signals that the demo is personalized, not generic, which reduces drop-off
- Mobile: stack the right panel below the form on screens <768px; the "what to expect" list reduces abandonment when visible before submission
