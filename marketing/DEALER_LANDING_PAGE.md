## Conversion Optimization Notes

### Above the Fold (Priority Elements)
1. Headline must lead with outcome, not feature ("Sell More Cars" not "Manage Your Listings")
2. Two CTAs: primary (Book a Demo — high intent) and secondary (See Pricing — self-serve)
3. Social proof element in hero: dealer count or cars sold figure, even if small ("Trusted by 25+ dealers across Metro Manila")
4. No nav distraction — sticky header with just logo + "Book Demo" button
5. Hero image: real dashboard screenshot, not illustration — builds credibility

### Trust Signals (Use Throughout Page)
- LTO OR/CR verification badge graphic
- V8Atlas trust score example (show the score widget)
- Founding Dealer limited-spots counter
- Money-back guarantee badge ("14-day money-back guarantee")
- Named dealer testimonials with dealership name and location (even 2-3 real ones are powerful)
- Security badge: "Your data is protected" (SOC2/HTTPS/Render hosting)

### Urgency Tactics
- Founding Dealer: "5 spots. 2 remaining." — update dynamically or manually
- Founding Dealer section above pricing, not after
- Countdown timer on Founding Dealer offer (optional, if there's a hard deadline)
- CTA copy: "Claim Your Founding Spot" vs generic "Sign Up"

### Conversion Flow
- Primary path: Hero CTA → Book Demo → Sales call
- Secondary path: Pricing section → Start Free Trial → Self-serve onboarding
- Tertiary path: ROI Calculator → See result → Upgrade prompt

## Section 4: Feature Deep-Dives

### 4a. CRM Deep-Dive

**Headline**: Your Entire Lead Pipeline, in One Place

**Subheadline**: The 8-stage CRM built for how Philippine car dealers actually sell

**Body Copy**:
AutoBentaPH's CRM mirrors your real sales process. Every lead that comes in — from AutoBentaPH listings, your own referrals, or imported contacts — lands in your pipeline and moves through eight clear stages:

1. **New** — fresh inquiry, not yet contacted
2. **Contacted** — you've reached out, waiting for response
3. **Qualified** — buyer confirmed budget and intent
4. **Test Drive Scheduled** — commitment secured
5. **Negotiating** — price discussion active
6. **Closed Won** — deal done, unit sold
7. **Closed Lost** — deal fell through (captured for follow-up later)
8. **On Hold** — buyer paused (financing, waiting for a specific unit)

Built-in follow-up reminders, lead notes, and activity logs mean nothing falls through. When a "Closed Lost" buyer is ready six months later, you'll have the full history.

### 4c. Analytics Deep-Dive

**Headline**: The Dealer Scorecard That Shows You Exactly Where You Stand

**Subheadline**: Stop guessing. Start knowing.

**Body Copy**:
AutoBentaPH gives every dealer a weekly scorecard benchmarked against dealers in their category (by volume, price segment, and region). Your grade — A, B, C, or D — reflects your listing quality, response rate, conversion performance, and buyer satisfaction signals.

Beyond the scorecard, the analytics suite shows:

- **Lead Velocity**: how fast leads are entering your pipeline vs. previous weeks
- **Conversion Funnel**: where leads drop off (Contacted → Qualified is often the biggest leak)
- **Source Attribution**: which listing, channel, or referral source is closing best
- **Inventory Turn Rate**: how long units sit before selling — critical for cash flow planning
- **Revenue Projection**: forward-looking estimates based on current pipeline

The biggest barrier to closing a used car sale in the Philippines is trust. Buyers have been burned. They've seen tampered odometers, hidden flood damage, unclear titles. They lowball everyone because they assume something is wrong.

AutoBentaPH's verification system breaks that cycle.
