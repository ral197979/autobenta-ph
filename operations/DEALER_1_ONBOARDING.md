## Welcome Call Agenda (60 Minutes)

### 0–5 min: Welcome and orientation
```
"[First name], welcome sa AutoBentaPH! Excited na kaming makapagtrabaho kasama kayo. Ikaw ang isa sa aming 5 Founding Dealers — iyon ay espesyal.

Ang gagawin natin ngayon sa susunod na isang oras: ise-set up natin ang inyong account nang kompleto — profile, inventory, CRM — at sa katapusan, live na kayo at makikita na ng mga buyers.

Okay tayo sa oras?"
```

### 5–15 min: Platform orientation (screen share — your screen)
Walk them through the platform using your screen first so they can see the "finished state" before touching their own.

Tour stops:
1. **Dealer Dashboard** — "Ito ang makikita ninyo every time mag-lo-log in kayo. Dito ang mga leads, inventory summary, at analytics."
2. **Leads Inbox** — "Dito darating ang lahat ng inquiries mula sa buyers. Makikita ninyo ang pangalan, contact number, at kung anong listing ang tinanong nila."
3. **Inventory Manager** — "Dito kayo magma-manage ng listings — mag-add, mag-edit, mag-archive."
4. **CRM** — "Dito kayo mag-ta-track ng leads mula New → Contacted → Qualified → Closed. Ito ang puso ng sistema."
5. **Analytics** — "Dito makikita ninyo kung gaano karaming tao ang tumingin sa bawat listing at saan sila nanggaling."

### 15–25 min: Dealer profile setup (switch to their screen share)
Have them share their screen and you guide them.

- Business name (exactly as it appears on their BIR registration or Facebook page)
- Business address
- Contact number (the one buyers should call)
- Logo upload (if they have it — JPEG or PNG, at least 200×200px)
- Business hours
- Profile tagline: "Tungkol saan ang inyong dealership — one sentence: 'Quality used cars in [area], all units fully inspected and documented.'"

### 25–40 min: First inventory upload (3 units together)
Guide them through adding their first 3 listings live.

For each listing:
1. Click "Add New Listing"
2. **Photos first** — "Ang photos ay ang pinaka-importanteng bahagi. Minimum 8 photos per unit: front, rear, both sides, interior front, interior rear, odometer, engine bay. Ang malinis na background at natural light ay nagpapataas ng views ng 40%."
3. **Make/Model/Year** — specific: "Toyota Vios 2019 1.3E MT" not just "Toyota Vios"
4. **Price** — their asking price. If they're unsure: "Pwedeng lagyan ng 'Negotiable' sa description — pero lagyan pa rin ng suggested price para makita ng buyers."
5. **Mileage, fuel type, transmission, color, condition**
6. **Description** — guide them: "Key accessories, any warranty remaining, service history, reason for selling (if fleet/consignment). The more information, the more trust."
7. **Location** (their lot location)
8. Click Publish — confirm it's live

After the third listing: "Tatlong listings na kayo ngayon — live na! Pwede na kayong makita ng buyers ngayon."

### 40–50 min: CRM walkthrough
Show them how to respond to a lead using the demo account as reference.

Steps:
1. New lead arrives in inbox
2. Click to open — see buyer contact info and inquiry
3. Contact buyer via WhatsApp or phone (outside the platform initially)
4. Update CRM stage: drag or click to move from "New" → "Contacted"
5. Add a note: "Called buyer — interested in red Vios. Meeting bukas 2pm."
6. Schedule follow-up reminder

Key message: "The goal is never to have a lead sitting in 'New' for more than 4 hours. That's the Founding Dealer SLA — 4-hour response window, Monday to Saturday, 8am–6pm."

### 50–55 min: Notification setup and daily routine
- Confirm email notifications are on (new leads, views on listings)
- Optional: set up a WhatsApp forwarding workflow if they want alerts there too
- Daily routine recommendation: "5–10 minutes every morning — check leads, check new views, update any inventory. That's it. Hanggang 10 minutes bawat araw."

### 55–60 min: Schedule next touchpoints and close
```
"Okay — you're live! Lahat ay naka-set up na.

Sa susunod na ilang araw, mag-upload kayo ng buong inventory. Target: 10 listings by [Day 10 date].

May dalawa pa tayong touchpoints ngayong buwan:
1. Day 14 Lead Review — [date, time] — 30 minutes via Zoom
2. Day 30 Month 1 Success Review — [date, time] — 30 minutes

I-send ko ang calendar invites ngayon. Kung may tanong kayo kahit kelan — WhatsApp ako sa [YOUR WHATSAPP]. Ang guaranteed response ko ay 4 hours, but usually much faster.

May tanong pa bago tayo mag-end?"
```

**Message:**
```
[First name], kumusta na ang platform? Ilang listings na ang naka-upload?

Kung may katanungan kayo sa photo upload, description writing, o pricing — nandito lang ako. Pwede ring mag-quick screen share kung mas madali.

Target natin: 10 listings by [Day 10 date]. Handa na kayo? 💪
```

### What questions to expect and how to answer them

**"Di ko alam kung paano mag-upload ng litrato"**
```
Walang problema — simple lang ito. Dalawang paraan:

Option 1 — Phone: i-open ang AutoBentaPH sa browser ng inyong phone, pumunta sa "Add Listing," at i-tap ang camera icon. Pwede kayong mag-take ng picture directly o mag-choose mula sa gallery.

Option 2 — Computer: i-drag and drop ang mga photos mula sa folder ninyo papunta sa upload area.

Kung gusto ninyo, mag-quick screen share tayo ngayon — 10 minutes lang at tapos na kayong lahat. Kailan kayo free?
```

**"Magandang presyo ba ang aking nalagay?"**
```
Depende sa unit at kondisyon. Para sa reference:
- Toyota Vios 2019: ₱650K–₱750K (good condition)
- Honda Civic 2018: ₱800K–₱950K (good condition)
- SUVs ay nagbabago ng malaki depende sa brand at mileage

I-check din ang inyong competition sa platform — tignan kung ano ang presyo ng katulad na units. Gusto ko kayong competitive pero hindi pinakamababa — quality buyers ay hindi laging naghahanap ng pinakamura.
```

### If 0 leads by Day 7 — diagnosis checklist:
- [ ] Are photos high quality? (Minimum 8 per unit, natural light, no clutter)
- [ ] Are titles specific? ("Toyota Vios 2019 1.3E MT Grab-ready" not "Toyota Vios for sale")
- [ ] Are prices within market range? (Too high = no clicks; too low = suspicious)
- [ ] Are listings categorized correctly? (Sedan, SUV, etc.)
- [ ] Is the location set correctly? (Buyers filter by location)

## Common Onboarding Problems and Fixes

### "Di ko alam kung paano mag-upload ng litrato"
**Root cause:** Not technical — they haven't done it before and feel uncertain.
**Fix:** 10-minute screen share. Don't explain — do it together. Say "Sama-sama nating gawin ngayon."

### "Wala pa akong leads" (after Day 14)
**Root cause:** Usually one of: (a) photos are poor quality, (b) pricing is out of range, (c) titles are too generic, (d) inventory count is too low.
**Fix protocol:**
1. Check listing views (not leads — views tell you if buyers are seeing the listing)
2. If high views, low leads: price or description problem
3. If low views: title or category problem
4. Share 3 specific improvements they can make today
5. Set a 3-day check-in to see if views/leads improve

### "Hindi ko naiintindihan ang CRM"
**Root cause:** The concept of a CRM is abstract to dealers who track everything via text messages and a notebook.
**Fix:** Reframe in their language:
```
"Isipin ninyo ang CRM bilang inyong digital notebook. Bawat lead ay isang buyer — at ang CRM ang nagtatago ng history ng bawat nag-inquire.

'New' = 'Bagong mensahe, hindi pa nasasagot'
'Contacted' = 'Tinawagan/na-message na'
'Qualified' = 'Interested, nagtatanong pa ng details'
'Closed Won' = 'Nabenta!'
'Closed Lost' = 'Sumuko na ang buyer o hindi nagpatuloy'

Iyon lang. Bawat umaga, tignan ang 'New' — iyon ang priority."
```
