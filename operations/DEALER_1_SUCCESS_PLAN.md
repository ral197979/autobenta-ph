# Dealer #1 Success Plan
## AutoBentaPH — 30-Day Success Framework

**Purpose:** Define what success looks like for Dealer #1 in their first 30 days, track actuals weekly, and intervene before issues become churn risks.

---

## The Definition of Success at Day 30

Dealer #1 is successful at Day 30 if:
1. They have 10+ active listings on the platform
2. They have received at least 3 leads
3. They have responded to at least 80% of leads within 4 hours
4. They have moved at least 1 lead through a CRM stage transition
5. They have expressed satisfaction or neutrality (NPS 7+)
6. **They renew for Month 2**

If all 6 are true, Dealer #1 is a success story. If fewer than 4 are true, intervention is needed before Day 30.

### If 0 leads by Day 14
This is a listing quality problem, not a platform problem. Run the diagnosis:
1. Check listing views — are buyers seeing the listings?
2. If views are low: title and photo quality fix needed
3. If views are okay but no inquiries: price or description needs adjustment
4. Schedule an emergency 20-minute listing optimization session

Do not tell the dealer "mabagal lang ang merkado" — diagnose the actual problem and fix it.

## The Baseline Capture (Critical — Do This on Day 0)

Before the platform does anything for this dealer, record their "before" state. This is the foundation of the eventual case study.

**Questions to ask on the onboarding call:**
1. "How many leads are you receiving per month now, before AutoBentaPH?"
2. "How long does it take you to respond to an inquiry — usually?"
3. "How do you currently track your leads? Notebook, Excel, text messages?"
4. "What percentage of your inquiries actually turn into sales?"
5. "What is your primary marketing channel right now?"

**Record their answers here:**

|-----------------|-------------------|
| Monthly leads | ___ |
| Avg response time | ___ |
| Lead tracking method | ___ |
| Lead-to-sale rate | ___ |
| Primary marketing channel | ___ |

These numbers become the "before" in the case study. The Day 30 and Month 3 numbers become the "after."

## The Day 30 Narrative

At the Day 30 review, tell them their story back to them. Something like:

"30 days na kayo sa AutoBentaPH. Bago kayo sumali, [X leads/month sa Facebook]. Ngayon, [X leads] na kayo sa unang buwan pa lang — na may tracking, may history, may sistema. Ang average response time ninyo ay [X hours], which meets your Founding Dealer SLA.

Iyon ang concrete na pagbabago sa loob ng isang buwan. Imagine kung ano ang mangyayari sa 3 months, 6 months."

This is a renewal close disguised as a celebration. It works because it's true.
