# Dealer #1 Renewal Plan
## AutoBentaPH — Month-by-Month Renewal Calendar

**Purpose:** Ensure Dealer #1 never unexpectedly churns. Every renewal is managed proactively, with the next renewal already confirmed before the current billing period ends.

**Goal:** Dealer #1 at ₱3,599/mo → ₱5,097/mo by Month 6 (base + featured + extra seat).

---

## Part 1: The Billing Cycle

The billing cycle repeats every month on the same schedule:

| Day | Event | Action |
|-----|-------|--------|
| Day 20 | Pre-renewal check-in call | Call dealer to confirm satisfaction, surface issues |
| Day 25 | Invoice generated and sent | Email + WhatsApp |
| Day 27 | Follow-up if not paid | WhatsApp reminder |
| Day 30 | Payment due date | Monitor for payment |
| Day 32 | Grace period notice | If unpaid: WhatsApp with grace notice |
| Day 37 | Grace period ends | If unpaid: account suspended |

"Day X" is relative to the subscription start date, not the calendar month. If Dealer #1 activated on June 1, then Day 25 = June 25, Day 30 = June 30, etc.

For subsequent months, the cycle resets: "Day 25" = the 25th of each month.

## Part 2: The Day 20 Pre-Renewal Check-In Call

### Purpose
This call serves three goals:
1. Confirm the dealer is happy before the invoice arrives
2. Identify any concerns that could block renewal
3. Set the stage for a smooth payment

### Duration: 10–15 minutes (phone or Zoom)

### Script
```
"[First name], kumusta! Si [YOUR NAME] ito. Kumusta ang dealership ngayong buwan?

[Let them talk 2–3 minutes]

Okay — malapit na ang renewal invoice ninyo sa [Day 25]. Bago ko ipadala, gusto ko munang matiyak na okay ang lahat at walang concerns na kailangan nating ayusin.

Kumusta ang leads? May nangyari bang bagay na hindi gaya ng inaasahan?

[Address any concerns. If none:]

Maganda! I-se-send ko ang invoice sa [Day 25] — ₱3,599 lang, same terms. Kung may katanungan kayo, sabi lang. Salamat sa inyong oras!"
```

### If they raise a concern during this call
- Treat it as urgent. If it's a platform problem, fix it before Day 25.
- If it's a "value" concern: walk through their metrics with them on the call.
- Do not send the invoice until the concern is addressed.

## Part 5: Renewal Success Review Metrics

At each renewal milestone, share these numbers:

### Month 1 → Month 2 renewal metrics
- Total leads received in Month 1
- Lead response rate vs. their baseline (before AutoBentaPH)
- Listings published
- CRM stages movement
- Key win to celebrate

**Example framing:**
```
"Sa inyong unang buwan, nakatanggap kayo ng [X] leads — ito ay [X]x ng dati kayong natatanggap sa Facebook. At ang inyong response rate ay [X]%, which is above the platform average ng [X]%.

Iyon ang unang buwan pa lang. Ang subscription ninyo ay ₱3,599 — at ito na ang nagdadala ng mga buyers na aktibong naghahanap ng kotse direkta sa inyong listings.

I-renew ba natin?"
```

### Month 3 renewal metrics
Add to the above:
- 3-month cumulative leads
- Any sales that can be attributed to platform leads
- Improvement in response time vs. baseline
- CRM pipeline value (total value of vehicles in Qualified stage)

### Month 6 renewal metrics
Add:
- 6-month cumulative data
- Estimated revenue influenced (pipeline conversions × avg sale price)
- Comparison vs. cost (₱3,599 × 6 = ₱21,594 total paid; estimated leads × conversion × avg margin)
- Introduce expansion offer

### Month 12 renewal metrics
Full annual review:
- Total leads in 12 months
- Estimated revenue from platform leads
- Testimonial (if not collected yet — this is the best time)
- Annual plan offer (if you're offering annual billing by then)

## Part 6: Expansion Offer — Month 3

### Featured Listings (₱999/month)

**Trigger conditions:**
- Health score 75+
- At least one expressed satisfaction moment
- No unresolved complaints
- Dealer has been responding to leads consistently

**Timing:** Bring up during Month 3 renewal call or success review.

**Framing:**
```
"[First name], may gusto akong ipag-alok sa inyo ngayon — opsyonal ito, walang pressure.

Ang aming Featured Listings add-on ay nagbibigay sa inyo ng 5 pinned listings bawat buwan. Ang mga featured listings ay nagpapakita sa tuktok ng search results at may espesyal na badge na nagpapahiwatig sa buyers na premium ang listing.

Sa aming data, ang featured listings ay nakakakuha ng 3x mas maraming views kaysa regular listings — at mas mataas ang click-through rate.
```

## Part 8: At-Risk Renewal Protocol

If you arrive at Day 20 and the dealer is showing any of these signs:
- Health score below 50
- No login in 7+ days
- Unpaid invoice from prior month
- Expressed frustration that was not resolved

**Do not send the invoice on Day 25 until you've had a save conversation.**

Use the save call script from `sales/CHURN_PREVENTION_PLAYBOOK.md` before proceeding with the renewal cycle.
