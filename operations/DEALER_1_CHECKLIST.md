# Dealer #1 Checklist
## AutoBentaPH — Demo Booked to Go Live

**Purpose:** Day-by-day binary checklist from the moment the demo is booked through the first 30 days of operation.

**How to use:** Every item is either ✅ Done or ⬜ Pending. Nothing moves forward until the prior stage is complete.

---

## Pre-Demo (24 Hours Before Demo Day)

⬜ Research the dealer: Google their business name, find their Facebook page, note their inventory size, price range, and any visible pain points (slow response, no website, FB-only presence)

⬜ Prepare the demo account: Log in to the demo dealer account, ensure it has at least 5 sample listings with photos, prices, and descriptions loaded

⬜ Test screen share on Zoom/Google Meet — audio, video, screen share all working

⬜ Confirm demo appointment via WhatsApp the morning of the day before: "Kumusta [First name]! Reminder lang — meeting tayo bukas sa [time]. Heto ang link: [Zoom link]. See you then!"

⬜ Print or have ready: the Founding Dealer one-pager PDF (pricing, what's included, scarcity — 5 slots only)

---

## Demo Day

⬜ Log into demo account 15 minutes before the call — have the listings page and CRM dashboard visible

⬜ Run the demo in this exact order:
1. Marketplace view (what buyers see — show a sample listing)
2. Dealer dashboard (lead inbox, inventory manager)
3. CRM (show a lead moving from New → Contacted → Qualified)
4. Analytics (show listing views, lead source breakdown)
5. Mobile experience (show it works on phone)

⬜ Follow the Demo Script from `sales/DEALER_DEMO_SCRIPT.md` — no winging it

⬜ Ask qualification questions during the demo:
- "Ilang kotse ang nasa inventory ninyo ngayon?"
- "Paano kayo nag-ge-generate ng leads ngayon?"
- "Gaano katagal ang average response time ninyo sa inquiries?"

⬜ Close the demo with: "Ano ang pinaka-interesting sa inyo sa nakita ninyo ngayon?"

⬜ Make the ask: "Dalawa na lang ang Founding Dealer slots namin. Gusto ninyong mag-move forward ngayon?"

---

## Post-Demo (Same Day as Demo)

⬜ Send the ROI summary email within 2 hours of demo ending (template in `sales/PROPOSAL_TEMPLATE.md`)

⬜ Send WhatsApp follow-up within 30 minutes of demo: "Salamat sa oras ninyo kanina! Napadala ko na ang summary sa email. May katanungan kayo?"

⬜ Update CRM stage: Move dealer from "Demo Scheduled" to "Demo Complete"

⬜ Log demo notes: what they said, what interested them, what objections they raised

---

## Day 2 (Follow-up)

⬜ Send Day 1 follow-up WhatsApp (see `DEALER_CLOSING_PLAYBOOK.md` — Day 1 urgency sequence):
"[First name], kumusta? Nandoon ba kayo ngayon? Gusto ko lang matiyak na natanggap ninyo ang email ko. May katanungan kayo?"

⬜ If verbal yes was given during demo: Agreement should already be sent. Check if signed.

⬜ If no verbal yes: Note the objection from the demo and prepare a tailored response

---

## Day 3 (Verbal Yes → Agreement Sent)

⬜ Upon verbal yes: Send Dealer Agreement PDF via email within 30 minutes

⬜ WhatsApp confirmation: "I-send ko na ang agreement sa email ninyo — isang pahina lang ang kailangan ninyong basahin at pirmahan. Pwedeng i-photo ang signature at i-WhatsApp sa akin."

⬜ Update CRM: Stage = "Agreement Sent"

⬜ Set reminder for Day 4 follow-up if agreement not returned

---

## Day 5 (Agreement Signed → Invoice)

⬜ Receive signed agreement (photo or PDF)

⬜ Save signed agreement: `agreements/DealerName-Agreement-Signed-[YYYY-MM-DD].pdf`

⬜ Send confirmation WhatsApp: "Natanggap ko na ang signed agreement — maraming salamat! I-generate ko na ang invoice ngayon."

⬜ Generate invoice within 2 hours of receiving signed agreement (template in `operations/DEALER_1_PAYMENT_FLOW.md`)

⬜ Send invoice via email with WhatsApp follow-up

⬜ Update CRM: Stage = "Invoice Sent"

---

## Day 7 (Payment Received → Account Activated)

⬜ Confirm payment received (GCash screenshot or bank transfer confirmation)

⬜ Send immediate WhatsApp confirmation: "Natanggap! Salamat [First name] — confirmed ang ₱3,599."

⬜ Activate dealer account in admin panel (set account status = Active, set tier = Founding Dealer)

⬜ Set Founding Dealer flag in database (rate lock confirmed)

⬜ Issue Official Receipt — log in accounting spreadsheet

⬜ Send OR via email within same business day if possible

⬜ Schedule Welcome/Onboarding Call (60 minutes) — offer 2 time slots: "Pwedeng bukas ng 10am o 2pm — alin ang mas komportable sa inyo?"

⬜ Update CRM: Stage = "Payment Received — Active"

⬜ Send Welcome WhatsApp:
"[First name], welcome sa AutoBentaPH Founding Dealer family! Ang account ninyo ay active na. Mag-se-schedule tayo ng 60-minute onboarding call — ako mismo ang mag-gu-guide sa inyo sa lahat. Kailan kayo available?"

---

## Day 8 (Onboarding Day 0 — 60-Min Welcome Call)

⬜ 15 min before call: confirm via WhatsApp "Kita-kita tayo sa [time] — heto ang Zoom link: [link]"

⬜ Run the full onboarding call agenda (see `operations/DEALER_1_ONBOARDING.md`)

⬜ During call — complete these with the dealer:
- [ ] Dealer profile fully set up (business name, address, contact info, logo uploaded)
- [ ] At least 3 inventory items added during the call (with photos)
- [ ] CRM walkthrough completed
- [ ] Notification settings confirmed (how they want to be alerted on new leads)
- [ ] Mobile app or mobile browser tested on their device

⬜ End call with: "Okay — you're live! Ang unang lead review natin ay [Day 14 date]. I-send ko ang calendar invite ngayon."

⬜ Send Day 0 recap email within 2 hours of call

---

## Day 9 (Inventory Import Support)

⬜ Send WhatsApp check-in: "Kumusta ang pag-upload ng inventory? Nandito lang ako kung may katanungan."

⬜ If they need help: offer a 20-minute screen share session to assist with bulk uploads

⬜ Target: 10+ listings live by end of Day 10

⬜ Verify in admin that listings are live and correctly formatted (photos, price, description, condition)

---

## Day 10 (First Listing Live — Celebration)

⬜ Check admin dashboard: confirm at least 1 listing is live and visible to buyers

⬜ Send celebration WhatsApp:
"[First name]! Nakita ko na live na ang inyong listings sa AutoBentaPH — congratulations! Pwede na kayong makita ng mga buyers sa Metro Manila. Excited na akong makita ang unang lead ninyo."

⬜ Screenshot their live listing and send to them: "Ganito kayo makikita ng mga buyers!"

⬜ Verify SEO: listing appears in search within the platform for the vehicle type/brand

---

## Day 14 (First Lead Review Call — 30 Minutes)

⬜ Schedule confirmed (should be set during Day 8 call)

⬜ Pull pre-call data: leads received, response rate, inventory views

⬜ Run 30-min lead review (see `operations/DEALER_1_ONBOARDING.md` — Day 14 section)

⬜ Celebrate any leads received (even 1 is a win to celebrate)

⬜ If 0 leads: diagnose together — listing quality, photo quality, pricing, category

⬜ Set Day 21 check-in

---

## Day 21 (3-Week Check-in — 15 Minutes)

⬜ WhatsApp or quick call

⬜ Check: any sales or near-sales from platform leads?

⬜ Check: CRM stages being used? Any leads moved to Qualified or beyond?

⬜ Note any feature requests or frustrations for your backlog

⬜ Confirm Day 30 success review is on the calendar

---

## Day 30 (Month 1 Success Review — 30 Minutes)

⬜ Run full success review using `sales/CUSTOMER_SUCCESS_REVIEW_TEMPLATE.md`

⬜ Present Month 1 metrics: leads, response rate, listings, CRM activity

⬜ Ask the 5 review questions

⬜ Collect NPS score

⬜ Begin renewal conversation (invoice coming Day 25 of next month cycle)

⬜ Schedule Month 2 review

⬜ Update dealer health score

⬜ If NPS 9–10: ask for testimonial quote
