# Dealer #1 Case Study Plan
## AutoBentaPH — Capturing and Leveraging the First Success Story

**Purpose:** Systematically capture Dealer #1's transformation story so it becomes the primary sales asset for acquiring Dealers #2–50.

**Core insight:** Dealers buy from other dealers. A real story from a real dealer in their city is worth more than any feature sheet or pricing deck.

---

## Part 1: When to Start Capturing Data

**Start on Day 0 — the onboarding call.**

Do not wait for success to start documenting. You need the "before" numbers first, or the "after" numbers have no context.

The story structure is:
```
[Name] ran [dealership type] in [area]. Before AutoBentaPH, they were [old process]. In [timeframe], they [specific result].
```

You can only fill in "before" if you captured it before the platform did anything.

## Part 4: Month 3 Case Study Interview

### When to conduct
At the Day 90 (Month 3) success review — or as a dedicated 30-minute conversation separate from the review.

### Pre-interview conditions
Only conduct the interview if:
- [ ] Health score 65+
- [ ] No unresolved complaints
- [ ] They have expressed satisfaction (verbally or NPS 7+)
- [ ] They have at least one concrete result to share

### Interview format
Conversational, not formal. WhatsApp voice note or Zoom recording (with permission).

**Permission script:**
```
"[First name], tatlong buwan na tayo — gusto ko sana i-document ang inyong experience para matulungan kaming ipakita sa ibang dealers kung paano gumagana ang AutoBentaPH.

Pwede ba akong magtanong ng ilang bagay tungkol sa inyong experience? Ang inyong sagot ay pwedeng gamitin sa aming website at sa conversations namin sa ibang dealers — pero hihingiin ko muna ang inyong pahintulot bago kami mag-publish ng kahit ano.

Okay ba iyon sa inyo?"
```

---

### The 10 Interview Questions

Ask these in order. Let them answer in their own words — don't script their answers.

1. "Bago kayo sumali sa AutoBentaPH, paano kayo nag-ge-generate ng leads? Ano ang proseso?"

2. "Ano ang pinaka-malaking frustration ninyo sa dati ninyong paraan ng pagkuha ng leads?"

3. "Naalala ninyo ang unang lead na natanggap ninyo sa AutoBentaPH? Ano ang pakiramdam noon?"

4. "Sa loob ng 3 buwan, magkano ang pagbabago sa dami ng leads na natanggap ninyo?"

5. "Nagbago ba ang paraan ninyo ng pagte-track ng leads? Paano?"

6. "May naging sale ba na masasabi ninyong 'dahil sa AutoBentaPH iyon'? Maikukwento ba ninyo?"

7. "Ano ang mga bagay na mas mahirap kaysa inaasahan ninyo? (Honesty is important — we use this to improve)"

8. "Kung kausapin ninyo ang ibang dealer na hindi pa sumubok ng AutoBentaPH, ano ang sasabihin ninyo sa kanila?"

9. "Sa scale ng 1–10, gaano ninyo ire-rekomenda ang AutoBentaPH sa ibang dealers? Bakit iyon ang number?"

10. "Kung may isang bagay kayong masasabi tungkol sa inyong experience — isang sentence lang — ano iyon?"

## Part 5: Case Study Format

Use this template when writing up the case study for publication.

```
---
CASE STUDY: [Dealer Business Name]
[Area], Metro Manila
---

THE CHALLENGE
[2–3 sentences describing what the dealer was struggling with before AutoBentaPH.
Use their exact words where possible.]

Example: "[Name] has been selling used cars in [area] for [X] years. Before AutoBentaPH,
his leads came primarily from Facebook posts and walk-in customers. 'Hindi ko alam
kung gaano karaming tao ang nakakakita ng aking inventory,' he says."

---

THE SOLUTION
[2–3 sentences describing what they did with AutoBentaPH — keep it simple,
focus on what they actually used, not the feature list.]

Example: "After joining as an AutoBentaPH Founding Dealer, [name] published [X]
listings in his first week. He set up the CRM to track every buyer inquiry and
respond within 4 hours."

---

THE RESULTS (After [X] Months)
• Leads per month: [before] → [after]
• Lead response time: [before] → [after]
• Listings published: [number]
• [Any specific sale or win: "Nabenta ang [vehicle] sa isang buyer na nagfind ng listing sa AutoBentaPH"]

---

IN THEIR WORDS
"[Direct quote from interview question 10 or the strongest quote from the interview]"
— [First Name], [Business Name], [Area]

---
```

## Part 7: Testimonial Collection Script

Use after the Month 3 interview or when NPS is 9+.

```
"[First name], napakaraming salamat sa inyong honest na feedback — napakahalaga niyon sa amin.

Isang favor lang — pwede ba kaming gumamit ng inyong pangalan at isang sentence na sinabi ninyo para sa aming website? Hindi namin ipo-post ang buong interview — isa lang itong quote na katulad ng:

'[Testimonial quote]' — [First Name], [Business Name]

Pwedeng i-review ninyo muna ang eksaktong text bago namin i-publish. Okay ba iyon sa inyo?"
```

If they agree: send them a draft quote via WhatsApp for their review and approval before publishing anywhere.

## Part 8: Video Testimonial (Optional — Month 3+)

Only request if:
- NPS is 9+
- They have been consistently positive
- They are the type who is comfortable on camera (gauge from your interactions)

**Request:**
```
"[First name], may isa pang request — optional talaga ito. Gusto sana naming gumawa ng isang maikling video ng inyong experience. Hindi ito malaking production — pwedeng recorded Zoom lang o selfie video gamit ang inyong phone.

30 seconds lang ang kailangan: kung sino kayo, paano ninyo ginagamit ang AutoBentaPH, at isang resulta na nasasabi ninyo.

Interesado ba kayo? Walang pressure — kung hindi, okay lang, ang text testimonial ay sapat na."
```

If yes: schedule a 30-minute Zoom, do a casual recorded conversation, and clip the best 30-second segment.
