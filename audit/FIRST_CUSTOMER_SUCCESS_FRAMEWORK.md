# First Customer Success Framework
## AutoBentaPH — Dealer #1 Success Definition and Measurement

**Document type:** Audit / Strategic Framework
**Scope:** Definition of success, 4-phase model, metrics per phase, success score, case study readiness

---

## 1. Definition of "Dealer #1 Success"

Dealer #1 is not just the first paying customer. Dealer #1 is the proof-of-concept that makes everything else possible — the next 49 dealers, investor conversations, and the entire value proposition of AutoBentaPH.

**Dealer #1 is successful when all 5 conditions are true:**

1. **Active** — Logged in at least 3 times per week for 60+ consecutive days
2. **Responding to leads** — 80%+ lead response rate, consistently
3. **Renewing** — Paid Month 2 and Month 3 without requiring an escalation
4. **Willing to give a testimonial** — NPS 8+ and has agreed to be quoted
5. **Has a measurable result** — Can point to at least one thing that changed because of the platform (leads increased, response time improved, a sale happened, CRM replaced a notebook)

If all 5 are true, Dealer #1 is a case study. If fewer than 3 are true at Day 90, the product-market fit question is still open.

## 2. The 4 Phases

### Phase 1: Closing (Day 0 → Day 7)
**Objective:** Convert a qualified prospect into a signed, paying dealer.

**Starts when:** Prospect is in Stage 4 (Demo Complete).
**Ends when:** Payment received, account activated.

**Key activities:**
- Demo follow-up and objection handling
- Agreement signing
- Invoice and payment collection

**Success metric:** Signed agreement + ₱3,599 received within 10 days of demo.

**Failure mode:** Prospect goes cold after demo. Most deals are won or lost in the 72 hours after the demo.

---

### Phase 2: Onboarding (Day 7 → Day 30)
**Objective:** Get the dealer to their first tangible result on the platform before Day 30.

**Starts when:** Account is activated.
**Ends when:** Month 1 success review is complete (Day 30).

**Key activities:**
- Welcome call (Day 8)
- Inventory import (Day 8–10)
- First listing live (Day 10)
- First lead received (Day 14)
- Lead review call (Day 14)
- Success review (Day 30)

**Success metric:**
- 10+ listings published
- 3+ leads received
- 80%+ lead response rate
- CRM stages used

**Failure mode:** Dealer uploads a few listings and never returns. The Day 14 lead review call is the highest-leverage intervention point.

---

### Phase 3: Adoption (Day 30 → Day 90)
**Objective:** Platform becomes part of the dealer's daily workflow. Results are consistent and attributable.

**Starts when:** Month 1 success review complete.
**Ends when:** Month 3 renewal confirmed.

**Key activities:**
- Monthly success reviews (Day 60, Day 90)
- Proactive health score monitoring
- ChurnRisk intervention if triggered
- Renewal invoices (Month 2, Month 3)
- Expansion conversation at Month 3

**Success metric:**
- Health score 70+ for 60+ consecutive days
- Lead volume growing or stable month-over-month
- Month 3 renewal confirmed without escalation
- At least one expansion conversation initiated

**Failure mode:** Dealer uses the platform but doesn't see a clear ROI — "walang leads" or "hindi ko na-close ang anumang deal." Usually a listing quality or follow-up process problem.

---

### Phase 4: Renewal (Day 90+)
**Objective:** Dealer renews indefinitely. Revenue is predictable. Dealer is a reference customer.

**Starts when:** Month 3 renewal is confirmed.
**Ends when:** Dealer churns (not the goal) or reaches Year 1 milestone.

**Key activities:**
- Monthly billing cycle
- Quarterly success reviews
- Expansion upsells (Featured Listings at Month 3, extra seat at Month 6)
- Case study development
- Testimonial collection

**Success metric:**
- Month 12 renewal confirmed
- NPS 8+
- Testimonial on record
- At least one expansion purchase

## 4. The Success Score (0–100) and Business Outcomes

The DealerSuccessScore is described in full in `ONBOARDING_OPERATIONS.md`. This section maps score ranges to business outcomes.

| Score | Meaning | Business implication |
|-------|---------|---------------------|
| 85–100 | Thriving | Very likely to renew, expand, and refer. Request testimonial. |
| 70–84 | Healthy | On track. Standard renewal. Introduce expansion at Month 3+. |
| 55–69 | Stable | Using platform but not at full value. Coaching intervention to unlock more. |
| 40–54 | Underperforming | Risk of churn within 60 days if not addressed. Proactive call required. |
| 25–39 | At risk | High churn probability. Save call + hands-on session urgently. |
| 0–24 | Critical | Imminent churn. Same-day call. Consider pause offer. |

### Score trends matter more than point-in-time score
A dealer at 55 and improving is less at risk than a dealer at 65 and declining. Track week-over-week score movement in the admin panel.

## 6. How Dealer #1's Success Story Becomes the Sales Engine for Dealers #2–50

### Why the case study is the most important sales asset
New dealers in the Philippines make decisions based on:
1. **Peer proof** — "Kilala ko ang dealer na iyon — kung nag-work dito para sa kanila, pwede rin para sa akin"
2. **Specific results** — Not "increases leads" but "17 leads in 30 days, 3 sales confirmed"
3. **Trust in the founder** — "Si [YOUR NAME] mismo ang nag-onboard sa kanila — mapagkakatiwalaan"

### How to use it

**In outreach messages (WhatsApp):**
Insert the headline result: "Isa sa aming Founding Dealers sa [area] ay nakatanggap ng [X] leads sa kanilang unang buwan."

**In the demo intro:**
"Bago ako mag-show ng platform, gusto ko munang ikwento sa inyo ang isang dealer na [similar to prospect]. Ito ang nangyari sa kanilang unang 30 days..."

**In the proposal:**
Add a 1-page case study insert between the pricing page and the closing page.

**On the /for-dealers page:**
Testimonial card with photo (if permitted), quote, result stats.

**On the sales deck:**
Slide 3: "This is what [Dealer Name] achieved in 30 days" — real numbers, real quote.

### The referral engine
At Month 3, if NPS 9–10, ask:
```
"[First name], natutuwa akong marinig iyon. May isang favor — kilala ninyo ba ang ibang dealer sa [area or nearby area] na baka makinabang din sa AutoBentaPH? Hindi kayo kailangang mag-sell — ipapakilala ko lang ang sarili ko at kung gusto nilang marinig ang inyong experience, okay lang iyon."
```

Every Founding Dealer referral closes with a higher conversion rate than any cold outreach — because the trust is already transferred.
