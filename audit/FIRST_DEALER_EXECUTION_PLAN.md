## Call Order Rationale

**Referrals first — always.** Three reasons:

1. **Lower resistance:** The prospect already has social proof from someone they trust. "Pinarefer ka ni [name]" changes the opening dynamic from cold pitch to warm introduction.
2. **Faster to demo:** Referral prospects typically agree to a demo on the first or second contact. Cold outreach averages 3–5 touches before a demo.
3. **Higher close rate:** Referral deals close at 3–5× the rate of cold outreach in SME sales. The 14-day window requires maximizing close probability, not coverage.

**After referrals:** No-CRM/paper dealers (Bayanihan), then high-volume Excel dealers (FastLane, Luzon Auto Network), then Facebook-only dealers (RJ Motors, North Metro), then platform-savvy dealers (Santos Auto Sales, Dela Cruz).

**CDK/Reynolds dealers last** in the sprint (Luzon Auto Network, Visalink): highest revenue potential but longer sales cycle. Start the conversation in Week 1 but don't expect to close in 14 days.
